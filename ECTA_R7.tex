\runtitle{Neural Bellman Operators}

\support{This paper develops the numerical framework accompanying \citet{qi2024}. The author thanks Darrell Duffie for discussions.}
\begin{abstract}
We study reusable policies for changes in economic contracts and transition laws. Neural Bellman Operators separate proposal, evaluation, improvement, and certification. For affine finite-horizon transition mixtures, a corrected endpoint chord compresses a sharper count-information upper oracle. We characterize their coefficient ordering, horizon work, storage, and total policy-bank error, and compare them on common policy libraries and autonomous refinements. In a portfolio economy with adjustable risk aversion, class-specific tensor certificates establish opposite risk rankings with and without deliberate adjustment throughout a joint contract--transition region. A primitive consumption-exposure condition explains a positive risk-specific adjustment premium in a tractable CRRA family. The full stopped economy, recursive utility, temporal selves, and persistent capacity competition retain their distinct economic roles. Exact structural optimization and mesh-only countercomparisons remain part of the evidence. The results connect computational precision to economic decisions without identifying neural training residuals with policy optimality.
\end{abstract}
