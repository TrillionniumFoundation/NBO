\title{Neural Bellman Operators:\\Certified Response Sets and Operating Commitments}
\runtitle{Neural Bellman Operators}
\begin{aug}

\support{This paper develops the numerical framework accompanying \citet{qi2024}. The author thanks Darrell Duffie for discussions.}
\begin{abstract}
We study procurement when operating service is an endogenous response to a dynamic contract. Finite value queries characterize a sharp outer response set for affine reward perturbations, including ties and near-optimal behavior. Rational support bounds convert this information into participation and purchaser-surplus certificates. An enforcement-capacity theorem and interval response relaxations extend the comparison from a contract menu to continuous fees. In a stochastic settlement economy, menu refinement changes the compulsory-term ranking, and a continuous-instrument certificate identifies executable contracts with explicit global regret bounds. Joint service--surrender queries strengthen coordinate secants; incidence and quality counterfactuals identify the institutional sources of the comparison. A primitive opportunity-ordering result and an adverse parameter map distinguish adjustment gains from financial exposure. Neural proposal, policy evaluation, and verification remain separate, and matched benchmarks retain unfavorable neural outcomes. The arithmetic and open-mandate semantics are integrated into the theorem chain. A model-to-decision transfer theorem separates fixed-array verification from continuous-state approximation, whose constructor discrepancy is audited directly.
\end{abstract}
