\title{Neural Bellman Operators}
\runtitle{Neural Bellman Operators}

\support{This paper develops the numerical framework accompanying \citet{qi2024}. The author thanks Darrell Duffie for discussions.}
\begin{abstract}
We study policy reuse after changes in an economic contract or a transition law. Neural Bellman Operators separate proposal, evaluation, improvement, and certification. For affine mixtures of positive finite-horizon transition kernels, we construct an endpoint-value correction whose additional error is second order in parameter-interval width. Independently evaluated policy polynomials and statewise switching then certify welfare throughout the interval. In a stopped portfolio economy with adjustable risk aversion, a duration--effort ratio characterizes regular risk-indifference loci, while a matched restriction on adjustment identifies its risk-specific option value. The implementation certifies reward and transition counterfactuals, retains first-action sign exclusions, and compares optimistic support, mesh-only policy banks, and reusable structural optimization. An exact parametric quadratic program outperforms learned deployment in the retained quadratic resource model. Recursive utility, temporal selves, and persistent capacity competition preserve the broader economic framework. The results connect computational errors to economic choices without identifying a training residual with policy optimality.
\end{abstract}
