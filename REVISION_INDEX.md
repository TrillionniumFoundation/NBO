## Scientific organization

The revision separates policy evaluation, feasible policy improvement, and verification. It retains recursive utility, endogenous preferences, time inconsistency, dynamic games, and high-dimensional computation, with explicit equations and evidence for each. The analytical results concern declared operators and admissibility classes, not an unconditional convergence claim for Adam.

Equation-trained neural networks, structural policy iteration, supervised neural projections, finite-state game computation, and synthetic identification experiments are identified separately. Historical illustrative curves are preserved for audit but are not represented as newly observed experiments. The replication record includes the unsuccessful pilot initializations as well as every declared final seed.
