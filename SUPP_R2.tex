\section{Time-Inconsistent Agents: Extended-HJB Specification}
A sophisticated temporal self chooses a one-shot action while taking the continuation policy of future selves as given. Let $\mathcal V$ denote the continuation value under that policy. The equilibrium condition is
\begin{equation}
 c^*(t,s)\in\argmax_{c\in\mathcal C(s)}\{\beta u(c)+\diffop^{c}\mathcal V(t,s)\},
\qquad -\mathcal V_t=u(c^*)+\diffop^{c^*}\mathcal V,
\label{eq:supp_r2_ehjb}
\end{equation}
with terminal and boundary data stated by the application. The policy-evaluation step uses the second equation with $c^*$ detached; the improvement step uses the first equation with $\mathcal V$ detached. The equilibrium residual is a one-shot deviation gain on a held-out grid, not a penalty that combines the two graphs. The no-present-bias limit $\beta=1$ is tested against the exponential-discount solution.

\begin{definition}[Separated temporal-self iteration]
At iteration $n$, solve the evaluation equation for $\mathcal V^n$ under the frozen continuation policy $\hat c^n$. Then update the acting policy by maximizing the first expression in \eqref{eq:supp_r2_ehjb} with $\mathcal V^n$ detached, and set the continuation policy equal to the updated policy (or use a declared target update only as a numerical accelerator). The reported equilibrium error is the maximum one-shot deviation gain.
\end{definition}

\section{Dynamic Games: Player-Specific Operator Iteration}
For each player $i$, rivals $\pi_{-i}$ are detached during the improvement step:
\begin{equation}
\pi_i^{+}\in\argmax_{a_i\in\mathcal A_i}\{f_i(s,a_i,\pi_{-i})+\diffop^{a_i,\pi_{-i}}V_i\}.
\label{eq:supp_r2_game}
\end{equation}
The evaluation equation is solved for $V_i$ at the frozen profile. The audit reports the unilateral gain from an independently computed best response and repeats the calculation from multiple initial profiles. In the static Cournot regression test, $q_i=1/3$ is the Nash quantity while $q_i=1/4$ is the joint-profit quantity; the latter therefore fails the unilateral test.

\begin{definition}[Player-specific game update]
For each $i$, minimize the evaluation residual for $V_i$ with all policies frozen. Holding $V_i$ and $\pi_{-i}$ fixed, maximize player $i$'s Hamiltonian over $\pi_i$. Update players sequentially or with explicitly detached simultaneous blocks. The scalar sum of all players' losses is not used as a Nash certificate.
\end{definition}

\section{Trace Estimation and Negative Controls}
If $\widehat q$ is an unbiased estimator of a diffusion trace $q$, then
\[
\E(a-\widehat q/2)^2=(a-q/2)^2+\operatorname{Var}(\widehat q)/4.
\]
R2 therefore reports probe-count bias against exact derivatives on low-dimensional problems, applies probes to the symmetric part of the diffusion-weighted Hessian, and records the resulting value, policy, boundary, and runtime errors. Historical curves with no run-level provenance remain negative controls.
