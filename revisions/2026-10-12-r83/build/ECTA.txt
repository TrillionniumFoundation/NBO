24                                         1. INTRODUCTION                                       24

25                                                                                               25
       A continuation approximation matters through the economic decision it produces.
26                                                                                               26
     Prediction error, original-optimum policy loss and verification work are different
27                                                                                               27
     objects. This paper connects them through a constructive Neural Bellman Operator
28                                                                                               28
     (NBO), its attained own-future continuation and the complete feasible controller
29                                                                                               29
     implemented under the original law.

15   that region without a fitting-success model or additional sample. Labels and the        15

16   continuation policy remain fixed.                                                       16
