We study neural policy evaluation, improvement, and independent verification in a continuous economy with costly preference adjustment. An affine-preference market-deflator dual
and validated policy evaluation certify regret below .01 for three adjustment-cost coefficients
under the original utility, adaptive controls, and first-exit contract. Access to adjustment and
the welfare cost of more expensive adjustment are resolved by intervals narrower than their
respective effects. A quantitative output-error allowance, interval neural differentiation, and a
structured global action oracle connect implemented policies to verified quantities. We add
prospectively frozen method-specific tuning and independent holdout evidence without replacing the earlier comparison. Reference-solvable tracking, mean-reverting, and ill-conditioned
control problems provide absolute-accuracy frontiers for stored held-action actors. Their
exact error decomposition separates deployment error from implemented optimization error.
Proofs, retained unsuccessful outcomes, and independent recomputations distinguish policy
evaluation, optimality, sampling uncertainty, and the economic inequalities established.

4.2. Policy generation, verified refinement, and the stopping rule

he lower-bound policies start from the retained one-input, twelve-unit neural time-control
rs. A constrained L-BFGS-B step polishes their sixteen consumption and adjustment outputs
g the original payoff and the same exact budget projection. This is an explicit enlargement
m a fixed network’s image to the feasible time-control outputs; it is not described as addial Adam iterations or as a theorem that the neural optimizer converges. Consumption and
stment constants are rounded inward relative to the exact decimal action bounds, and feasiy is checked using their exact binary rational values. The finite-node training objective is a
posal mechanism, not an asserted untruncated Gaussian expectation of singular CRRA utility.
independent polynomial-moment evaluator in Appendix H then encloses each deployed
tinuous-time policy under exact first preference exit. The full network histories, original
puts, polished outputs, and output-optimization records remain separate.
he dual reference is fitted with Gaussian quadrature, but its fitted objective is never used
bound. Verification uses t = v 2 , subdivides each of the sixteen time slabs, and covers the
dard Gaussian coordinate on [−6, 6]. Constant and linear Taylor terms are integrated with
rval-enclosed weighted moments. Hessian ranges enclose the entire remainder, including
es crossing the consumption constraint. Gaussian tails, the terminal tangent, the conditionalance allowance, localization, and outward arithmetic are separately included. The small
-dimensional interval tests establishing (15) precede every certificate execution.
he stopping rule is simple. Given a policy lower bound L and an upper bound U from
orem 3, return the policy with tolerance ε only when U − L ≤ ε. Otherwise refine the
drature or return the explicit unachieved target at the declared work limit. Soundness does
require optimizer convergence. Nor does soundness imply that an arbitrary tolerance can be
hed with a fixed affine relaxation. For the three declared .01 instances, the finite executions
w supply the termination certificate.
