\section{Exhaustive robustness in the original investment economy}\label{sec:study52}
The action-null result permits a diagnostic in which the true policy and its economic cost are held fixed while a critic's irrelevant component changes. This avoids confusing a new representation with a new economic policy. Before execution, we fixed all eight R49 target-two checkpoint policies retained in the R51 package: witness and uniform FVI for every two-state price--horizon cell. We enumerate every one of the $256^2$ closed eight-bit observation cells for each policy. There is no selection of cells by favorable cost or by the new results.

For each cell we retain the original robust capacity repair, the action lattice $1/4096$, and the convex final-date proposal. The centered outward interval for its actual conditional cost difference is the same object used in the inherited terminal gate. We compare the global-width and contrast-aware gates after replacing $g$ by $g-n_M$, for $M\in\{0,1,16,4096\}$. Proposition~\ref{prop:investment-null52} establishes the zero action-error allowance algebraically; it is not estimated from this grid. Closed-cell enclosures cover boundary states, while execution retains the original observation tie convention. Symbolic cancellation precedes interval evaluation.

The records retain the incumbent and proposed action indices, the returned contrast-aware index, the exact binary64 endpoint encodings, and every global-width decision for every cell. An independent record audit checks cell completeness, cell-wide capacity, all gates, counts and accepted upper bounds. The contrast-aware actions are also compared with a fresh call to the unmodified inherited terminal implementation on every cell. Thus the reported invariance concerns the actual stored policy, not equality of two rounded average costs. The exact finite-state suite separately exercises nonzero action-error bounds, rejection, and all passes of the finite-sweep theorem.

The experiment measures resistance to irrelevant continuation error. It is not a new estimate of $J^{\rm witness}-J^{\rm FVI}$. The contrast-aware policies are identical to the unperturbed terminal-repaired incumbents, so repeating their cost simulation would not create additional independent economic observations. The prior actual-cost intervals, their confidence events, and the 93 witness-higher, zero witness-lower and three unresolved R49 comparisons remain unchanged. Safe improvement of either incumbent remains different from superiority over the competing policy.

Every service includes input loading, proposals, true-cost interval comparisons, independent inherited-policy replay, amplitude decisions and durable cell output. Machine-independent query counters, serialization time and peak memory are retained separately. The supplementary work table is the cost of this exhaustive diagnostic, not the construction cost of an alternative learned model. A full $T$-pass investment-economy execution is not inferred from a final-date ablation. The general finite-sweep result has explicit verified-error hypotheses; the continuous economic experiment executes only the declared specialization.
