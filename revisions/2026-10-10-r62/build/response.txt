               1.2. M1 and B10: One coherent contribution within the original paper          10

11                                                                                           11

12       The revised introduction separates three parts of the NBO method: construction      12

13   of a continuation from its own fitted future, recovery of a feasible action, and        13

14   certification of the complete implemented policy. The new contribution completes        14

15   the third part at the original Bellman optimum, rather than promoting an exact          15

16   local action solver into a general economic platform. We retain the original core and   16

17   broader applications under their existing hypotheses, but do not apply the new          17

18   convexity theorem to a diffusion, nonlinear recursive preference or game merely         18

19   because it appears elsewhere in the paper.                                              19

20       The reading order places the primitive regularity and actual-policy theorem         20

21   after the exact witness and fixed-cover stability results. The new experiment then      21

22   tests that theorem directly. The earlier range-table certificate, unchanged-actor       22

23   revalidation, initial-law service catalogue and exact query experiments remain          23

24   separate observations, with their original unfavorable results. This is a development   24

25   of the same NBO topic, not the differently titled replacement paper suggested in        25

26   the report.                                                                             26

27       The full development and proof companions preserve the earlier material. The        27

28   preservation audit checks inherited labels and scientific source identities. We have    28

29   not obtained a more favorable contribution claim by removing conventional wins          29

30   or failed targets. The cumulative companions remain long; the active reading path       30

31   now identifies the added proof–implementation–economic-cost chain explicitly. No        31

32   claim is made that formatting alone resolves every editorial question about scope.      32

       All new methods now face the same maximum-over-states-and-dates Bellman-
3                                                                                                3
     accuracy targets. The lower bound covers the continuous feasible action set, not
4                                                                                                4
     merely the proposed lattice. The upper action arrays belong to the actual returned
5                                                                                                5
     controller. The policy theorem adds off-grid interpolation, original continuous-law
6                                                                                                6
     quadrature, arithmetic, state acquisition, downward action rounding and future
7                                                                                                7
     policy error. A gain relative to zero investment cannot pass this criterion.
8                                                                                                8
       The important mathematical step is to prove joint convexity of the true Bellman
9                                                                                                9
     objective despite the bilinear transition. The primitive state curvature dominates the
10                                                                                               10
     nonaffine composition defect at the original discount 15/16. A uniform Lipschitz
11                                                                                               11
     bound of 27/d and semiconcavity bound of 54/d close the backward induction
12                                                                                               12
     for every finite horizon. Coordinate bounds then give a second-order positive-
13                                                                                               13
     interpolation error. This produces nodal Bellman brackets whose error does not
14                                                                                               14
     contain the entire value range over every reached rectangle.
15                                                                                               15
       A separate theorem transfers feasible nodal actions to the actual barycentric
16                                                                                               16
     acquired actor. Joint convexity bounds its optimal advantage, and backward re-
17                                                                                               17
     cursion bounds its complete loss. The fitted critic need not be convex, and the
18                                                                                               18
     value of the returned policy is not assumed convex. The pure fitted actor and its
19                                                                                               19
     common-augmented counterpart have different recorded upper arrays and different
20                                                                                               20
     certificates; neither receives the other’s accuracy by attribution.
21                                                                                               21
       The reported frontier retains both first-crossing verification prefixes and full
22                                                                                               22
     catalogue-release costs. Every method is charged the entire common reference cal-
23                                                                                               23
     culation, its own primitive fitting and verification, all preceding rungs, serialization,
24                                                                                               24
     full shared inference and cold compilation. We do not call a prefix without inference
25                                                                                               25
     the complete cost of an unexecuted stopping algorithm.

                    1.8. M7: Timing, memory and complete service boundaries
12                                                                                               12

13       The new timing account charges all reference rungs, primitive fitting, proposal         13

14   construction, verified node evaluations, actor transfer, serialization, full shared cost    14

15   inference and cold compilation. Shared work is charged in full to each method               15

16   rather than assigned an unmeasured marginal cost. Pure and augmented variants               16

17   are produced jointly, and each is conservatively charged that joint verification work. 17
18   The complete frontier is therefore a finite catalogue-release account, not a claimed        18

19   minimum-work sequential schedule.                                                           19

20       The native interpolation kernel exposes its tensor weights, arithmetic count and        20

21   logical arrays. The records include observed platform, processor affinity, numerical- 21
22   library versions, compilation receipts and peak resident memory of the full process. 22
23   They do not claim controlled processor frequency, hardware performance counters,            23

24   measured memory-bus traffic or independent per-method peak-memory measure- 24
25   ments. A multi-architecture randomized timing experiment is not added in this               25

26   release. The earlier paired native timings remain the relevant replicated implemen- 26
27   tation evidence, while the new complete-service clocks are explicitly descriptive.          27
