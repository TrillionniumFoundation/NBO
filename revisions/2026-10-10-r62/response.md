## B9. Economic interpretation, replacement fees and numerical units

The numerical units remain those of the original normalized investment model. No installation-price estimate, electricity tariff, processor-frequency estimate or external welfare calibration is inferred from hosted execution time. Instead, the manuscript gives the full symbolic decision account in those units.

For identical returned policies, an implementation change affects computation cost. At work price $\lambda$, setup difference $k_1-k_0$, per-use work difference $w_1-w_0$, use count $H$ and additional implementation fee $I$, the new-minus-old total cost is $\lambda[H(w_1-w_0)+(k_1-k_0)]+I$. This yields the entire break-even use-count account whenever a measured per-use saving exists, while a negative saving remains a negative result.

For different policies, a gain interval $[l,u]$, replacement fee $\tau$ and incremental work charge $\lambda\Delta w$ give net benefit $[l-\tau-\lambda\Delta w,u-\tau-\lambda\Delta w]$ on the same coverage event. The cases of supported replacement, ruled-out benefit and unresolved benefit are stated separately.
