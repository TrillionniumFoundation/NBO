\subsection{Full-support computation under identical paid information}\label{sec:supportresults126}

The finite solver processed all 74 predeclared paid pools, 148 candidate
actions and 888 pairwise contrasts. It made 5,920 capped linear-programming
calls and no additional economic query. There were no failed pools or
reported LP failures. Its whole parent-process cost was 20.2483 seconds,
additional to the original prefixes; the shared host was not controlled.
An independent implementation reconstructed all 33,331 saved cuts and 1,508
nonzero rational weights, and verified every contrast and continuous-action
regret aggregation. The 1,924 record checks took 4.2228 seconds internally,
with their separate parent receipt retained.

The 48 improvements establish that the complete compatibility restrictions
can sharpen a cheap, equally informed conventional bound in executed original
model records. They do not establish a decision gain. All six smaller regret
bounds concern the capacity candidate in online-control pools away from the
first state. The improved bounds remain far above the ordinary service
tolerance. No candidate becomes a newly qualified strict improvement and
none changes the primary policy. The smaller contrast or regret is therefore
an information improvement with positive computational cost in this study.
The full-support calculation has not earned an economic adoption claim here.

The comparison already gives the conventional method the global derivative
cap and the paid original-optimum lower bound. The raw cut certificate bounds
the complete-support information class. Its final intersection with the
conventional bound is an original-policy bound and is not labeled a sharp
solution of that class when the optimum floor was not encoded in its defining
constraints. No independently feasible primal gap was supplied by this capped
run; neither exact finite optimality nor achievement of the ideal limiting solution
is claimed. All candidate failures, unchanged-policy outcomes, exact witnesses
and algebra costs remain available in the development account.
