\subsection{Independent original economics and adoption conditions}

The independent continuous-law evaluator covers all 515 distinct final
state--risk--action keys and uses 32,740 of its 32,768 allowed terminal
contexts. Its 884 evaluations and 915,872 actual bisection iterations are
additional research work, with an internal elapsed time of 491.7335 seconds.
It independently establishes the ordinary regret threshold in all 1,584
decisions and the stronger regret threshold in 1,175. It confirms 1,277 of the specific reported regret
bounds and verifies 471 reported value intervals by containing the independently
reconstructed interval inside the reported one. The remaining 307 regret
checks and 1,113 interval-containment checks remain unresolved under the fixed
cap. A separately recorded post-execution exact audit of the already saved
independent bounds verifies the width gate in all 1,584 cases; their maximum
independent width is below $0.000107509$. Thus the combined independent bounds
qualify all ordinary services and 1,175 stronger services. This additional
saved-bound check leaves the frozen regret-only summary fields unchanged and
performs no further economic evaluation. There are zero contradictions. These counts describe different tests;
an ordinary regret-threshold pass does not confirm every tighter production
claim or by itself recheck its separate value-width gate,
and an overlapping interval is not containment.

There are 1,044 paired comparisons of the direct route against the fused and
warm route for the same actor, risk and state. Independent actual-policy bounds
certify 238 direct gains and 39 direct losses, establish 517 exact complete-policy
ties, and leave 250 orderings unresolved. A stronger regret upper bound therefore
does not guarantee that a service beats a particular competing feasible policy.
Against the fused control separately, there are 214 gains, 13 losses, 260
exact ties and 35 unresolved comparisons; the outward fixed-census mean
contrast is contained in $[-0.0003681,-0.0003530]$. Against warm adaptation,
there are 24 gains, 26 losses, 257 ties and 215 unresolved cases, with mean
contrast contained in $[-0.000007002,0.000007125]$. Thus an aggregate original
cost improvement over fused control is certified, while the mean comparison
with warm adaptation remains unresolved. The full split and all unresolved
pairs remain in the companion and machine-readable ledger. These deterministic results carry no
population confidence interval.

The three complete first-state timing processes total 17.7213 seconds. In 68
of 116 matched first-state comparisons, the direct route's largest measured
complete time is below the comparator's smallest; none satisfies the reverse
strict range separation. The other comparisons have overlapping ranges. This
first-state observation coexists with the unfavorable nine-state total above;
it must not be generalized to all states. Model loading, output and the
whole-process residual are included in these timing ranges.
