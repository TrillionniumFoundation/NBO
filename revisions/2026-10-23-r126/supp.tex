\begin{abstract}
This supplement supplies the complete proofs for Neural Bellman Operators:
feasible own-future construction; finite-horizon existence, uniqueness and
stability; action and centered recursive-risk accounts; continuous-risk
qualification; constrained original marginal regularity; actor/critic and
smooth computational graphs; and the conditional Brownian recursive-utility
bridge with its stopping boundaries. The current additions prove direct action and value stopping, rational multi-cut
certification, continuous-action regret resolution, conditional economic stopping,
and independently checked finite-design fit bounds. The retained results prove exact finite
interpolation above all paid quadratic Bellman supports, rational residual
certification, an exact action-information witness, and complete precision-work
and terminal decision bounds. The prior memory and joint interpolation results
are included with their full proofs and original scope. Fixed-information
minimax statements retain their specified interfaces. Results for distinct
objectives and historical experiments are not transferred by notation.
The frozen original-economy service implements only the algorithms explicitly
identified in its source protocol. Analytic extensions completed after that
freeze do not modify its scientific code or measured outcomes.
Statements introduced here are numbered S.*.
\end{abstract}
