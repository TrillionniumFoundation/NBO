## 5. Foundational requirements that must remain enforced

The original September 15 report supplies concrete falsification cases, not optional language advice:

1. The joint critic/actor composite objective can prefer a nonzero Bellman residual even with exact terminal data. Separate actor and critic parameter objectives and all detached paths must remain explicit; the original counterexample must remain a regression check.
2. Interior residuals do not select terminal/boundary conditions, Markov closure, transversality, or recursive-utility existence and comparison. Every extension must preserve its own state/admissibility/domain assumptions.
3. Vanishing population L2 residual does not select the viscosity solution; the wrong triangular limit survives exact boundary data. No neural spectral-bias slogan supplies the missing monotone/consistent/stable comparison argument.
4. The original Merton constants, wealth-kink claim and Epstein--Zin sign/normalization were wrong. Their corrected anchors, utility domains and historical false figures must remain distinguishable from actual regenerated experiments.
5. Zero-centered targets do not imply update stability or constant-step Adam convergence. Occupancy/adjoint weighting, full versus semigradient differentiation and sampling laws remain part of the theorem's premises.
6. NDU preference-state viability/cardinality, sophisticated-agent temporal deviations and unilateral game deviations cannot be borrowed from a different economic objective or information set. Joint profit maximization is not a Nash computation.
7. Unbiased Hessian trace estimation creates a biased squared-residual objective unless the extra variance term is handled. Probe cost, covariance operations, derivative error and feasibility are separate from total fixed-accuracy complexity.
8. Separable high-dimensional economies must be compared with separability-aware conventional algorithms. Analytic curvature, formulas, features or labels available to NBO must be available and charged symmetrically to comparators.

Later valid theorems and diagnostics should be cited as actual repairs where they apply. This audit does not infer a general closure of these requirements from a later revision number or successful publication build.
