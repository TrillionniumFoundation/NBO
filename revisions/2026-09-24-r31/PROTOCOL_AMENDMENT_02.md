# Amendment 02 — exact bit control and occupancy stress

The primary model, horizons, tolerances, neural seeds, fitting recipe and spline comparators remain fixed. Initial pilot certificates are retained, not substituted by the formal study. They establish coupled all-restart bounds, but the all-defer incumbent and first neural seed yield zero dynamic-versus-pointwise intervention savings. Such zeros will remain in the reported cohort.

Before the complete cohort is executed, the witness compression implementation adds a dyadic encoding step. First interpolate within half the local allowance. Round retained knot coordinates and sampled values to dyadics, then prove the total error on the union of every original and new knot, including endpoints. Increase precision if this exact check fails. This controls the otherwise large rational denominators generated by successive envelope intersections. Both rounding work and the complete error check are charged. No floating tolerance supplies a certificate.

A separately labeled mechanism stress incumbent is added, not included in neural performance counts: at all nonterminal dates it prescribes maintain (action 1) if x>=9/10, and defer (action 0) otherwise; the last decision always defers. The economic primitives and accuracy budgets remain unchanged. This intentionally poor installed rule tests whether current revisions can change future exposure to a misconfigured operating instruction. It is not a fitted candidate, a calibrated institutional policy, or evidence for neural superiority. All three declared horizons and both tolerances will be reported, regardless of whether dynamic savings are positive.

The purpose of these additions is to expose arithmetic and occupancy mechanisms identified by the pilot, not to remove adverse comparisons. The full cold pipeline will continue to be compared with the structure-aware exact solver and classical fitted proposals. A lack of end-to-end neural advantage will not be relabeled as an advantage.
