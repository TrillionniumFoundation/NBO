\subsection{What is reproduced and what is preserved}
The new replay consists of 80 neural expert checks, 20 final-expert MPFR checks, 16 direct-stochastic checks, and 20 compensated-policy checks: 136 executions. The first group includes initialization and all three retained proposals for each of twenty experts. The MPFR processes are isolated from the binary processes to avoid sharing the loaded interval module. Every reported actor hash is checked. The script fails on a non-overlapping price, reserve, portfolio, or payoff interval. Generated tables are derived from directed calculations and round in the conservative direction.

The inherited record retains the all-ten-seed actor--critic factorial, 30 trace checkpoints, signed classical decompositions, continuation-lift interventions, and high-dimensional learned-gradient/correction study. These are not counted among the new 136 replays. The original all-domain neural error of approximately 7.3, the width-dependent architecture reversal, and accelerated-gradient advantages at stringent nonlinear-control tolerances remain visible. The corrected localized success does not remove any of those observations.
