## Recommended argument in the main text

Start with the original dynamic economic decision, its recursive Bellman
operator and its nonlinear risk/continuation law. The actor is a feasible
decision rule; the critic approximates the original continuation or its
action information. The final economic target is the original complete
policy objective, with its actual future controller and original capacity
constraints. Neither the neural fit criterion nor a changed risk law is
the object being improved. The historical theory and numerical evidence
remain the foundation, with the current dependency map locating the full
proofs and the development companion preserving the longer history.

Introduce the new problem through a concrete information loss: a paid
continuation calculation returns both levels and action marginals, while
an envelope that uses those pieces separately may miss restrictions on
the action contrast. The new exact interface result characterizes the
compatible scalar continuation class and gives a rational certificate
whose validity does not depend on optimizer convergence. Its economic
content is the conversion to a safe improvement of the same complete
policy, a valid original-regret bound and a separately checked absolute
value width. The exact witness shows that joint information can improve
the best guaranteed action gain over the stated separate-envelope
interface; the theorem does not claim an advantage over every algorithm
that can use the original model's further formulas or raw supports.

State the inherited mathematical sources explicitly. Smooth strongly
convex interpolation and performance estimation are established by the
cited Taylor--Hendrickx--Glineur work. The strong-convexity chord bound in
B is elementary established convex analysis. The contribution should not
be framed as invention of either general principle or of conic
optimization. The author addition is their exact original-policy
information interface, the verifiable residual correction and its
complete decision qualification, the saturation diagnosis, and the
costed integration with the NBO actor/critic problem. All are
representation-neutral mechanisms available to neural and conventional
methods on the same information.

Present A as a complete empirical test with a precisely explained
negative result. There were no changes in any of 928 paired actions and
no strict tightening in any of 404 joint solves. Every solve began at the
only paid anchor. The post-A theorem proves that this information class
already attains the derivative-envelope extreme, and the independent
rational census constructs the primal witness for every saved solve.
Consequently more accurate optimization of that same certificate could
not help those contrasts. The optional global derivative cap does not
resolve these cases: the localized curvature witness satisfies it. All
historical solver iterations, failures and elapsed work remain charged.
The bypass is a newly proved equivalent computation rule, not a retimed
or rescored A algorithm.

The other A failure is different. All 98 two-anchor joint rows had an
empty distinct-action menu, and their incumbents were interior. Their
individual derivative intervals contained zero, so each original
gradient-based proposal stayed at the incumbent. The abstract exact
two-anchor example proves that this menu can omit an action with a
strictly guaranteed level-based gain. It does not prove that those 98
economic states had such a gain. Separate the actual census from the
mathematical possibility and from the new B experiment.

Then introduce B as a design informed by A, frozen before its own new
states. It compares three complete services: full original fused,
fused plus the cheap paid-value bridge, and fused plus bridge plus joint.
All use the same original paid ledger; the latter two use the same menu
fixed at the fused action. Pairs propose a curvature-adjusted value
minimum and midpoint, with the six declared local neighbors. Both stages
check the realized rounded action, the original q lower and original
optimum lower, the one future allowance and all original qualification
gates. The joint arm pays for and preserves the complete bridge stage.
The pointwise order is a theorem; comparative economic gains and costs
are empirical questions. The experiment's fewer-than-two-anchor gate is
a declared design restriction, not a claim of information optimality
after a fused action has moved away from the sole paid anchor.

Retain every conventional arm and equal-feature hinge. A neural
advantage, if one is later observed, must survive the same-information,
same-feature, same-feasible-policy and complete-work comparisons. The
mechanical ReLU/hinge equivalence and any conventional winners are
scientific evidence. Neither a smaller joint dual bound with unchanged
policy nor the proved nested policy order establishes an advantage in
economic objective plus computation cost.

## Important terminology choices

Use "original-policy improvement" for the certified strict contrast,
"certificate tightening" when only an upper bound changes, and "action
generation" for an expanded realized feasible menu. Use "current
information saturation" only for the stated class and origin. Avoid
"optimal precision allocation" for the one-anchor bypass without the
qualifier that it stops redundant certificate arithmetic while leaving
new original information acquisition and action search open. Report
native service clocks and failed attempts separately from hypothetical
reuse/adoption domains, and do not call the latter measured throughput.

For the stochastic-control and NDU/FBSDE connections, retain the actual
Brownian, decoupled-forward, Lipschitz and well-posedness premises from
the dependency audit. The current original nonlinear-risk experiment
does not establish a fully coupled FBSDE result or an unrestricted
noncash recursive-utility theorem. This conditional scope keeps the
original research direction and identifies what further theoretical
work remains.
