\subsection{The capital experiment}
\label{sec:r15experimentdesign}
The experiment uses the capital state equation and objective in
\eqref{eq:r10state}--\eqref{eq:r10payoff}, in dimensions ten and fifty.
It changes the volatility parameters to $\sigma_I=0.6$ and $\sigma_C=0.3$;
the remaining primitives are $T=1$, $\rho=0.04$, $a=0.10$,
$\kappa=0.15$, $\zeta=0.2$, $\chi=0.03$, and
$[\underline m,M]=[0.02,2]$. The initial population assigns equal mass to
nine profiles with means $-0.5,0,0.5$ and cross-sectional spreads
$0,0.25,0.5$. The methods share the declared radius-$0.1$ feasible tube,
coupling matrix, initial population, and payoff evaluator. This is a
parameter regime of the same economic model. The earlier comparison,
scalar study, and protected-observation experiment retain their original
volatility parameters and their separate evidence records.

Training uses a global grid of 64 cells. Its reference policy is the
cell-average analytical schedule and remains fixed through all three
work levels. The cumulative occupation samples contain 256, 512, and
1,024 states. Each state comes from an independent reference trajectory
at a uniformly selected decision node. Postdecision inputs are the
pre-noise mean and the next decision date. NBO and Raw share four-antithetic
continuation targets; all member rollouts and all reused labels are
counted. These sample sizes define increasing information budgets,
not equal floating-point work across methods.

At each stage NBO fits the spatial continuation derivative by full-replay
Sobolev mean-square loss, with 1,200 Adam updates followed by L-BFGS.
The latter has nominal iteration and evaluation limits of 300 and 400;
the current line search is completed and every actual closure call is counted.
A separate time component then receives 400 value-fitting updates with
the spatial component fixed. NBO and Raw use the same feasible actor
objective and minibatch/update streams, with 200, 400, and 800 additional
actor updates at the three stages. DPO and HJB use 100, 200, and 400
additional updates. The minibatch size is 128; the actor, spatial-critic,
time-component, and HJB learning rates are $0.003$, $0.002$, $0.003$,
and $0.002$, respectively. Model weights and replay continue across
stages. These are declared optimization procedures; a common sample
budget does not make their optimization costs identical.

Online certification and independent final confirmation both use 2,048
decision cells and 8,192 paths per stream. The selected fixed networks
are deployed on that finer grid. The learned 64-cell continuation
derivative is consequently a predictor in the fine-grid mechanism
assessment, whose error account includes that discrepancy; it is not
identified with an exact fine-grid or continuous-time costate.
The protocol and numerical source were frozen before the confirmation
experiment. Exploratory pilots that selected the design remain distinct
from the confirmatory observations.

The measured process clock begins before a fresh worker is launched and
ends after its final evidence is durably written. It includes imports,
model setup, deterministic constants, fitting, checks, weight reloading,
confirmation, and output. Dependency installation and artifact extraction
precede this clock and are not charged as method execution time.
