# Primary-source literature check, 13 October 2026

The current entry point keeps the original NBO subject and does not claim the convex-order inequality, AVaR threshold representation or Hoeffding concentration as new. The added contribution is the implementable composition of continuous-law quadrature, complete-service admission, decision-scale resolving power and acquisition/reuse accounting.

- Econometric Society author guidance: https://www.econometricsociety.org/publications/econometrica/information-authors ; the retained econsocart author support class and author-year bibliography are used.
- W. Hoeffding (1963), *Probability Inequalities for Sums of Bounded Random Variables*, JASA 58(301), 13–30, DOI 10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830. Primary publisher record: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830 .
- M. W. Alomari (2016), *A Generalization of Hermite-Hadamard's Inequality*, arXiv:1603.08045. Primary author preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.08045 . Used to contextualize established composite midpoint/trapezoid inequalities, not as the source of an asserted NBO novelty claim.
- The 2025 value-function-learning paper's second author is **Shangzhe Yang**, not Wenhao Yang. The publisher identifies the authors as Andrzej Ruszczyński and Shangzhe Yang: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10479-025-06701-w .
- The actual title of arXiv:2605.00654 is **Reinforcement Learning with Markov Risk Measures and Multipattern Risk Approximation**, by **Andrzej Ruszczynski and Tiangang Zhang**. The inherited title “Feature-Based Risk-Averse Learning” and coauthor “Yiran Zhang” are incorrect. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00654 . Its stated result concerns a finite-horizon feature-based Q-learning regret bound; it is not a deterministic continuous-state certification theorem. It remains labeled as a preprint.

The old bibliography is preserved rather than silently overwritten in history. This is a targeted verification of the references relevant to the current additions, not a claim to have revalidated every inherited bibliographic field.

The new AVaR threshold citation additionally resolves to Rockafellar and Uryasev (2000), Journal of Risk 2(3), 21--41, DOI 10.21314/JOR.2000.038. Primary publisher record: https://www.risk.net/journal-risk/2161159/optimization-conditional-value-risk .
