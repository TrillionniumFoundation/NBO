## B5, M5: Non-tensor execution and explicit dimensional work

The new acquired-state representation is a locally split binary partition with 32 or 128 leaves, rather than a Cartesian product of coordinate grids. The complete services run the original nonlinear family at `(d,T)=(2,2),(4,4),(8,6)`. This is a genuinely multidimensional non-tensor implementation, not a nonuniform tensor grid described as non-tensor.

The work proposition charges the literal cell-membership calculation as `O(T A q N^2 d)` plus primitive and critic work, where `N` is the number of leaves and `A` counts candidates and covering intervals. The signed smooth-reference calculation costs `O(A N q d T^2)` and can stream its innovation children. It needs neither a full state-pair table nor enumeration of `q^T` future innovation histories. The primitive Jacobian gives the new dimension-uniform smooth-reference stability bound `1920/91` for the original normalized family.

This bound and the executed eight-dimensional service are positive, specific results. They do not imply that a fixed leaf count resolves arbitrary dimensional models at a fixed all-state accuracy. The manuscript retains that separate approximation burden in its explicit work and error accounts rather than presenting non-tensor storage as a universal elimination of dimensional complexity.
