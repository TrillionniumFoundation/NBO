                                            1. INTRODUCTION
26                                                                                                26

27     A continuation approximation matters because it changes an economic decision. 27
28   Its fitting error, the loss of its implemented policy, and the work needed to certify an     28

29   improvement are different objects. This paper develops Neural Bellman Operators              29

30   around that distinction. A construction uses its own fitted future values, returns           30

31                                                                                                31

32     Qian QI: qiqian@pku.edu.cn                                                                 32

1    feasible action witnesses, and connects numerical resources to the cost of the policy      1

2    actually implemented.                                                                      2

3        There are two linked numerical contracts in the same controlled economy. Under         3

4    effective primitive moduli, the constructive backend returns a feasible policy with a      4

5    prescribed error account against the original Bellman optimum. Theorems 2 and 3            5

6    retain the construction and its exact continuous original-owner implementation. A          6

7    fitted backend instead proposes actions from learned features. Those proposals be- 7
8    come deployable only after a whole-cell policy certificate is satisfied. A prospective     8

9    service then stops when the complete policy attains a prespecified expected-cost           9

10   target relative to an installed controller. This additional contract neither changes the   10

11   Bellman optimum nor turns an empirical fitting error into an optimality guarantee. 11
12       The trained neural backend has an explicit computational structure. Under the          12

13   model’s scalar uniform innovation, the expectation of a one-hidden-layer ReLU              13

14   continuation is analytic. For the scalar investment action, the integrated objective       14

15   is piecewise quartic, and a finite collection of rationally isolated cubic stationary      15

16   points supplies exact action-lattice witnesses. This produces a terminating action         16

17   search for a fitted nonlinear continuation without invoking another unconverged            17

18   nonconvex optimizer. It is distinct from compiling a native min-plus envelope into         18

19   an identical neural circuit. The verification theorem nevertheless applies to both         19

20   neural and conventional candidates.                                                        20

21       The policy account is implemented on a locally split, non-tensor observation           21

22   partition. A one-state residual and value cache encloses the old policy’s contin- 22
23   uation without a full table of state pairs. A signed sensitivity calculation for the       23

24   smooth installed reference further retains economically useful continuation direc- 24
25   tion. We prove its validity and give explicit state-dimension, leaf-count and horizon      25

26   costs. The original nonlinear investment family has a dimension-uniform smooth- 26
27   reference stability bound, although the work needed for a chosen approximation             27

28   accuracy can still grow with dimension. Every later discontinuous acquired actor is        28

29   handled by the generic cache, not by differentiating away its jumps.                       29

30       The economic experiment compares trained ReLU, quadratic and tree-based                30

31   Bellman continuations in dimensions two, four and eight and horizons two, four             31

32   and six. The original compiled-witness and tensor-FVI frontends remain in the two- 32

1    dimensional comparison. Each method pays for its own construction, verification,       1

2    inference and durable output. Targets require two- or ten-percent expected-cost        2

3    reductions from installed zero investment. A separately frozen signed-sensitivity      3

4    extension attains both targets in every declared service; its preceding cache-only     4

5    failures remain in the record. The complete-policy costs, nonterminal changes          5

6    and all-in clocks determine what this means economically. They do not establish        6

7    uniform neural superiority: common verification actions often determine the same       7

8    returned policy for several generators, and quadratic FVI is an important low-work     8

9    comparator.                                                                            9

12   and the remaining Bellman comparison with the same validation confidence box. 12

14   invariant, while retaining the maintained nonlinear drift and innovation model.        14

1                                       11. CONCLUSION                                          1

2                                                                                               2
       Neural Bellman Operators connect an own-future continuation to a feasible
3                                                                                               3
     action and an economic cost account. The constructive backend retains the original
4                                                                                               4
     Bellman-accuracy objective. The trained backend now has analytic innovation
5                                                                                               5
     integration and a terminating exact action-lattice search, while its proposals remain
6                                                                                               6
     subject to whole-cell true-advantage verification. These are complementary parts
7                                                                                               7
     of the same numerical method, not interchangeable claims about the name of a
8                                                                                               8
     function class.
9                                                                                               9
       The non-tensor cache and signed reference calculation turn the policy account
10                                                                                              10
     into an executable prospective service. Their proofs retain the cost of leaf member-
11                                                                                              11
     ship, critic evaluation, state dimension and horizon. The declared expected-cost
12                                                                                              12
     targets are attained in the sensitivity experiment, with genuine nonterminal action
13                                                                                              13
     changes and complete own-service clocks. Conventional generators share the same
14                                                                                              14
     verification treatment, and policy identities make their role observable. The finite ex-
15                                                                                              15
     periments identify successful returns and their costs; they do not establish universal
16                                                                                              16
     neural dominance or calibrated welfare effects.
17                                                                                              17
       The full-action gap, the actual expected cost and the price of acquiring or verifying
18                                                                                              18
     a policy remain separate economic quantities. Keeping all three explicit permits a
19                                                                                              19
     decision maker to choose the appropriate contract without treating fitting accuracy,
20                                                                                              20
     certificate sharpness or a favorable timing observation as a substitute for the cost of
21                                                                                              21
     the implemented policy.
