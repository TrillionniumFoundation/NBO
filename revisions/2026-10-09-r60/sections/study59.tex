\section{Equal-policy work at prospective economic targets}\label{sec:study59}
The preceding experiment asks whether an exact trained-neural witness changes a returned policy. A different, necessary question is how economically that same witness can be computed. The lossless screen permits a controlled comparison in which economic accuracy, fitted parameters, whole-cell verification and statistical stopping remain identical. We integrate the separately frozen R58 execution into this revision and independently reconstruct its solver, certificate and path calculations. The integration creates no new training run or independent statistical observation.

\subsection{A matched intervention within Neural Bellman Operators}
The experiment retains the original nonlinear cyclic investment laws. Its tasks are $(d,T)=(2,2),(4,4),(8,6)$ and its targets are ten- and twenty-percent expected-cost reductions relative to installed zero investment. Each task--target has two solver modes and three sequential complete-process repetitions, for 36 services. Both modes independently train the same 32-feature own-future ReLU continuation. Exact stored-parameter hashes establish matching; the common seed alone is not used as proof of matching.

The sole intervention is the nonterminal fitted-action solver. Algebraic search partitions the hinge objective and isolates its cubic stationary roots. Screened search performs exact activation-region reduction, outward lattice elimination and rational comparison of survivors. Both use the same robust action spacing $1/4096$, the same smallest-index tie rule and the same exact terminal solver. They retain the nine common actions and the critic-menu proposal; the exact action is an additional candidate. The same signed installed-reference verifier supplies all whole-cell upper endpoints and continuous-action lower covers.

The acquired observation geometry is the existing locally split binary partition, not a tensor grid. The two resource attempts use $(256,32,4)$ and $(1024,64,8)$ for training rows, acquired leaves and innovation bins. All attempts are checked against installed zero investment. Intermediate candidates are not deployed, and no derivative argument is applied to a changed discontinuous acquired actor. This experiment is a prospective refinement service, not a claim that two refinement attempts are two policy-iteration sweeps.

\subsection{First attainment, including nonattainment}
At each attempted resolution the complete policy is frozen before inference. The target estimand is
\[
 D_q=J(\pi)-qJ(0),\qquad q\in\{9/10,4/5\}.
\]
Cumulative sample sizes are $4096$, $16384$ and $65536$. A nonpositive upper endpoint of $D_q$ returns immediately. A strictly positive lower endpoint advances the construction attempt. An unresolved interval advances the inference look; an exhausted look budget advances the attempt. Failure to certify after the last attempt returns a safe policy with status \emph{budget exhausted}. The protocol specifies these rules before its production execution, rather than selecting a favorable pass from a completed catalogue.

The R58 stopping stream is distinct from the R57 stopping and comparison streams. Within a task, target and attempt, the two solver modes and timing repetitions deliberately regenerate the same stream. Repeated computation of that stream does not multiply sample size. The confidence family has error at most $1/100$ under the maintained independent-bin model. Its conservative maximum possible number of interval evaluations is
\[
 36\cdot2\cdot3\cdot3=648<2048,
\]
including cost, target contrast and gain at every possible look. The frozen empirical-Bernstein implementation retains its verified logarithm allowance. The union bound requires no independence between policies or looks. It does not authorize unplanned sample sizes or an arbitrary future stopping rule.

The machine-independent records identify the mechanism. Across the frozen workloads, the screen removes noncrossing integrated ridges exactly and retains one lattice point per nonterminal neural query. That point is compared rationally against the original objective. No production call uses the algebraic fallback. The number of original-objective rational evaluations therefore falls to the number of neural queries, whereas the algebraic mode compares several candidate points per query and performs cubic-root isolation. The screen is not an approximate optimizer with a favorable tolerance. The audit recomputes both solvers from every distinct saved model, compares every recorded witness and endpoint hash, regenerates each whole-cell certificate, and reconstructs the original interval paths. Distinct computations are memoized only after their input identities are checked. Every service file and stopping transcript is still compared. Separate exact fixtures verify all lattice endpoints for disjoint small problems, signed weights, activation boundaries, ties, non-dyadic lattices and fallback cases.

Median action-search times decrease in all six task--target groups, as do the complete process medians. The full-service algebraic-to-screened ratios range from approximately $1.028$ to $1.101$. These gains are smaller than the action-search gains because training, common terminal search, verification and inference are unchanged. The eight-dimensional unsuccessful target is included in the work comparison: faster exhaustion is not described as faster successful attainment.

The effect is a measured implementation gain within the trained NBO procedure at exactly matching policies and statistical accuracy. It is not a ranking of neural methods above common-only, quadratic or tree-based alternatives. The stronger conventional comparisons, the common-menu attribution results and all unresolved differences in the preceding studies remain part of the evidence.

\subsection{Complete work and the economic decision}
Every displayed service clock surrounds a separate process from invocation through exit and log flush. It includes imports, source checks, independently repeated fitting, action search, signed verification, own path evaluation and inference, all attempted resolutions and durable records. Available frequency-governor information, processor affinity, numerical-library versions, output bytes and peak resident memory are retained. The parent driver's own checkpoint-writing overhead is outside that measured interval and is identified as controller overhead, not assigned a zero cost.

Within a task, processes run sequentially on one available core with one numerical-library thread, and solver order rotates by repetition and target. All three measurements are printed in the supplement. Frequencies are observed where available but not controlled. Different task runners do not supply a hardware-controlled dimension experiment. Three fixed-work repetitions establish neither a distribution-free runtime advantage nor a population of independently fitted policies. The three distinct fitted seeds in the preceding experiment answer a different question and remain separately identified.

For a specified computation price, Corollary~\ref{cor:price59} converts an equal-policy resource difference directly into a total-cost difference. Relative to the installed controller, the same actual gain interval supports a larger region of installation fees and computation prices when the specified implementation charge is lower. This is a substantive difference in the cost of obtaining an unchanged economic answer, not a claim that two representations of the same policy produce different welfare. The model's units remain normalized; no empirical installation fee, computation price or welfare calibration is inferred from the timings.

The original accuracy objective remains visible throughout. Every returned policy retains its one-sweep all-state loss account, including the installed-reference continuation bound as well as its full-action directed gap. Lossless search changes neither that account nor the amount of acquisition information. A successful initial-law cost-reduction target is not substituted for convergence to the original Bellman optimum. The constructive resource theory, fitted-witness transfer and directed finite-sweep theorem continue to state the conditions under which that stronger objective is controlled.
