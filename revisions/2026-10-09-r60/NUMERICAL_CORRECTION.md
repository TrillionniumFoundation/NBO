# Outward subtraction and descriptor aggregation correction

The primary and common-accuracy designs and their raw results are preserved unchanged. During publication review, the cross-policy direct evaluator was found to use nearest-rounded NumPy subtraction of already outward path endpoints. An additional directed rounding step is required to make the difference endpoints themselves outward. Negating the centered own-repair difference is exact and does not have this issue.

The corrected evaluator applies nextafter toward minus infinity to each lower difference and toward plus infinity to each upper difference. It then replays all 28 groups with the identical frozen policies, bin keys, path counts and pseudorandom streams. These are numerical replays of the same sample, not additional independent observations. The corrected results are in results/direct-verified; original results/direct and the executed primary/common sources remain immutable. Both old and corrected hashes and their interval changes are audited. The active manuscript and tables use only the corrected direct records. Corrected evaluation clocks are kept separate; no earlier clock is rewritten or added to a historical service. The correction is frozen before replay in REPLAY_FREEZE.json.

A second reporting issue concerns the raw common-service prefix_counts map, which adds every field, including the descriptor named maximum_coefficient_bits. That additive descriptor is not a maximum and is not used as one. Publication reconstructs the true maximum across attempts and reports additive operation counts separately from maximum bit lengths and peak/live storage. This is a reporting correction; no numerical policy or result is changed.
