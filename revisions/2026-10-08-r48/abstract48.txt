This paper develops Neural Bellman Operators for policy evaluation and feasible improvement in controlled economies. Centered continuation errors link economic transport to full-policy loss. A constructive neural backend retains the feasible actions attaining its labels and supports implementation under acquired states and endogenous capacity. We give an exact continuous-state compiler that preserves the original policy, including ties, while removing a redundant state-cover factor from its evaluation work. The construction combines classical distance transforms with witness-preserving off-grid evaluation and numerical certification. A priced observation technology determines precision from a certified economic account. Direct constrained-policy comparisons distinguish signed cost differences from the ability to recoup a controller replacement fee. Matched services include uniform and error-driven conventional methods, failed refinements, and three- and four-dimensional nonlinear stress cases. The results separate representation savings, complete work, and economic decisions without attributing a native-envelope capability exclusively to neural encoding. Broader applications retain their own comparison conditions.
