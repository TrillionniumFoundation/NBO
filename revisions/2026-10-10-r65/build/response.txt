1                 1.2. M1 / B2 / B6. Separate the learned generator from its verifier            1

2                                                                                                2
         The revised introduction distinguishes three objects. The original constructive
3                                                                                                3
     continuation has a witness-preserving neural realization with an explicit own-future
4                                                                                                4
     loss account. The trained query predictor proposes a location at which to obtain
5                                                                                                5
     Bellman information. The verifier supplies original-law endpoint and continuous-
6                                                                                                6
     action lower bounds independently of that predictor. A pure fitted-value policy and
7                                                                                                7
     a complete query controller are therefore not the same economic object.
8                                                                                                8
         The inherited primitive regularity and actual-policy theorem remains in the
9                                                                                                9
     article. Its role is now explicit: convexity, semiconcavity and Lipschitz bounds
10                                                                                               10
     are properties of the original scalar investment problem, not assumptions silently
11                                                                                               11
     imposed on a fitted ReLU output. The new recursive controller uses those bounds
12                                                                                               12
     to evaluate descendants only when they are requested. It does not build or retain a
13                                                                                               13
     conventional action at every state before allowing a neural proposal to participate.
14                                                                                               14
         This change addresses the common-fallback concern without making the cer-
15                                                                                               15
     tificate specifically neural. The learned content can change the query transcript
16                                                                                               16
     and returned action; it cannot change the feasible action set, tolerance, economic
17                                                                                               17
     kernels or validity inequalities. The conventional methods receive the same verifier.
18                                                                                               18
     The new result concerns a concrete trained implementation of the original NBO
19                                                                                               19
     procedure, not priority for generic coupling, safe improvement or warm starts.

            1.3. M2 / B1 / B3 / B4. Preserve the adverse prospective findings prominently
22                                                                                               22

23       The abstract and introduction state that the earlier pure fitted policies are adverse   23

24   evidence and that the new learned services do not win the complete cold-start               24

25   comparisons. The study explicitly retains R62’s twenty strictly positive pure-ReLU- 25
26   minus-common cost intervals, its unresolved augmented comparisons, and the                  26

27   common method’s twenty wins among attainable complete-work cells. The new                   27

28   query workload is not presented as reversing those expected-cost findings.                  28

29       There is nevertheless a measurable incremental result. In R64’s matched models,         29

30   gating reduces deployment Bellman queries relative to unconditional insertion in all        30

31   sixteen ReLU task–target–seed comparisons and all sixteen quadratic comparisons. 31
32   Against adaptive parabolic search, the ReLU route uses fewer queries in nine                32

1    comparisons, the same in three and more in four; the quadratic route uses fewer               1

2    in eleven, the same in two and more in three. Complete median process time is                 2

3    smallest for a conventional method in every one of the eight task–target cells: five          3

       The query implementation is compared with adaptive parabolic branch-and-

     bound over the full continuous feasible action interval and with bisection. Each
14                                                                                                 14

15                                                                                                 15

16                                                                                                 16

17                                                                                                 17
     derivative certification. Its contribution is not credited to a network.
18                                                                                                 18

19                                                                                                 19

20                                                                                                 20

21                                                                                                 21

22                                                                                                 22

23                                                                                                 23

           1.6. M5 / B7. Specify the reliability estimand and retain failures and warnings
24                                                                                                24

25       Both scientific protocols fixed their task catalogue, targets, fitting seeds, training   25

26   caps, repeats and source identities before execution. The reliability estimand is            26

27   return and worst observed complete work over that finite initialization catalogue,           27

28   not a probability over unspecified future initializations. R63 has 96 isolated services      28

29   and R64 has 160. All 256 returned their stated contract. The maximum recorded                29

30   complete process time is 2.5831 seconds, rounded upward to four decimals.                    30

31       There are 104 retained fitting warnings across the two catalogues. They are              31

32   reported with the individual service records. A warning does not invalidate an               32
                                   RESPONSE TO THE REFEREE                                 5

1    independently verified returned policy, but omitting it would distort the fitting          1

2    history. The workload rows are not optimizer replications. Two repetitions of the          2

3    same seed have identical fitted parameters and traces; their distinct clocks are           3

4    retained rather than interpreted as new training draws.                                    4

5      The theorem’s finite refinement and exact-recovery account is separate from              5

6    that finite empirical reliability statement. It supplies validity for arbitrary supplied   6

7    predictions and a return procedure at the declared precision/tolerance regime. It          7

8    does not give a hardware-uniform wall-clock upper bound for arbitrary neural               8

9    training.                                                                                  9

11          1.7. M6. Replace residual allocation with isolated complete-process clocks          11

12                                                                                              12
       Every new scientific service runs in its own process with single-CPU affinity
13                                                                                              13
     and one numerical-library thread. The parent clock begins before process launch
14                                                                                              14
     and ends after exit and join. It includes imports, construction of training labels,
15                                                                                              15
     fitting, all queries for the actual complete policy, exact rational workload costing,
16                                                                                              16
     serialized outputs, durable summaries and the final clock record. There is no post
17                                                                                              17
     hoc unallocated residual added to each method.
18                                                                                              18
       Two process repetitions accompany every service configuration. All individual
19                                                                                              19
     clocks and fitting warnings are printed in the supplement. Machine-independent
20                                                                                              20
     counts separate training Bellman queries, deployment Bellman queries, refinement,
21                                                                                              21
     routed and declined predictions, prediction-free returns, terminal calls, maximum
22                                                                                              22
     batch, stored state-grid nodes and exact-rational recovery. CPU frequency remains
23                                                                                              23
     uncontrolled. Consequently the paper treats small clock differences as descriptive,
24                                                                                              24
     not statistically identified runtime effects or hardware-general rankings.
25                                                                                              25
       The publication replay has its own cost and is not substituted for a scientific

     service’s original clock. No record is retimed or overwritten in R65.

          1.9. M8 / B5. Quantify state-grid-free scaling and remaining resource dependence
21                                                                                             21

22       The new execution reaches dimensions two, eight and sixteen with targets 1/32         22

23   and 1/128, and includes both two-date and three-date controllers. All services have       23

24   zero stored state-lattice nodes. In particular, the sixteen-state, two-date controller    24

25   returns the 1/128 contract without the tensor node array used in R62. The uniform         25

26   theorem covers the initial states absent from the finite workload; the experiment         26

27   itself does not extrapolate its observations into such a theorem.                         27

28       The primitive bounds make the action and scalar-innovation budgets indepen- 28
29   dent of state dimension at fixed horizon and tolerance in this normalized economy. 29
30   Costs and transitions still require linear work in the number of coordinates; ReLU        30

31   parameters scale with dimension times width, while a dense quadratic grows                31

32   quadratically. The continuation tree remains expensive as horizon or innovation           32
                                   RESPONSE TO THE REFEREE                                 7

1    dimension grows. The recorded maximum live batch is 2,048 states, and the largest          1

2    peak process memory is 98,860 KiB.                                                         2

3      The new query study is scalar-control with a scalar innovation. The full two- 3
4    control original-policy result remains preserved from R62, but is not mislabeled           4

5    as a tensor-free query execution. Likewise, zero stored lattice nodes is not zero          5

6    storage, and exact-rational recovery is not a unit-cost floating operation. The revision   6

7    improves the state-dimensional implementation while leaving those distinct claims          7

8    distinguishable.                                                                           8

10                   1.10. M9 / B9. Specify the economic objective and units                    10

11                                                                                              11
       The decision maker purchases a callable controller. The article states a capital
12                                                                                              12
     normalization, a positive dollar conversion per model-loss unit, a computation
13                                                                                              13
     price per measured second and a fixed deployment charge. For a stated number of
14                                                                                              14
     uses under a specified initial-state law, the objective is expected monetary policy
15                                                                                              15
     loss plus the complete computation and deployment charge.
16                                                                                              16
       A valid expected-gain interval can then be multiplied by the positive loss conver-
17                                                                                              17
     sion and number of uses, and reduced by the incremental service charge. A positive
18                                                                                              18
     lower monetary gain supports purchase. In contrast, a uniform regret certificate
19                                                                                              19
     supports an ex ante accuracy constraint, not a directional expected-gain claim. The
20                                                                                              20
     current dyadic workload mean is not inserted into the monetary expectation as
21                                                                                              21
     though it were independently estimated under the continuous law.
22                                                                                              22
       These are declared economic preferences and prices, not estimated primitives
23                                                                                              23
     or an empirical welfare calibration. The paper also states the exact arithmetic
24                                                                                              24
     amortization condition for a fixed model and repeated identical workload. It does
25                                                                                              25
     not claim unmeasured amortized timings or that unrelated future states will deliver
26                                                                                              26
     the same savings. At the observed cold-start contract, a conventional service is the
27                                                                                              27
     least expensive eligible purchase in every median comparison.
