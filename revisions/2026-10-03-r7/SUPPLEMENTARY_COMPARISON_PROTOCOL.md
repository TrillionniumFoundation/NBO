# Supplementary comparisons and analytical domain design

These are supplementary studies fixed after the primary local R7 results were observed. They do not change the primary targets or select a favorable primary seed. Primary source remains frozen at 5bab4649728860fb655c5371f3705787f10b4a78.

1. On the identical R6 NDU finite economy, use a two-cell width-32 gated DGM-style critic, exact feasible maximization, 480 Adam updates and at most 300 L-BFGS iterations per stage. Run seeds 11, 29 and 47. This is a gated-architecture Bellman comparator, not a claim to implement the original differential DGM training objective. Its parameter count differs from the matched plain-MLP comparison and must be reported.
2. On that same economy, execute tensor Chebyshev projection with degrees 3, 5, 9 and 13, a boundary lifting, a fixed SVD pseudoinverse cutoff 1e-12 and backward Bellman targets. Preserve every degree, including failed tolerances. Compute the same independent full finite-model policy-payoff envelopes. Classical exhaustive backward induction remains the strongest small-grid reference; do not omit it from cost comparisons.
3. Derive an all-policy continuous-time domain-exit bound for the existing coupled-capital primitives, from the origin. Combine the Brownian reflection/union argument with an explicit uniform second-moment bound on the remaining return to bound the cost of a zero-exit-payoff stopped approximation. Evaluate domains [-3,1] and [-3.5,1.5], dimensions 10 and 20, using outward mpmath interval evaluation of a Gaussian Mills bound. This bounds the domain-truncation component only: it does not certify the fitted high-dimensional critic or its PDE residual.

All supplemental costs, seeds, raw coefficients, weights, closure counts and failures are saved. Comparisons use the same absolute .1 finite-utility policy target and .2 value target, not a claimed percentage welfare loss. Neural and polynomial targets depend on the solver's own continuation estimate, not on reference values supplied as training labels. Publication of these supplemental results is separate from the already running immutable primary study.
