## Factorial methods and attribution

Five modes are compared: common-only, relu-menu, relu-exact, quadratic-exact, and extra-trees-menu. Trained modes use the inherited own-future Bellman fitting procedure. ReLU hidden and output parameters are fitted and dyadically quantized, not copied from a min-plus representation. All modes use the same non-tensor partition and the same critic-independent signed sensitivity verifier for the installed zero-investment policy. At the terminal date they all receive the same exact terminal-action solver. Thus a terminal analytic improvement is not attributed to the neural representation.

The common menu contains nine robustly feasible actions. Relu-menu and extra-trees-menu additionally propose the inherited 17-menu critic minimizer. Relu-exact retains that entire menu and adds the exact lattice minimizer of the trained ReLU continuation objective. Quadratic-exact receives the same lattice-solving opportunity for its fitted quadratic continuation. Algebraic search uses exact rational hinge breakpoints, cubic root isolation, rational candidate comparisons and smallest-index tie breaking. The same retained continuous-action lower cover is used for every mode. Removing the extra algebraic proposal from a stored exact-mode certificate yields its within-stage baseline decision, upper advantage and directed gap. Every objective regret and every changed or unchanged proposal is retained.

The common-only control does not fit a critic and pays no fictitious fitting cost. Its cheaper construction is a legitimate comparator. The all-state Bellman target remains distinct from the actual initial-law stopping target. Every record reports a valid one-sweep all-state loss account; the directed old-policy greedy gap is not mislabeled as final Bellman loss.

## Prospective services and targets

Targets require 10% and 20% reductions from the installed policy: q=9/10 and 4/5. Each mode/task/target/seed starts from primitives in its own process. No pretrained fit or free cost-inference output is loaded. Two attempts use (training rows, acquired leaves, innovation bins)=(256,32,4),(1024,64,8). The same inherited 32-feature, 120-step ReLU fitting specification and ExtraTrees specification are retained.

Each attempt proposes a complete policy, verifies it at all dates against the fixed installed zero policy, and freezes it before inference. Attempts are not deployed between refinements. The derivative verifier is never applied to a discontinuous acquired incumbent. Each attempted policy is statewise safe relative to the installed policy; no monotonic ordering between different attempted policies is assumed.

Own inference uses fresh cumulative looks of 4096,16384,65536 interval paths. A nonpositive upper endpoint for J(candidate)-q J(installed) returns immediately. A positive lower endpoint advances the attempt; an unresolved interval advances the look. If both attempts fail, return budget_exhausted with the last statewise-safe candidate and do not claim the economic target is attained. No later look or attempt is run after success.

## Fresh policy-cost contrasts and simultaneous inference

After all five services for a task/target/seed finish, use a separate 65,536-path stream to compare the returned relu-exact policy with each of the other four returned policies. This stream is independent of all construction and stopping information under the sampling model. The paired comparison evaluates the original continuous law with forty-bit initial-state and innovation cells and analytic last-innovation integration. Retain signed path differences, including negative gains; no clipping based on expected safety is allowed. Exact identical-policy contrasts are zero identities, not independent observations.

The bounded empirical-Bernstein routine is inherited with logarithm upper bound 14, simultaneous family limit 2048 and total error 1/100. At most 90 services times 2 attempts times 3 looks times 3 estimands, plus 18 comparison sets times 4 contrasts, equals 1692 intervals. Exact rational arithmetic checks that exp(14)>4*2048/(1/100). Reused streams across modes do not invalidate the union account; confidence is conditional on the relevant frozen policies. Pseudorandom streams establish reproducibility under the independent-bin statistical model, not a proof of mathematical independence of a seed.

The fresh comparison service has its own complete process time. It is additional study overhead and is not hidden inside the own-target-service clock. At a common target report attainment, failure, actual cost, paired cost difference, action-search work, root counts, verified proposal relevance and complete work. The economic net difference includes replacement and computation prices; a critic-objective improvement alone is not an economic-policy improvement.

## Freezing, failures and publication

Before production, commit all scientific files and this protocol, run disjoint tiny correctness fixtures, and create SOURCE_FREEZE57.json. Production checks both that freeze and the inherited scientific freeze. Development fixtures use horizons, budgets and seeds outside the declared production cases. Preserve any failed production process and stop its task rather than silently overwrite it. A scientific correctness change after execution starts requires a new frozen cohort.

Publication regenerates every distinct action-search result and signed certificate from saved fitted parameters, reconstructs stopping decisions and validates stored moments against exact rational moments. It does not rerun training, draw additional observations or replace original clocks. Ordinary manuscript sources, response, all tables, the service registry, raw records, publication PDFs, clean-rebuild evidence and remote branch identities constitute the release.
