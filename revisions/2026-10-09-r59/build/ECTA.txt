23                                          1. INTRODUCTION                                       23

24                                                                                                24
       A continuation approximation matters because it changes an economic decision.
25                                                                                                25
     Its fitting error, the loss of its implemented policy, and the work needed to certify an
26                                                                                                26
     improvement are different objects. This paper develops Neural Bellman Operators
27                                                                                                27
     around that distinction. A construction uses its own fitted future values, returns
28                                                                                                28
     feasible action witnesses, and connects numerical resources to the cost of the policy
29                                                                                                29
     actually implemented.

1        There are two linked numerical contracts in the same controlled economy. Under         1

2    effective primitive moduli, the constructive backend returns a feasible policy with a      2

3    prescribed error account against the original Bellman optimum. Theorems 2 and 3            3

4    retain the construction and its exact continuous original-owner implementation. A          4

5    fitted backend instead proposes actions from learned features. Those proposals be- 5
6    come deployable only after a whole-cell policy certificate is satisfied. A prospective     6

7    service then stops when the complete policy attains a prespecified expected-cost           7

8    target relative to an installed controller. This additional contract neither changes the   8

9    Bellman optimum nor turns an empirical fitting error into an optimality guarantee. 9
10       The trained neural backend has an explicit computational structure. Under the          10

11   model’s scalar uniform innovation, the expectation of a one-hidden-layer ReLU              11

12   continuation is analytic. For the scalar investment action, the integrated objective       12

13   is piecewise quartic, and a finite collection of rationally isolated cubic stationary      13

14   points supplies exact action-lattice witnesses. This produces a terminating action         14

15   search for a fitted nonlinear continuation without invoking another unconverged            15

16   nonconvex optimizer. It is distinct from compiling a native min-plus envelope into         16

17   an identical neural circuit. The verification theorem nevertheless applies to both         17

18   neural and conventional candidates.                                                        18

19       The policy account is implemented on a locally split, non-tensor observation           19

20   partition. A one-state residual and value cache encloses the old policy’s contin- 20
21   uation without a full table of state pairs. A signed sensitivity calculation for the       21

22   smooth installed reference further retains economically useful continuation direc- 22
23   tion. We prove its validity and give explicit state-dimension, leaf-count and horizon      23

24   costs. The original nonlinear investment family has a dimension-uniform smooth- 24
25   reference stability bound, although the work needed for a chosen approximation             25

26   accuracy can still grow with dimension. Every later discontinuous acquired actor is        26

27   handled by the generic cache, not by differentiating away its jumps.                       27

29   tree-based Bellman continuations in dimensions two, four and eight and horizons            29

                                       15. CONCLUSION
7                                                                                              7

8      Neural Bellman Operators connect an own-future continuation to a feasible               8

9    action and an economic cost account. The constructive backend retains the original        9

10   Bellman-accuracy objective. The trained backend now has analytic innovation               10

11   integration and a terminating exact action-lattice search, while its proposals remain     11

12   subject to whole-cell true-advantage verification. These are complementary parts          12

13   of the same numerical method, not interchangeable claims about the name of a              13

14   function class.                                                                           14

15     The non-tensor cache and signed reference calculation turn the policy account           15

16   into an executable prospective service. Their proofs retain the cost of leaf member- 16
17   ship, critic evaluation, state dimension and horizon. The declared expected-cost          17

18   targets are attained in the sensitivity experiment, with genuine nonterminal action       18

19   changes and complete own-service clocks. Conventional generators share the same           19

20   verification treatment, and policy identities make their role observable. The finite ex- 20
21   periments identify successful returns and their costs; they do not establish universal    21

22   neural dominance or calibrated welfare effects.                                           22

23     The full-action gap, the actual expected cost and the price of acquiring or verifying   23

24   a policy remain separate economic quantities. Keeping all three explicit permits a        24

25   decision maker to choose the appropriate contract without treating fitting accuracy,      25

26   certificate sharpness or a favorable timing observation as a substitute for the cost of   26

27   the implemented policy.                                                                   27

28     The new action-search execution completes another part of that chain. Exact             28

29   rational witnesses eliminate the stored critic’s lattice optimization loss, and the       29

30   transfer theorem identifies the remaining acquisition, evaluation, continuous-cover       30

31   and verification obligations. The nested-candidate comparison makes a stricter            31

32   certified upper endpoint attributable to an added witness under a fixed verifier. 32
