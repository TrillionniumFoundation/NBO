We thank the referee for identifying the need to connect a valid policy
certificate to a specific candidate computation, a prospective economic
return and the complete work required to obtain that return. This
revision continues the original \textbf{Neural Bellman Operators} paper.
Its title, controlled economic laws, constructive own-future backend,
feasible action witnesses, primitive resource account and original
Bellman-accuracy objective are retained.

The controlling report is the 9 October 2026 advisory review on commit
\texttt{adf1256cff9cde365246a3db2dac90c72fda3b13}, assessing R54
manuscript \texttt{00e837adb431f4d4b5248fc6d5ff1fc927dd3b65}. It is not
a decision of the Econometric Society. The repository subsequently
completed the R56 and R57 manuscripts and a separately frozen R58
scientific execution. R59 starts from R58 development commit
\texttt{4b5f1cc3241fe8df103844f225913d91c387795b}, whose ordinary main
article still contained the R57 exposition. The revision integrates that
later execution, supplies its lossless-search and prospective-invariance
proofs, and reconstructs the complete scientific record. It does not
rename already executed services as new R59 observations.

The new main-article sections are \textbf{Lossless search for trained
neural action witnesses} and \textbf{Equal-policy work at prospective
economic targets}. They extend, rather than replace, the original
constructive operator and the fitted-witness transfer theory. The
supplement gives every timing repetition, complete service record,
component clock, activation-reduction count and datewise accuracy
account. The original seven-document family is retained: article,
technical supplement, referee response and four complete/development
companions.

The main new conclusion is precise. A lossless implementation of the
trained-neural action solver returns the same exact witness and, under
the stated controller premises, the same acquired policy, verification
endpoints and prospective stopping history as the algebraic
implementation. The 36-service matched execution identifies a reduction
in recorded complete-process median work at the same economic and
statistical accuracy. This is not a claim that identical policies have
different welfare, nor that a general safe-improvement theorem belongs
exclusively to neural representations.

\hypertarget{m1-and-b1-the-original-neural-computation-and-the-role-of-safe-improvement}{%
\subsection{M1 and B1: The original neural computation and the role of
safe
improvement}\label{m1-and-b1-the-original-neural-computation-and-the-role-of-safe-improvement}}

We retain the original NBO title and research object rather than replace
the manuscript with a differently titled safe-policy-improvement paper.
The reason is substantive: the article now distinguishes the
constructive operator, a trained-neural action computation, its lossless
implementation, and the representation-neutral verification rule that
makes the returned action economically admissible.

The constructive backend still produces an own-future continuation and
feasible witnesses from primitive moduli, with an explicit error and
resource account against the original Bellman optimum. The trained
backend is not merely a ReLU encoding of that min-plus construction. Its
stored affine--ReLU continuation has analytically integrated innovation
expectations and an exact action-lattice minimizer obtained by rational
hinge partition and cubic-root isolation. The preceding R57 execution
actually deployed that minimizer and retained common-only, menu-neural,
exact-quadratic and tree-based controls.

The new theorem reduces the work of computing precisely that
trained-neural witness. Features whose entire action range lies in one
integrated activation region are reduced exactly to polynomial
coefficients or zero. Remaining lattice objectives are enclosed outward;
only points proved unable to minimize are excluded. Surviving values are
compared rationally with the original tie rule. The proof permits
negative fitted weights and does not assume a convex fit. It also
establishes a survivor-gap bound, a conditional output-cardinality
bound, exact fallback behavior and explicit arithmetic/storage costs.

The interval-screening principle itself is representation neutral. The
NBO-specific implementation is the exact reduction and evaluation of
integrated trained ridges and its preservation of the original action
witness. Likewise, the incumbent-protection theorem remains
representation neutral and available to conventional generators. The
manuscript does not infer a universal neural advantage from either
principle.

\hypertarget{m2-and-b4-a-prospective-stopping-algorithm-not-a-retrospective-catalogue}{%
\subsection{M2 and B4: A prospective stopping algorithm, not a
retrospective
catalogue}\label{m2-and-b4-a-prospective-stopping-algorithm-not-a-retrospective-catalogue}}

The earlier R54 catalogue remains historical evidence and is not
relabeled as a prospective service. The subsequent R55/R57 protocols
introduced actual first-attainment controllers, and the R58 matched
experiment follows a separately frozen prospective rule. Every
solver/task/target/repetition service begins from primitives and pays
for its own fit, verification, inference and outputs.

The targets require ten- or twenty-percent expected-cost reductions from
installed zero investment. The estimand is the signed contrast
\texttt{J(candidate)-q\ J(installed)}, with \texttt{q=9/10} or
\texttt{4/5}, rather than a ratio of two estimated means. At each
construction attempt, the complete candidate is frozen before inference.
A nonpositive upper endpoint returns immediately. A positive lower
endpoint advances the construction attempt; an unresolved interval
advances the next declared inference look. Final failure returns a safe
policy with budget-exhausted status, not a claimed successful or
impossible target.

The R58 design has two declared construction attempts, three cumulative
inference looks and 36 separate complete processes. Its finite family
covers all possible cost, target-contrast and gain intervals. The audit
reconstructs every first-attainment decision and verifies that no look
or attempt was performed after the corresponding stopping condition.
Identical policies and streams yield identical stopping histories in the
two solver modes.

The new transcript-invariance theorem states its premise explicitly:
controller decisions depend on mathematical outputs, not elapsed time or
solver diagnostics. A wall-clock deadline is a different contract and
need not produce the same stopping history. Intermediate construction
attempts are not deployed; all are verified against the same smooth
installed reference, not against a silently differentiated discontinuous
updated actor.

\hypertarget{m3-and-b2-matched-economic-accuracy-and-the-full-computational-bill}{%
\subsection{M3 and B2: Matched economic accuracy and the full

The new comparison matches more than a nominal target. Corresponding
modes have identical fitted parameters, acquired partitions, action
witnesses, selected policies, whole-cell error accounts, interval-path
endpoints and statistical decisions. Their policy-cost difference is
therefore exactly zero under every admissible initial law. This is
stronger than an unresolved paired confidence interval, and it does not
require a new paired sampling service.

The complete recorded service includes process invocation, imports,
scientific-source checks, independently repeated fitting, every
attempted action search, verification, own inference, serialization,
process exit and log flush. The parent controller's later checkpoint
write is separately identified and is not described as zero overhead.
The supplement distinguishes component medians from complete-process
medians and reports all output bytes and resident-memory peaks.

Thirty of the 36 services attain the declared target. Six exhaust the
budget at the eight-state, six-date twenty-percent target. All are
retained. The complete-process algebraic-to-screened median ratios lie
between approximately 1.028 and 1.101 across the six task--target
groups. The unsuccessful group is explicitly a comparison of complete
exhaustion work, not successful first-attainment work. All three timing
repetitions are printed rather than selecting the best run.

The R57 independent neural-versus-conventional cost comparisons remain
the evidence for those distinct policies. Equality between the two
lossless implementations is not transferred to other generators, and the
new within-neural work gain is not used to reverse the inherited
conventional ranking.

\hypertarget{m4-and-b3-error-decomposition-and-a-targeted-removable-cost}{%
\subsection{M4 and B3: Error decomposition and a targeted removable
cost}\label{m4-and-b3-error-decomposition-and-a-targeted-removable-cost}}

The fitted-witness transfer theorem already separates center
optimization loss, within-cell action-contrast variation,
action-relevant continuation error, continuous-to-robust-lattice
coverage and verification overshoot. Its explicit neural moduli and the
directly computed continuous-action cover remain in the main article.
Exact fitted minimization removes only the first error term.

R59 targets the computational cost of obtaining that zero optimization
error without changing the remaining error budget. The lossless solver
returns exactly the original algebraic witness, so its fitted
optimization error, acquired feasibility, verified action gap and
propagated Bellman-loss account are unchanged. A tighter numerical
stopping condition is not smuggled into one mode to create a favorable
runtime comparison.

The recorded datewise quantities include \texttt{U-L}, \texttt{U-C} and
\texttt{C-L}, selected actions, extra-witness changes and the baseline
policy without the added exact candidate. These quantities add cellwise;
their separately displayed maxima need not add. The new tables preserve
this convention. The all-state policy-loss account includes the
installed-reference continuation term and is not replaced with the local
greedy gap.

The new survivor bound quantifies the effect of endpoint width on
exact-comparison work. Under an additional discrete quadratic-growth
condition it also bounds the number of survivors. The condition is
stated, proved and tested as a conditional result; a global curvature
constant is not inferred merely because this catalogue happened to leave
one survivor per query. The paper does not claim to have performed an
unexecuted six-factor causal error ablation.

\hypertarget{m5-and-b5-non-tensor-multidimensional-execution-and-explicit-scaling-costs}{%
\subsection{M5 and B5: Non-tensor multidimensional execution and
explicit scaling
costs}\label{m5-and-b5-non-tensor-multidimensional-execution-and-explicit-scaling-costs}}

The retained and new prospective services use the original nonlinear
cyclic investment family at dimension/horizon pairs \texttt{(2,2)},
\texttt{(4,4)} and \texttt{(8,6)}. Their observations use locally split
binary partitions with 32 or 64 leaves, not unequal-spaced tensor grids
presented as non-tensor. Robust capacities and downward action indices
are valid on entire leaves. The signed smooth-reference verifier and its
dimension/horizon work account remain common to all candidate
generators.

R59 adds a distinct work account for trained-neural action recovery. For
width \texttt{m}, dimension \texttt{d}, \texttt{N} action-lattice
points, \texttt{m\_c} crossing ridges and \texttt{s} survivors,
coefficient formation/reduction requires \texttt{O(dm)} arithmetic
operations; interval screening requires \texttt{O(N(1+m\_c))} interval
operations; exact original-objective comparison requires \texttt{O(sm)}
rational operations. The vectorized implementation uses
\texttt{O(N(1+m\_c))} interval working storage. Rational bit costs are
explicitly separate.

These bounds do not erase dimension, horizon, acquisition or lattice
dependence. Nor is the new screen claimed to be universally faster:
valid wide intervals may retain the whole lattice, and a fallback pays
for the original exact solver. The recorded multidimensional executions
and operation counts are genuine; different task runners do not
establish a hardware-controlled asymptotic scaling law. The original
stronger Bellman-accuracy target remains in the theoretical and
returned-policy error account.

\hypertarget{m6-and-b6-strong-controls-and-an-attributable-intervention}{%
\subsection{M6 and B6: Strong controls and an attributable
intervention}\label{m6-and-b6-strong-controls-and-an-attributable-intervention}}

The manuscript retains the R57 five-mode experiment: common-only,
ReLU-menu, ReLU-exact, quadratic-exact and ExtraTrees-menu. Common-only
legitimately incurs no fictional fitting cost. Every mode receives the
same exact terminal analysis and critic-independent signed verifier.
Matched ReLU-menu/exact parameters isolate the added witness, while the
raw nested-candidate record attributes strict endpoint and
selected-action changes to that addition.

The R58 experiment answers the next question within that already
executed neural generator: can the same exact witness be returned with
less work? It changes only the nonterminal solver. Each mode
independently reconstructs the fit, and the audit checks the resulting
parameters rather than assuming identity from seeds. The same actions
enter the same verifier in the same order. This prevents a change of
verifier, fit, target or policy from being credited to the screening
algorithm.

The observed reduction in exact comparisons has a complete accounting
counterpart: all lattice points evaluated by the interval screen are
counted. Terminal root solving is identical and excluded from the
reported neural intervention counts. The earlier evidence that the
common menu determines many actions is preserved; the paper does not
assign the whole verifier's economic gain to the neural proposal.

\subsection{M7 and B7: Fitted approximate structure and transition

The complete sample-split learned-direction theorem and R55
structural-uncertainty execution are retained from R56/R57. Directions
are fitted on construction data; independent validation forms a
confidence box for uncertain action exposure; the same confidence box
qualifies the nuisance allowance and the remaining action comparison.
The corrected gate does not obtain an unknown true exposure from the
simulator as a free verification oracle.

The maintained uncertainty model has a known nonlinear drift and bounded
action-invariant innovation structure with uncertain exposure. The
manuscript states this scope rather than generalizing it to an arbitrary
unknown transition kernel. The original fitted-direction result is also
kept distinct from the earlier deterministic hand-perturbation
catalogue, including their different harmful plug-in counts and
statistical meanings.

The new lossless search concerns a fixed stored fitted objective and
therefore does not add an estimation assumption to that structural
procedure. It preserves the exact witness supplied to any separately
valid uncertainty-qualified verifier. It does not turn a fitted
approximately null direction into an exact identity or waive the
confidence-set allowance.

\hypertarget{m8-and-b8-controlled-repetition-versus-independent-learned-objects}{%
\subsection{M8 and B8: Controlled repetition versus independent learned
objects}\label{m8-and-b8-controlled-repetition-versus-independent-learned-objects}}

The R57 experiment contains three distinct fitted seed realizations,
checked by parameter identity. Its timing variability mixes changes in
learned objects and computation. R58 instead deliberately repeats one
fixed fitted workload and its path streams three times, isolating
repeated work measurements. The two kinds of evidence are separately
identified and are not pooled into a larger statistical sample.

Within each task, the R58 processes run sequentially on one available
core with single-thread numerical libraries. Solver order alternates by
target and repetition. The full environment, governor availability,
memory, serialized bytes and complete process clocks are retained.
Frequency was not controlled, and the paper says so. All three values
and their medians appear in the supplement.

The audit reconstructs 2,304 distinct solver executions across the six
saved model/verification inputs, including both algorithms and common
terminal calls. There are 864 distinct nonterminal neural queries. It
regenerates nine distinct stopping streams comprising 380,928 path rows,
checks 132 recorded inference looks and 396 cost/contrast/gain interval
records, and verifies 9,216 cell-date decisions across the full service
catalogue. Memoized repeated calculations are checked by input and
output identities, not counted as new independent observations. These
are finite, reproducible workload results, not a population guarantee
over economic tasks or hardware.

\hypertarget{m9-and-b9-an-economic-consequence-at-an-unchanged-answer}{%
\subsection{M9 and B9: An economic consequence at an unchanged
answer}\label{m9-and-b9-an-economic-consequence-at-an-unchanged-answer}}

The executed targets concern actual expected cost under the original
continuous initial and innovation laws. The positive gain intervals
relative to the installed controller remain the basis for adoption
decisions, including the eight-dimensional case whose stronger
twenty-percent target is not certified at the available budget.

The new implementation comparison has an especially direct economic
interpretation. When both solvers return exactly the same policy, its
economic cost cancels from their total-cost comparison. For specified
computation charges and a nonnegative computation price, the total-cost
difference is exactly the price times the charge difference. Relative to
installation, the same gain interval is shifted by the implementation
charge and installation fee. A lower specified computational charge
expands the supported adoption region without inventing a difference in
policy welfare.

The main article states and proves this result and reports the complete
equal-policy work measurements that make it operational in the finite
study. Recorded medians do not provide a distribution-free
expected-runtime estimate, and no external computation-price or
installation-fee calibration is claimed. The contribution is a
constructive and executable method for obtaining a verified economic
answer more economically on the recorded matched workloads, together
with its exact policy and stopping invariance; it is not an asserted
universal inability of conventional methods to obtain that answer.
