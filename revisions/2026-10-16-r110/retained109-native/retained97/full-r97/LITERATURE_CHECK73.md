# R73 primary-source and style check

The new mathematical contribution is not a claim of priority for confidence
sequences, Hoeffding bounds, Ville's inequality, convex readout fitting or safe
policy improvement. The main text distinguishes those established principles
from its original-law NBO observation and qualification contracts.

* Howard, S. R., A. Ramdas, J. McAuliffe and J. Sekhon (2020), Time-uniform
  Chernoff bounds via nonnegative supermartingales, Probability Surveys 17,
  257–317. Primary publisher DOI: 10.1214/18-PS321. This supports the general
  nonnegative-supermartingale concentration connection, not the new NBO dual.
* Karampatziakis, N., P. Mineiro and A. Ramdas (2021), Off-Policy Confidence
  Sequences, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 139, 5301–5310.
  Primary source: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/karampatziakis21a.html.
  The comparison concerns contextual-bandit evaluation, not an assertion that
  the present simulated original-law NBO contrasts are importance weighted.

The Econometric Society's Information for Authors page links its LaTeX support
and instructions. The official Paper Length Guidance encourages shorter papers
and explicitly does not impose one universal page limit. The R73 limits of
45 pages for its active main article, 25 for its Technical Supplement and
150 abstract words are internal revision targets, not an official journal rule.
The detailed instruction subpages were not retrievable in this check; no claim
of an independently verified current submission checklist is based on them.
