\section{Computed effect of the restart restriction}\label{sec:restart-results}
\subsection{Fixed comparison and provenance}
The restart experiment holds the two-sided operating witnesses, support portfolio, deployed policies, primitives, tolerances, and installed rules fixed. Its only change is to strengthen the lower cost bound by Theorem~\ref{thm:restart}. The local multiplier portfolio is the same eight-element set used for the support calculations. The protocol was committed before implementing or evaluating this propagation. The preceding support results and the inherited maintenance evidence were already known. This is a prospectively specified extension within an exploratory research program, not an independently preregistered confirmation or a new neural training cohort.

All 42 configurations are computed and independently checked. The additional proof object records eight local minima per decision date, their action selectors, the outer source selector, and the complete propagated function. Its canonical hash binds it to the previously certified primal--dual input and its support hashes. In a fresh process, the complete audit rechecks the 252 base objects and then the 42 propagated objects with the optimizing envelope routines disabled. Thus 294 exact certificate objects, including every whole-cell and isolated-point comparison, constitute the final audit.
