9      We thank the referee for recognizing the positive high-dimensional menu evi- 9
10   dence and for distinguishing a valid retrospective certificate from a prospective        10

11   computation. This response addresses the advisory report of 5 October 2026 on the        11

12   original Neural Bellman Operators paper. The report was commissioned by the              12

13   repository owner, not the Econometric Society. The reviewed publication and report       13

14   are pinned in the source baseline.                                                       14

15     The revision develops centered decision loss, continuation transport and refresh,      15

16   true-objective stopping, and a completed query-volume comparison. It retains every       16

17   prior economic model and all favorable, unfavorable and unresolved scientific            17

18   records. Four detailed components move intact to the supplement. The integrated          18

19   computation replays the already frozen design and the same three streams; it is          19

20   not counted as additional independent observations. All 252 service identities and       20

21   failed checks are retained. The reported machine clocks belong to that particular        21

22   complete execution.                                                                      22

                     B2. SYMMETRIC RISK AND ITS CONNECTION TO DECISIONS
9                                                                                             9

10       The new completed study evaluates every scalar predictor at the same exogenous       10

11   lower, middle and upper actions, and separately at that predictor’s own selected         11

12   action. It reports absolute continuation risk, centered risk, own-action risk, finite- 12
13   menu payoff loss and binary/menu misclassification. Full finite-law means provide        13

14   the target, so a noisy squared simulation outcome is not mislabeled absolute risk. 14
15   Raw caches are regenerated across three complete fitting/cache streams. The risk- 15

17   centered error; the primary scalar-interval guarantee is obtained independently          17

18   from the true objective. Three fixed streams are retained individually and are not       18

19   presented as precise inference over every future cache distribution. The earlier 24      19

                  B5. CONTRIBUTION-ISOLATING COMPARATORS AND LITERATURE
20                                                                                            20

21     We add quadratic and radial-basis postdecision regressions using the same roll- 21
22   out value and derivative labels as the scalar NBO, a shared state-and-task actor,        22

23   and full-support enumeration alongside cached SAA. These complement the inher- 23
24   ited vector-costate, materialized-Raw and direct-policy comparisons. The literature      24

25   discussion now distinguishes postdecision approximation, regression continua- 25
26   tion methods, fitted value iteration, actor–critic methods, simulation reuse and         26

27   valid sequential assessment. The center/contrast identity, strong-concavity residual     27

28   inequality, difference-constraint closure and probability tools are identified as stan- 28
29   dard ingredients. The claimed contribution is their economic linkage and completed       29

30   comparison, not a new principle of optimality or a universal superiority theorem         30

31   for neural regression.                                                                   31
