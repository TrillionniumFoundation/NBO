# Response to the R18 Referee Report on Neural Bellman Operators

This response addresses the repository-owner-commissioned advisory report of 5 October 2026, review commit `74f0a278aeeb5c62fa556a65f758092621cedf02`, on the R18 publication `20afc6c1c4828e3c469e7906366bcc8730cc04d0`. It is not a representation that the report was an Econometric Society editorial decision.

We thank the referee for recognizing the positive high-dimensional menu evidence and for distinguishing a valid retrospective certificate from a prospective computation. The revision develops the original paper through a centered decision-loss theorem, transport and refresh conditions, a true-objective stopping certificate, and a completed query-volume study with stronger surrogates and symmetric diagnostics. All previous models, proof blocks and favorable, unfavorable or unresolved scientific records remain available. Four detailed components are moved intact to the supplement to improve the main argument's order.

The integrated execution repeats the already frozen design and the same three streams. It is not counted as additional independent observations. All 252 service executions and their failed checks are retained; no outcome-dependent change to the frozen scientific modules or economic inputs is made. Machine clocks from distinct executions are not pooled or silently substituted.

## B1. Beyond an identical-future menu

The main article now proves a continuation-transport theorem for bounded changes in future transitions and rewards. A second form treats recursive evaluation operators on a verified invariant utility domain. Together with the centered decision-loss proposition, these results define a nontrivial neighborhood of counterfactual economies in which an anchor continuation can be reused, and an explicit condition for refresh. This answers the theorem route requested in the report rather than inferring broad applicability from a finite menu. An explicit finite-feature corollary also derives sufficient fitting and Raw simulation budgets, accounts for dictionary construction and legitimate cache reuse, and gives a complete-work threshold. Its assumptions are not asserted for an arbitrary all-layer neural fit. The original controlled-economy, recursive-utility, preference, temporal-self and game models remain. Their individual domains and deviation criteria are not waived. The new finite-support study is one capital application, not a fabricated experiment across all those models.

Locations: `sec:r19decision`, `thm:r19transport`, `app:r19proofs`.

## B2. Symmetric risk and its connection to decisions

The new completed study evaluates every scalar predictor at the same exogenous lower, middle and upper actions, and separately at that predictor's own selected action. It reports absolute continuation risk, centered risk, own-action risk, finite-menu payoff loss and binary/menu misclassification. Full finite-law means provide the target, so a noisy squared simulation outcome is not mislabeled absolute risk. Raw caches are regenerated across three complete fitting/cache streams. The risk-to-decision proposition explains exactly which finite-menu loss is bounded by centered error; the primary scalar-interval guarantee is obtained independently from the true objective. Three fixed streams are retained individually and are not presented as precise inference over every future cache distribution. The earlier 24 conditional comparisons remain unchanged, including the absence of a favorable NBO comparison at the 64-path budget.

Locations: `prop:r19decision`, `tab:r19risk`, `sec:r19experiment`.

## B3. Real stopping rather than a retrospective selected clock

Seven procedures now execute a frozen sequence of budgets. Each service stops at its first true-objective certificate below the common tolerance, charges all attempted fits, queries, failed checks and durable records, and otherwise remains unresolved. Every query volume receives an actual service execution, not a prefix allocation from a longer run. The 252 service identities and all stage records are published. A deterministic outward certificate makes the stopping guarantee simultaneous across the attempted stages without a statistical optional-stopping shortcut. The theorem also states the conditional-error requirement for a stochastic implementation. The complete comparison bill remains separate from an individual deployment bill: we do not charge only the retrospectively cheapest method for a search that ran them all. This integrated execution is a replay of the already frozen source and seeds, not an additional independent experiment.

Locations: `prop:r19stopping`, `tab:r19components`, `app:r19record`.

## B4. Query-volume amortization frontier

The executed volumes are 1, 4, 16, 64, 256 and 1,024 in both dimensions. Tables report the actual complete work needed to return the declared accuracy, retain unsuccessful services, and separate fixed/cache/fitting work, queries and all verification checks. The strict break-even formula states what follows when a fixed-plus-linear cost model is justified; no such shape is imposed on the measured costs. Cost bounds require upper-versus-lower separation before a guaranteed ranking can be inferred. The tables identify an observed discrete frontier on this machine, not an invented continuous breakpoint between tested volumes. Conventional procedures are retained when they are cheaper.

Locations: `eq:r19break`, `tab:r19frontier10`, `tab:r19frontier50`, `tab:r19components`.

## B5. Contribution-isolating comparators and literature

We add quadratic and radial-basis postdecision regressions using the same rollout value and derivative labels as the scalar NBO, a shared state-and-task actor, and full-support enumeration alongside cached SAA. These complement the inherited vector-costate, materialized-Raw and direct-policy comparisons. The literature discussion now distinguishes postdecision approximation, regression continuation methods, fitted value iteration, actor--critic methods, simulation reuse and valid sequential assessment. The center/contrast identity, strong-concavity residual inequality, difference-constraint closure and probability tools are identified as standard ingredients. The claimed contribution is their economic linkage and completed comparison, not a new principle of optimality or a universal superiority theorem for neural regression.

Locations: `sec:r19experiment`, `sec:r19decision`.

## B6. Economic meaning of the decision menu and tolerance

The new exercise computes current withdrawal responses to temporary resource charges while preserving the capital production law and fixed future policy. Its task distribution is stated before the numerical procedures and is not represented as an estimated empirical distribution. We prove that the true optimal withdrawal is nonincreasing in the charge, give the interior derivative, report zero-charge counterfactuals using the same fitted objects, and convert the welfare-loss allowance into an external log-utility grant. The primary machine guarantee remains the outward welfare-loss bound; the grant display is a descriptive binary64 conversion. This supplies a interpretable model counterfactual and a compensated-loss normalization without inventing an observed tax experiment or an equilibrium welfare conclusion.

Locations: `eq:r19capitaltask`, `eq:r19charge`, `tab:r19economic`.

The original continuous-time formulation, transfer bounds, exact-trace HJB comparison, utility selection and application proofs are all retained, including their mixed numerical findings. We strengthen the shared economic decision account without transferring a finite certificate to an unverified control class.
