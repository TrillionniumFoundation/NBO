# Primary literature used in the added discussion

The new manuscript adds attribution, not novelty claims, for these established methods.

- Longstaff, Francis A., and Eduardo S. Schwartz (2001), *Valuing American Options by Simulation: A Simple Least-Squares Approach*, Review of Financial Studies 14(1), 113–147. Publisher DOI: 10.1093/rfs/14.1.113.
- Powell, Warren B. (2011), *Approximate Dynamic Programming: Solving the Curses of Dimensionality*, second edition, Wiley. Publisher DOI: 10.1002/9781118029176.
- Konda, Vijay R., and John N. Tsitsiklis (1999), *Actor-Critic Algorithms*, Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 12. Official proceedings: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/1999/hash/6449f44a102fde848669bdd9eb6b76fa-Abstract.html .
- Ernst, Damien, Pierre Geurts, and Louis Wehenkel (2005), *Tree-Based Batch Mode Reinforcement Learning*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 6(18), 503–556. Official article: https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v6/ernst05a.html .
- Howard, Steven R., Aaditya Ramdas, Jon McAuliffe, and Jasjeet Sekhon (2021), *Time-Uniform, Nonparametric, Nonasymptotic Confidence Sequences*, Annals of Statistics 49(2), 1055–1080. DOI: 10.1214/20-AOS1991; author preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08240 .

The new deterministic finite-support certificate uses no estimated confidence sequence. Sequential statistical allocation is stated separately as a conditional extension. Difference-constraint closure and fixed-dictionary least squares are explicitly identified as standard tools.
