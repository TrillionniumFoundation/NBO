\section*{Blocking concerns B1--B10}
\paragraph{B1. Neural specificity.}
We add a same-information, same-geometry conventional hinge arm. Its entire policy and certificate records are exactly identical to ReLU's in all risk-by-service comparisons. Main Sections~\ref{sec:tail109} and~\ref{sec:study109} distinguish action-aligned approximation requirements from neural exclusivity. The compiler and safeguard are offered to every representation. We do not label their benefits neural-specific. A neural-exclusive competitive region remains unestablished; that scientific obligation is not marked resolved by a method-neutral improvement.

\paragraph{B2. Information complexity.}
The nonuniform-law and randomized-quantifier results of Section~\ref{sec:information99} remain stated with the exact oracle restrictions inherited from R99. The current addition is the positive-gap precision scale in Section~\ref{sec:improvement109}, with a full paid dyadic-work bound and a precise interval-information obstruction. The compiler uses formula access and is not misrepresented as violating a point-value lower bound. No randomized optimal-rate, multivariate tractability or end-to-end Bellman information lower bound is claimed without proof.

\paragraph{B3. Representation versus decisions.}
The exact R99 original-decision mechanism and its proofs are retained, including the equivalence with conventional oblique hinges. The current investment experiment directly reports original policy costs, reference-sharpened regret and action-distance bounds, not only function approximation. In the $3/4$ catalogue ReLU's cost is slightly below rank-six quadratic's, but hinges reproduce the same gain and online is still better; at $1/2$ the ordering reverses. This is a genuine economic-objective comparison, not a neural separation theorem.

\paragraph{B4. Misaligned acquisition.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:tail109} proves why pointwise directional error alone misses a changing adverse tail and supplies the additional centered quantile-band term. The exact counterexample has zero directional error but original regret $1/4$. Proposition~\ref{prop:safe109} then verifies actual cost improvement against a completed incumbent. The executed acquisition remains the frozen least-squares procedure. It is not retroactively called a prospective test of the new tail-aware training loss; such a test remains separate research.

\paragraph{B5. Complete work and conventional competitiveness.}
Tables~\ref{tab:clocks109}--\ref{tab:economics109} and the full Development Appendix report every arm. The formula-derived Bellman oracle is a strong semi-analytic comparator available to online and learned services alike. All labels, candidate trials, audits, serialization, certificates, repairs and refused safeguard trials are charged. Compilation lowers observed complete clocks, but inexpensive conventional services remain competitive and safeguards add substantial work. The R97 quadratic victory is unchanged.

\paragraph{B6. Economic purchase and calibration.}
We replace no historical sensitivity region by an invented adoption claim. The new tables contain strict original-policy cost reductions and their actual additional computational charges. Section~\ref{sec:improvement109} states a unit-consistent stopping dominance rule; prices, workloads and external welfare calibration remain inputs. There is no newly calibrated purchase region or neural adoption over quadratic claimed here. This concern remains partly open at the application level.

\paragraph{B7. New actors and population inference.}

The comparison supplies exact numerical bounds on the complete catalogue, not population confidence intervals.
