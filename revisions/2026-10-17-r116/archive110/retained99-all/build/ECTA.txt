20       The acquisition problem is more demanding than certificate validity. Our retained    20

21   matched experiment finds that every certificate-oriented readout improves its vali- 21
22   dated residual span, but aggregate first-proposal rejections increase from 644 to 757. 22
23   Quadratic least squares remains the complete-work winner. We therefore derive            23

24   an action-aligned certificate that separates the original-objective enclosure width, a   24

25   supporting-slope mismatch and the surrogate’s constrained stationarity error. A          25

26   supporting slope is not assumed to be an exact derivative of the optimal continua- 26
27   tion. Its verified quadratic minorant directly bounds the proposal’s all-action gap. 27
28   Selection over a prospectively fixed menu then has a population rejection guarantee,     28

29   or a complete-work guarantee when work itself is the selected bounded loss. These        29

30   results explain which training objective is entitled to predict which service outcome. 30
