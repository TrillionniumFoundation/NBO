The historical ledger is a retention/location aid, not a verdict that every old scientific objection has been closed. In particular, an editorial rearrangement does not fix an incorrect historical numerical example; a conditional Brownian verification theorem does not rerun an NDU application; the current deterministic catalogue does not supply an independent population experiment; and a successful current source closure does not replace missing old evidence. Full historical dependencies and remaining scientific obligations are stated separately in OPEN_OBLIGATIONS110.md.
