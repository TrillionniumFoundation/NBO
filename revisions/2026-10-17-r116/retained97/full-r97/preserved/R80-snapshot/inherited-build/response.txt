23       The new sharp deployment-allocation theorem extends the original controller- 23
24   choice problem. Nonnegative controller shares and a common attaining cost po- 24
25   tential reduce worst-case expected catalogue regret to a finite matrix game. Exact         25

26   primal and dual feasible probabilities certify the solution and its optimization           26

27   gap. The new proof distinguishes a pre-observation mixture of entire policies from         27

28   averaging actions, fitted coefficients or information sets. Installation costs are not     28

29   presumed linear: an installed-subset formulation charges every supported controller        29

30   and retains outside controllers as oracle comparators. A finite-cohort construction        30

31   realizes the allocation in expectation without pretending that the statistical sample      31

32   size is the deployment horizon.                                                            32

           1.9. M7 / B7. Independently reimplement the critical numerical calculations
13                                                                                           13

14     The original-law cost experiment has two separate full-bin implementations. One       14

15   uses outward binary64 arithmetic on centered primitives. The other uses directed        15

16   integer endpoints on a dyadic grid, expanded polynomials and independently              16

17   enumerated acquisition rectangles, importing none of the production economic or         17

18   actor routines. Both evaluate every new bin for every controller. The published end- 18
19   points use their hull, intersected with the proved support, rather than an optimistic   19

20   intersection. Additional exact-Fraction trajectories and boundary tests complement      20

21   the full-bin replay.                                                                    21

22     The R79 release procedure repeats that full dual-arithmetic calculation and re- 22
23   computes all statistical intervals. The new decision calculation is independently       23

24   implemented using all-pairs Floyd closure and repeated single-source Bellman– 24
25   Ford relaxation. Exact cost potentials are checked against every original constraint    25

26   and attain every claimed upper difference. Negative cycles cause failure; they are      26

27   not repaired by widening selected intervals. Exact fee-boundary and minimax-tie         27

28   tests exercise the economic conclusions.                                                28

29     This fully covers the critical new constructed-controller cost and choice calcu- 29
30   lations. It does not independently regenerate every descendant of the historical        30

31   eight-million-query vector computation. That distinction and the earlier aliasing       31

32   counterexample remain explicit.                                                         32

17   the original economy. It is not substituted for stronger conventional query baselines. 17
18   Matched attained ReLU-versus-Bernstein comparisons and gains over the installed          18

19   reference are separate results.                                                          19

20       The new theorem gives the exact maximum catalogue regret implied by the              20

21   retained cost intervals. Minimizing this quantity uses precise paired information        21

22   instead of only marginal upper costs. It yields a controller choice for every resource- 22
23   charge vector on one coverage event. The attaining-potential proof states exactly        23

24   the sense in which the bound is sharp: relative to the retained interval inequalities,   24

25   not relative to all information in the raw observations or all possible statistical      25

26   procedures.                                                                              26

30   coarse controller is (u-f)+. The fine controller is universally optimal below l, the     30

31   coarse one above u, and the regret-minimizing choice changes at (l+u)/2, approxi- 31
