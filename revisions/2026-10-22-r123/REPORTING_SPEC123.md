This specification is part of F123. It governs reporting before the independent 32-state draw. PROTOCOL123.md governs the economic law, unchanged actors, four services, fixed menus, prospective cost and inference rules. The exact executable definitions are code123/report123.py and code123/stats123.py; numerical outputs may not change those rules.

## Simultaneous confidence statement

Condition on the published source and fixed actors and valid numerical premises. States are32independent draws from the prespecified finite dyadic distribution. Safe gains lie in[0,tau], signed qualified comparisons in[-tau,tau], tau=1/256. Two-sided Hoeffding radii are7tau/16 and7tau/8. The common exponent is49/4. A degree 30 rational Taylor lower bound for its exponential exceeds 46400, so union failures are below .01 for232 safe means and .03 for696 signed means. One-sided Clopper--Pearson event bounds use exact binomial sums, individual failure 1/23200 and48 rational bisections, total .01. Event threshold times its probability lower bound also bounds mean safe gain. The entire joint assertion has failure at most .05, with arbitrary dependence across methods, risks and repetitions. No asymptotic normal, clock confidence interval or unregistered threshold is substituted.

## Full cost and price menus

Carry each actor's actual historical complete acquisition allocation, including fitting/label acquisition/common process work. Each fresh service pays its own entire original certification and fallback service; no paid support is handed across services. Keep all refused candidate arithmetic, probes, failed enhancement attempts, original-law calls, serialization, cold model load and process residual. Distinguish actual research-parent wall time from nested components so it is not double-counted. Historical unknown interrupted parent durations remain unknown.

For each actual primary/timing process, service cell and deployment volume N in{32,256,4096,65536}, use C(N)=A+H+L+(N/n)(T-L), where A is historical complete actor acquisition allocation, H is the full parent residual charged to a standalone service, L is its measured cold load, T its measured cohort and n the actual cohort size. These are explicitly conditional extrapolations, not measured deployment throughput. Repeat all calculations with each actual clock and its matching sampled economics; do not select a favorable clock.

These use deterministic numerical interval comparisons on the sampled scenarios, not unprovided population confidence intervals for all pairwise menus. Actual external adoption requires the declared loss-to-time price, deployment volume, hardware and validation premises. No universal or neural-specific adoption is presumed.

## Output and failure rules

The reporter writes complete machine-readable economics, inference, cost, constraints, menus, clock intersections, source/input hashes and research ledger. It never fits a model, draws states, reruns clocks, selects cells or changes the economic solver. Any failed run keeps its original output directory and outer log. Corrected infrastructure may receive a separately documented new directory; a science change requires a new prospective freeze and must not retroactively change F123. Development history and current confirmatory evidence remain separately identifiable. New main-text tables are generated from the full output with declared aggregations, not a subset chosen for favorable results.
