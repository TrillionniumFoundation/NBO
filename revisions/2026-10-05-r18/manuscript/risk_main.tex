\section{Continuation Risk at the Economic Action}\label{sec:r18risk}
The payoff comparison establishes the economic performance of the complete
NBO procedure. To assess the fitted continuation itself, we compare its
prediction with a cached simulation predictor at the same action pair.
For query $j$, let $x_j^+$ be the postdecision state of the final NBO action
and $x_j^0$ that of the reference action. The object to be predicted is
$c_j=S(x_j^+)-S(x_j^0)$ in the finite economy of
Section~\ref{sec:r16menumain}. The scalar network supplies
$p_{Nj}=\widehat S_N(x_j^+)-\widehat S_N(x_j^0)$; cached Raw supplies
$p_{Rj}$. Both are evaluated at these same states. Conditional on the
complete training record, these states, and the realized prediction caches,
define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:r18riskmain}
 R_M=J^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^J(p_{Mj}-c_j)^2,\qquad M\in\{N,R\}.
\end{equation}
Conditioning permits nonlinear fitting and learned features. It does not
replace the fitted network by a fixed linear dictionary.

A fresh common-future-path contrast $Z_{jk}$ has conditional mean $c_j$.
The difference of its two squared prediction errors is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:r18cancelmain}
 T_{jk}=(p_{Nj}-p_{Rj})(p_{Nj}+p_{Rj}-2Z_{jk}).
\end{equation}
Its conditional mean averages to $R_N-R_R$, and its conditional variance
is $4(p_{Nj}-p_{Rj})^2\operatorname{Var}(Z_{jk})$.
The common squared-noise term cancels exactly. Centering the remaining
continuation residual by its known terminal quadratic component gives a
bounded statistic with explicit clipping and arithmetic allowances.
Propositions~\ref{prop:r17-cancellation} and~\ref{prop:r17-risk-certificate}
give the complete identities and finite-sample interval. The same scalar
differences define a population contrast-training loss
(Corollary~\ref{cor:r17-training}); the policies reported here are the
unchanged fitted policies, not policies retrained with that loss.

The independent assessment fixes all predictions and geometric bounds before
drawing its audit innovations. It retains four calibrations, both dimensions,
all sixteen streams, and all 384 queries per stream. Each query receives 256
independent antithetic pairs. A sign pair is averaged into one observation;
the same audit is reused across the three Raw budgets and is not counted
three times as independent information. There are 12,582,912 distinct
pair observations over the eight cells. The comparison conditions on each
realized Raw cache, rather than averaging over unexecuted caches. It also
conditions on NBO's chosen action pairs; it is not an occupation-risk claim
for every feasible action or for Raw's separately chosen actor.

Table~\ref{tab:r18risk} reports all 24 comparisons, not only those favoring
learning. Eight intervals have upper endpoint below $-10^{-8}$ and establish
lower NBO contrast risk. Four have positive lower endpoint and favor Raw;
twelve include zero. At the four-path budget, every Quarterly, Long, and
Intermediate cell favors NBO. At sixteen paths, the ten-dimensional
Quarterly and Intermediate cells also favor NBO. In contrast, the
fifty-dimensional Low cell favors Raw at every budget, and Long in ten
dimensions favors the 64-path Raw predictor. This pattern identifies where
the observed fitted continuation improves prediction, and where additional
simulation removes that advantage.

The preassigned risk margin $10^{-8}$ is the square of the payoff unit
$10^{-4}$, not a payoff difference. Proposition~\ref{prop:r17-decisions}
bounds average binary-decision loss by $\sqrt{R_M}$, but a smaller risk
need not give a better action: prediction errors with different signs can
reverse that ordering. A difference of risks is not an absolute-risk
certificate. Accordingly, the risk result and the actual five-method payoff
result provide distinct evidence for NBO; neither is substituted for the
other.

The risk family has failure probability at most $.01$. Combining it with
the menu payoff family, whose allocation is $.018$, gives simultaneous
coverage at least $.972$ by a union bound, without requiring independence
between families. Combining it instead with all four earlier scientific
families, including their scalar events, gives coverage at least $.94$.
The individual family guarantees remain unchanged. The source freeze,
pre-audit receipts, and exact report replay appear in
Section~\ref{app:r18record}.
