\subsection{Independent payoff confirmation}\label{sec:r16replicationresults}
The independent confirmation evaluates all 128 previously selected policies,
without retraining or further selection. Table~\ref{tab:r16-replication-methods}
reports the schedule-relative method gains and the associated full-adapted-class
regret bounds. Table~\ref{tab:r16-replication-comparisons} reports every direct
NBO comparison under both the original and the prospectively fixed paired
transfer. All eight method endpoints and six direct contrasts belong to the
declared 238-event family, with total error allocation $0.01$.

The lower endpoints for NBO minus the original neural-HJB implementation are
$0.000176$ in dimension ten and $0.001342$ in dimension fifty, rounded
downward. Both exceed the prespecified materiality margin $10^{-4}$.
In dimension ten the original transfer still gives an interval containing
zero on these new observations. Thus the new experiment confirms the value
of the sharper paired account under a proof, constants, and reporting rule
fixed before its bank. The earlier use of that account remains a post-freeze
analytical reanalysis of the earlier observations.

The four NBO--Raw and NBO--direct-policy intervals remain unresolved at
$10^{-4}$. Their point estimates are close, but the protected intervals do
not establish practical equivalence. This confirmation therefore supports
the stated comparison with the original HJB implementation and the positive
schedule-relative gains; the reusable-continuation experiment supplies the
separate comparison of the critic's incremental economic value. The full-class
regret bounds in Table~\ref{tab:r16-replication-methods} retain the anchor gap.

The new per-policy certificates attain the primary schedule-gain target
$0.0005$ for 14 of 16 NBO policies in dimension ten and all 16 in dimension
fifty. The corresponding counts are 13 and 16 for Raw, 10 and 16 for direct
policy, and zero in both dimensions for the original HJB implementation.
These are outcomes of a new final assessment. They leave the original online
stopping decisions unchanged. The pooled dimension-ten HJB lower endpoint
exceeds $0.0005$, although no individual HJB policy has a new protected lower
endpoint exceeding that target. Technical
Section~\ref{app:r16replicationrecord} gives the complete target account and
additional verification work.
