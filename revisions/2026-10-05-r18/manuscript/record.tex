\subsection{Confidence families and chronology}
The original-policy confirmation, stronger-HJB robustness, and signed
Bellman assessments each have allocation $.01$; the finite menu has $.02$,
split into $.018$ for payoff and $.002$ for scalar accuracy. The independent
contrast-risk family adds $.01$. Thus the entire five-family collection
has a union-bound failure probability at most $.06$, not $.05$.
The central menu payoff and risk statements together have failure
probability at most $.028$. The catalogue closure spends no additional
probability because it holds deterministically whenever all original menu
payoff intervals hold.

The original dimension-ten HJB refinement remains a post-freeze analysis
of old observations. The later original-policy confirmation fixed the
refined analysis before new observations. The contrast-risk assessment
also fixed its source and within-cell prediction record before its own
fresh audit innovations. The catalogue closure was developed after the
menu and risk outcomes were available. Its validity follows from the
already simultaneous family, not from describing this later selection as
prospectively registered. No reported training run, failure, contrary
comparison, or earlier stopping decision is replaced by the new analysis.
