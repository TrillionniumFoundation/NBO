                                           1. INTRODUCTION
25                                                                                              25

26     A continuation approximation matters through the economic decision it produces. 26
27   Prediction error, original-optimum policy loss and verification work are different         27

28   objects. This paper connects them through a constructive Neural Bellman Operator           28

29   (NBO), its attained own-future continuation and the complete feasible controller           29

30   implemented under the original law.                                                        30

1        The operator fits each date against its own attained future and retains feasible       1

2    action witnesses. Its affine–ReLU construction and primitive error budget do not           2

3    require unrestricted nonconvex fitting to succeed. A complementary recursive               3

4    implementation uses verified curvature and protected conventional refinement;              4

5    trained proposals change optional query locations. Residual qualification permits an       5

6    attained shallow continuation to replace recursive value queries under a stated error      6

7    account. Affine calibration separates its represented derivative from a conservative       7

8    coefficientwise penalty.                                                                   8

9        Our next question is how much optimization accuracy an economic decision               9

10   needs. Rational switch barriers identify every acquired action possible throughout         10

11   a qualification sublevel. Original-law conditional contrasts price those actions,          11

12   instead of charging the full cost range on each unresolved cell. Exact thresholds          12

13   determine the entire region of tolerances supporting an ordering, cost-equivalence         13

14   or replacement conclusion. A verified primal–dual gap places a returned readout in         14

15   that region without a fitting-success model or additional sample. Labels and the           15

16   continuation policy remain fixed in that first-date comparison.                            16

17       The prospective investment experiment compares both bases, both qualification          17

18   rules and both observation resolutions. Calibrated qualification improves both             18

19   ReLU and Bernstein controllers and reverses their earlier matched cost ordering. 19
20   Independent arithmetic identifies original-law gains; actionwise transfer preserves        20

21   some comparisons at nonzero optimization errors without identical actions. The             21

22   full catalogue retains adverse and unresolved cases. Conditional economic choices          22

23   require separate charges and deployment counts, not an invented conversion of              23

24   runtime or simulation rows into welfare.                                                   24

25       We now pass from a first-date comparison to complete policies. Changing an             25

26   earlier action changes the distribution of later acquired states. The recursive transfer   26

27   therefore couples actual occupation flows with exclusions imposed by a common              27

28   parameter vector. A separate joint-domain construction produces one state-context          28

29   neural circuit, and proves its approximation and feasible-policy guarantees through- 29
30   out a compact economic family rather than only on its training catalogue. These            30

31   results extend the original NBO construction; they do not replace it by a finite-menu      31

32   benchmark.                                                                                 32

9      Control variates, telescoping evaluation and time-uniform concentration are            9

10   established tools (Munos, 2006, Jiang and Li, 2016, Howard et al., 2020, Karam- 10
11   patziakis et al., 2021), as are minimax-regret decisions and shortest-path difference    11

12   constraints (Manski, 2004, Floyd, 1962). The new link is from exact qualification bar- 12
13   riers and original-law action effects to economic stopping regions for a constructed     13

14   continuation, not priority for these underlying principles.                              14

15     The same readout coefficients determine actions in every observation cell. Sepa- 15
16   rate switch tests can therefore admit an alternative that never wins its full menu, or   16

17   combinations of alternatives that no common readout can select. Section 10 retains       17

18   both restrictions. An exact support dual certifies each winner event and selected        18

19   joint events; a second, cell-assignment dual transfers the resulting compatibility       19

20   information to original-law cost. This strengthens the existing NBO optimization- 20
21   to-policy account without changing a fitted label, future controller or economic         21

22   primitive.                                                                               22

23     The additional calculation is deterministic qualification of previously purchased      23

24   evidence. It reports the incremental work of full-menu and shared-readout checks         24

25   separately from construction, integration and policy-cost inference. The economic        25

26   enclosure remains valid when a numerical solver is inconclusive, because only            26

27   independently verified multipliers may exclude an event. Standard convex duality         27

28   supplies the lower-bound principle (Boyd and Vandenberghe, 2004); the contribu- 28
29   tion here is its connection to acquired NBO actions, common coefficients and the         29

30   paid attained-policy cost event.                                                         30

27                                                                                            27

18                                                                                           18

19     Neural Bellman Operators connect attained continuation construction to feasi- 19
20   ble decisions under the original law. Witnesses and residual qualification provide      20

21   the original-optimum error account; affine calibration identifies a representation- 21
22   dependent qualification charge. Actionwise cost transfer and exact tolerance fron- 22
23   tiers then turn verified optimization accuracy into a sufficient economic stopping      23

24   condition.                                                                              24

25     The investment comparison identifies gains from calibrated qualification in both      25

26   bases and retains the reversal of their earlier ranking. Some cost orderings and        26

27   equivalence conclusions hold throughout nonzero optimization sublevels without          27

28   identical actions or new samples. Unresolved coarse classes remain explicit. These      28

29   conclusions neither assume universal neural superiority nor establish unrestricted      29

30   fitting reliability.                                                                    30

31     Conditional purchases and deployment allocations require separately supplied          31

32   economic scales and charges. Original failed reuse, adverse query timings, vector       32

5                                                                                             5
     an arbitrary independent choice in every observation cell. Full-menu and shared-

     readout certificates respect this distinction and supply a constructive refinement
7                                                                                             7
     of the original actionwise stopping regions. The assignment relaxation need not
8                                                                                             8

9                                                                                             9
     identified as an enclosure rather than an observed worst-case loss. The verified
10                                                                                            10
     common-coefficient restriction strengthens the economic use of the original NBO
11                                                                                            11

27   interpretation. Their proofs identify every required regularity, qualification and       27

30   unmeasured costs by assumed savings.                                                     30
