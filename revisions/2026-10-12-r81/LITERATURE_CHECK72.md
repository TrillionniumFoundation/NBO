# Primary-source literature check

The new discussion of sequential value-function control variates cites:

Rémi Munos (2006), “Geometric Variance Reduction in Markov Chains:
Application to Value Function and Gradient Estimation,” Journal of Machine
Learning Research 7(14), 413–427.

Primary publication metadata and abstract checked at:
https://www.jmlr.org/beta/papers/v7/munos06a.html

The manuscript does not claim priority for telescoping identities or the
broad use of value-function control variates. Its additional conditions are
a verified original-law residual-oscillation range, terminal correction,
implemented-policy greedy-gap certificate, exact one/two-shock ReLU moments
and the explicit cost of whole-domain qualification.
