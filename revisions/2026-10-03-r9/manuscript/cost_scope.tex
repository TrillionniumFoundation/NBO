\paragraph{Construction of the shared initializer.}
The coarse complete-action cover starts from the saved actor-plus-search policy with seed 29. That initializer is not a costless oracle. In the one-policy column, the account adds its archived training, reference, and policy-evaluation costs whenever they are not already included in the candidate's own construction. For the seed-29 actor run, its own recorded construction includes the initializer and is not charged twice.

The nine-policy column concerns the complete nine-hybrid comparison. Its population includes the initializer, whose construction is therefore already counted once among the nine own-construction costs. Only the cover computation is additionally shared. The one-hundred-policy column is a hypothetical amortization under the same inclusion convention, not evidence from one hundred executed policies. Both the population convention and the initializer's source hash are recorded in the cost account. Historical construction times and newly measured verification times remain distinct; this accounting does not turn them into a same-machine timing experiment.
