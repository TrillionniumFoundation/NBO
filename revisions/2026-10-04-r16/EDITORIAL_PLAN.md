## Interpretation retained in every document

The continuation is shared only within a fixed transition law, future reference, terminal objective and exact finite-model coefficient set. Current preference, externality and cap tasks can vary while that continuation is unchanged. Cached innovations require reevaluation at a new postdecision argument. The fixed-dictionary projection theorem has predetermined arguments, features and positive weights, zero-mean finite-covariance noise, and a Euclidean orthogonal projection after square-root weighting. It applies equally to a Raw estimator using that same projection and is not automatically a theorem for nonlinear fitting.

Menu payoffs concern the uniform finite task-state and stream populations. Positive and equivalent intervals can overlap; materially superior means the lower endpoint reaches the predetermined .0001 margin. The six SAA equivalence/work results are tied to the final 64-path budget, with all cheaper earlier budgets retained. They do not establish minimum work to a target over all budgets.

The new scalar account certifies the whole continuous common-action interval for 80 of 128 fixed candidates. Its remaining upper bounds do not certify the target and do not show that true regret exceeds it. The vector optimum, adapted diffusion optimum and scalar finite-decision target remain distinct. The original full-adapted-class regret bounds are displayed beside schedule gains.

The HJB study assesses an exact-trace implementation with all four configurations and two deployments. Independent collocation residuals and iterate changes are diagnostics; terminal equality follows from the lift. The registered family contains no direct greedy-minus-distilled payoff event. Neither that study nor the original-policy replication ranks the entire HJB class. The continuous two-year long design is distinct from the finite menu’s four-year Long design; joint stress changes several primitives and does not isolate a single causal effect.
