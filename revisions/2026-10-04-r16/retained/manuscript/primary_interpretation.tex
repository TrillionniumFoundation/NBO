\paragraph*{Economic performance of the declared methods.}
Continuation evaluation and feasible improvement produce positive
continuous-economy gains in both dimensions. NBO's method means are
approximately $0.001268$ and $0.001342$ in dimensions ten and fifty; their
simultaneous lower endpoints exceed $0.000943$ and $0.000957$, respectively.
These statements average over all sixteen declared complete algorithm
streams. All 128 executions returned fitted policies; none used the
analytical fallback.

The direct comparison identifies a material advantage over the specified
neural HJB procedure in dimension fifty. With the original transfer account,
the NBO-minus-HJB interval is contained in $[0.001051,0.001945]$, whose lower
endpoint exceeds the prespecified $10^{-4}$ economic margin. The original
ten-dimensional interval, contained in $[-0.000073,0.000663]$, is inconclusive.
All four direct comparisons with cached Raw and direct policy optimization
are also inconclusive under that account. Thus the experiment establishes
a domain in which the NBO evaluation--actor construction improves economic
performance relative to direct residual fitting with the stated common
representation capacity. The finding concerns the declared architectures,
training schedules, stopping rule, and finite stream distribution; it is not
a ranking of every possible implementation of the three alternatives.

\paragraph*{Economic accuracy and complete work.}
In dimension fifty, all sixteen NBO, Raw, and DPO executions attained the
online gain target $0.0005$, while no HJB execution did. Their mean complete
work was approximately $357.80$, $346.22$, $596.94$, and $1690.10$ seconds,
respectively, including unsuccessful checks and final confirmation. NBO
therefore attained the same declared economic target with less measured
work than DPO on the recorded environment; Raw was cheaper than NBO.
HJB's unattained runs remain in the work distribution. The reported
900-second restricted-mean functional for an unattained target is a censoring
convention, not the actual work consumed or an assertion that the run stopped
at 900 seconds.

No ten-dimensional execution attained the online stopping target. Independent
final confirmation nevertheless places every returned NBO, Raw, and DPO
policy above $0.0005$ in that dimension, as it does in dimension fifty.
These final certificates do not alter the earlier stopping decisions.
No method has a positively certified stream attaining the secondary
$0.001$ threshold. The complete stream and checkpoint tables retain this
difference between measured stopping and final economic accuracy.

\paragraph*{Sources of the observed gain.}
The numerical decomposition records how the learned decisions change the
capital economy. For NBO, production contributes approximately $0.002303$
and $0.002297$ in dimensions ten and fifty, while the consumption contribution
is approximately $-0.001271$ and $-0.001343$. The terminal-dispersion
contribution is approximately $0.000236$ and $0.000387$. Their sums equal the
observed numerical gains. These quantities describe the production benefit
and the consumption cost of state-dependent decisions on the same paths;
the continuous-time confidence statement belongs to the total payoff,
not separately to these components.
