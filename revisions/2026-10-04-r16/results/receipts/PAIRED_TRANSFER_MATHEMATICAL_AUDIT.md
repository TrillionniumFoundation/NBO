# Independent mathematical audit of the R15 paired-policy transfer

Reviewed publication: `1cb3cc9efe135966ec228dfaf51c6841a6dfc97c`.
Review target: R15 referee B3/M3; the original theorem and deterministic
implementation are reviewed for prospective reuse. This is an internal
independent derivation audit, not an external journal referee endorsement.

## Conclusion

No algebraic or scope error was found in Proposition r15pairedtransfer or
its implemented coefficient account. The original bound is valid for two
adapted total-held controls in the specified common tube, driven by common
innovations, under the paper's unchanged arithmetic and ideal-normal
sampling contract. The proof does not require the actor to be Lipschitz.

The source files and both accepted deterministic upper bounds should be
frozen unchanged before a new independently seeded confirmation bank.
This audit does not itself supply a fresh economic replication or change
the historical status of the R15 dimension-10 result.

## Independent derivation checks

1. **Economic target and implemented actions.** The auxiliary Euler process
   and physical diffusion use the same actions actually generated by the
   original clipped, rounded controller. Thus the proof compares a fixed
   innovation-history policy with its implementation. It never recomputes
   that policy at an auxiliary state, which would introduce an omitted actor
   sensitivity term.

2. **Payoff identity.** Integration by parts with
   `omega(t)=exp(-rho*t)/rho+(1-1/rho)*exp(-rho*T)` gives the displayed
   production, log-consumption, consumption-level, adjustment-cost, and
   terminal-dispersion terms. Held consumption makes its within-cell
   integration exact. Common initial, exogenous drift, and Brownian terms
   cancel in the paired identity.

3. **Scalar production normalization.** For
   `psi(y)=kappa*sum_i tanh(B_i y)/d`, the dual normalized gradient is
   `kappa B^T u`, and the normalized Hessian bound is
   `kappa L2 ||B||_2^2`. There is no missing `sqrt(d)` factor in the scalar
   Hessian or the positive-Gram scalar-gradient bound.

4. **Vector four-point term.** The vector derivative difference does require
   `sqrt(d) max_i||B_i||_2`, which is retained in `H_x`. The split into a
   change of the state difference and a change of its base point yields
   exactly `beta*||e_a-e_b||_d + H_x*||Delta Z||_d*||e_b||_d`.

5. **Generator constants.** The first and second derivative changes of
   `L^v f` give the three stated terms in `L_v`. The scalar Hessian,
   scalar-gradient, and third-derivative changes give `L_m`. The term
   involving `diag(q)B` is multiplied by `kappa`, since the generator's
   one-half and the global `|tanh'''|<=2` cancel. The alternative minimum
   with `||B||_2 ||q||_d` is valid.

6. **Paired martingale normalization.** The normalized Hilbert-Schmidt
   squared norm is `kappa^2/d sum_i q_i (Delta tanh'_i)^2`; its bound is
   `H_Sigma^2||Delta Y||_d^2` with the displayed weighted matrix norm.
   Common shocks are included in `q_i`.

7. **Accumulated local error.** Stochastic Fubini produces one predictable
   integral with kernel `t_(j+1)-s`. Ito isometry is used on this integral
   before discrete Gronwall. No future random Jacobian is incorrectly
   treated as independent of a past martingale increment.

8. **Cell kernel inequalities.** A decreasing nonnegative kernel times
   increasing `D(s)` or `D(s)^2` is bounded by the product of its cell
   averages. These give the factors `h/2`, `h^2/3`, and the exact `J_k`
   used in the theorem. All functions involved have the required signs.

9. **Positive recursion.** The forcing retains all error accumulated up to
   the current date. Its cross term includes every earlier `D_j eta_j`,
   and the discrete Gronwall term includes every earlier `xi_j`. The
   implementation's cumulative variables have the correct index ranges.

10. **Terminal dispersion and mixture law.** The displayed quadratic
    identity is exact. Cauchy--Schwarz is applied conditional on each
    initial profile. Averaging the resulting affine bound legitimately
    uses mean spread, rather than incorrectly replacing a mixture second
    moment by the square of a mean.

11. **Arithmetic and fallbacks.** The original path and clipping errors,
    complete per-policy statistic allowances, and the subtraction cushion
    are retained. Two exact-schedule fallbacks remain zero. A mixed
    fallback retains the original full schedule-relative account.

12. **Positive series and spectral proof.** The three series coefficients
    and next-term ratio bounds are correct. Interval LDL proves each
    accepted spectral majorant, rather than trusting an SVD estimate.
    Nonnegative enlargement of diagonal weights preserves the required
    operator-norm upper bounds. Midpoint plus Frobenius radius correctly
    encloses the interval matrix.

## Independent numerical evaluation

`independent_paired_audit.py` implements the mathematical coefficient
formulas afresh at 90 decimal digits. It uses exact exponential formulas
for `D`, its two integrals, and the payoff weights, instead of the
production source's positive-series and quadrature enclosures. It also
uses a separately written cumulative recurrence. It reads no learned
weight, confirmation observation, training result, or work record.

All **42 field comparisons** (21 per dimension) are enclosed by the stored
outward quantities. The slightly smaller independent total is expected:
exact integral weights remove the conservatism of the inherited interval
quadrature for `J_k`.
