\subsection{Fresh confirmation of the paired comparison}
\label{sec:r16replicationreading}
The paired-transfer refinement in the preceding table was developed after the original numerical source was fixed. It is a deterministic refinement on the original event, but that chronology alone does not make it a prospectively executed calculation. We therefore preserve the original and refined old-bank intervals and evaluate every one of the 128 original selected policies on an independent bank after fixing the paired theorem, its constants, the interval code, and the new confidence allocation.

Each of the sixteen original streams receives 8,192 fresh paths on 2,048 time cells. There is no retraining, checkpoint replacement, or alteration of historical online stopping. The new $0.01$ familywise allocation covers its 238 declared events; it is not borrowed from the earlier confidence family. Table~\ref{tab:r16-replication-comparisons} gives all direct method comparisons.

With the prospectively fixed paired transfer, the fresh NBO-minus-HJB intervals are contained in $[0.000176,0.000415]$ and $[0.001342,0.001653]$. Both exceed the declared $10^{-4}$ economic margin at their lower endpoint. The comparison remains with the original specified HJB implementation. Its purpose is independent confirmation of the fixed policies and the paired calculation, not a claim about a newly trained HJB family or a new economic calibration.

All four fresh NBO-minus-Raw and NBO-minus-direct-policy intervals include zero and extend beyond the equivalence band. Thus neither equivalence nor noninferiority follows. The fresh broad NBO regret upper bounds are $0.025971$ and $0.036845$, rounded upward; they are explicit absolute guarantees rather than $10^{-4}$ near-optimality certificates. The full method table records every method, including the negative fifty-dimensional HJB sample mean.
