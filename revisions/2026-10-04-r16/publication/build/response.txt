              2. M1 AND B1: THE LEARNED CRITIC’S INCREMENTAL ECONOMIC VALUE                  10

11       Referee concern. Positive verified payoff for the NBO package does not establish    11

12   that fitting the critic improves on cached Raw continuation targets. The reported       12

13   Raw procedure has similar payoff, lower work, and unresolved direct intervals.          13

14                                                                                           14

15       Revision. Section 6 states the precise common-continuation object and the valid     15

16   forms of reuse. A payoff or derivative observation is reusable at its argument          16

17   when future primitives and the reference policy remain fixed. Innovations can           17

18   also be reused at another argument, but the state recursion and payoff then have        18

19   to be reevaluated. This prevents a comparison that artificially charges Raw for         19

20   unnecessary resimulation while treating NBO reuse as free.                              20

21       Proposition 6 gives an exact bias–variance identity for a scalar-gradient dictio- 21
22   nary fixed independently of the fitting labels. Proposition 7 then gives its economic   22

23   counterpart. In the stated quadratic Bellman tasks, the expected weighted payoff        23

24   advantage equals one half of removed economically weighted noise variance minus         24

25   approximation cost. Correct representation and nonzero removable economic noise         25

26   give strict improvement. The statement also requires that the optimizers be feasible    26

27   interior solutions; it does not replace a constrained nonlinear actor by an uncon- 27
28   strained first-order formula. The proof is complete, and independent finite-support     28

29   manufactured tests check both favorable and adverse approximation regimes.              29

30       The completed nonlinear comparison is presented in Section 7.13, with its design    30

31   and every registered outcome in Appendix R. A vector costate fit, cached Raw            31

32   actor, task-conditioned direct actor, and Raw sample-average optimizer receive          32
