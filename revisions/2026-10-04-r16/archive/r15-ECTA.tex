\title{Neural Bellman Operators}
\runtitle{Neural Bellman Operators}
\begin{aug}
\author[id=au1,addressref={add1}]{\fnms{Qian}~\snm{QI}\ead[label=e1]{qiqian@pku.edu.cn}}
\address[id=add1]{Peking University}
\end{aug}
\begin{abstract}
We develop Neural Bellman Operators for continuous-time economic models with recursive utility, preference adjustment, and strategic interaction. A critic evaluates continuation values and a feasible actor uses their derivatives to improve decisions. For a nonlinear capital economy, an exact finite Bellman identity connects occupation-weighted continuation error, action quality, and continuous-time payoff bounds. Complete sixteen-stream comparisons in ten and fifty dimensions establish economically material gains over the declared neural HJB implementation. In fifty dimensions, NBO attains the prescribed economic target with less measured work than direct policy optimization. Cached raw-costate improvement remains cheaper, and direct NBO--Raw and NBO--DPO payoff differences remain unresolved. The candidate-occupation mechanism account is numerically instantiated but does not certify positive mechanism gains. Independent scalar approximation and finite-observation experiments provide separate accuracy and implementation assessments. The original applications retain their distinct utility domains and equilibrium conditions.
\end{abstract}
