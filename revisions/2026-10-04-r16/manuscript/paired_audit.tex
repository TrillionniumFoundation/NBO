\subsection{An independent audit of the paired transfer}
\label{app:r16pairedaudit}

Before the new confirmation bank was generated, we reviewed the proof and
implementation of Proposition~\ref{prop:r15pairedtransfer} independently
of the original reporting calculation. The audit follows the actual held
actions through the physical and auxiliary states, checks the scalar and
vector dimension normalizations separately, verifies the predictable
accumulated martingale account, and applies terminal Cauchy--Schwarz
conditional on each initial profile before averaging. It also checks
the mixed-fallback case and retention of the original numerical allowances.
No algebraic or scope discrepancy was found under the stated contract.

A second implementation evaluates 21 reported quantities in each dimension
at 90 decimal digits. It uses closed exponential expressions for the three
positive-series functions and exact integrated payoff weights, together
with a separately implemented cumulative error recursion. All 42 quantities
are enclosed by the original outward endpoints. The resulting diagnostic
totals are approximately $5.7524\times10^{-5}$ and
$7.8466\times10^{-5}$; their modest difference from the protected endpoints
reflects the original quadrature enclosures. High precision by itself is
not an interval proof. The confirmation therefore retains the original
rigorous upper bounds, $5.8033751287117856\times10^{-5}$ and
$7.918471815054792\times10^{-5}$, without reducing them on the basis of
this diagnostic. The six original deterministic checks, including fresh
interval LDL verification of the stored spectral proposals, also pass.

This is an internal independent derivation and implementation audit,
recorded with the source identities. It does not retrospectively change
the status of the R15 refinement. The new confirmation is separately
labelled and uses the transfer, constants, and reporting decisions fixed
before its independent noise bank. Since it retains the previously selected
policies, its target remains their declared finite stream distribution;
it does not create a new population of training configurations.
