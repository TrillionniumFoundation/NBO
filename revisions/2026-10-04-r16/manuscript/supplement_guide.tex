\section*{Guide to the Technical Supplement}
This supplement is a current part of \emph{Neural Bellman Operators}. It contains the general verification results, the complete capital-model proofs, the paired-transfer and occupation accounts, observation and implementation arguments, and the full computational evidence. The separate \emph{Economic Applications} companion contains the full original application models and their derivations and proof arguments. All three scientific documents have live cross-references.

The first part develops the mathematical results by subject. The computational part preserves the previous complete experiment and its post-freeze analytical refinement together, followed by the current scientific records. Evidence from different simulation banks and confidence families retains its original scope. In particular, the previous dimension-ten paired refinement is not described as a prospectively fixed experiment, and fresh observations on the same policies are not new training streams.

Supplementary equations and mathematical results use the prefix S. The applications companion uses A. Every one of the 285 labels in the reviewed R15 article and supplement is present in one of the three current scientific documents. The source and proof preservation map in Section~\ref{app:r15index} includes complete proof sections as well as explicit proof environments.
