\subsection{Signed continuation assessment}
\label{sec:r16signedsummary}
The occupation account can retain the sign of the continuation prediction error. At a sampled candidate state and date, evaluate the reference continuation from the candidate and reference postdecision states using the same future innovations. Add the current reward difference before forming the interval. The conditional expectation is the reference Bellman advantage; averaging over the candidate's occupation distribution gives the finite policy gain.

Proposition~\ref{prop:r16signedbridge} makes this statement precise. The predicted improvement and its signed continuation error cancel sample by sample. A lower endpoint for their combined statistic therefore need not pay separately for the full range of the fitted predictor. Proposition~\ref{prop:r16signedrange} supplies an economic range using the action tube, bounded production derivatives, and antithetic future paths. It retains the original clipping and arithmetic account and the separate transfer to the continuous-time payoff. The complete derivation is in technical Section~\ref{sec:r16signedbridge}.

This calculation addresses the precision of an economic identity for the selected policy. The comparison of a learned critic with a Raw continuation remains a distinct empirical question. The repeated-decision design makes that comparison directly, including the cost and benefit of reusing each continuation representation.
