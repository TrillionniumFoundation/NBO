\section{Independent Confirmation of the Original Policies}
\label{app:r16replicationrecord}

independent confirmation banks, each with 8,192 paths on 2,048 cells, and
retains all 238 declared events. The main article reports all eight pooled
method gains and six direct NBO contrasts. The complete per-stream endpoint
array, original-transfer counterparts, allowance reconstruction, and finite
target-attainment account are retained in the source-bound numerical report.
