\section{Original-model investment decisions and complete costs}
\label{sec:numerical122}
\subsection{Economic design and immutable information}
The experiment uses the original two-date, two-state investment technology of
Section~\ref{sec:continuous95}. Innovations retain their continuous uniform law
on $[-1/32,1/32]$, discounting is $15/16$, and nested AVaR is evaluated at
$\alpha=1/2$ and $3/4$. The action capacity is
$A(x)=1/8+(x_1+x_2)/16$ and the direct investment cost is
$h(a)=a^2+4a^4$. These original primitives, constraints and risk preferences are
common to every method. Each final policy must satisfy the same original regret
tolerance $\tau=1/256$ and objective-width tolerance $\omega=1/8192$.

The 56 stored actors cross two risk levels, two acquisition budgets
($5$ and $9$), seven representations, and value versus marginal fitting losses.
The representation menu is quadratic, ReLU, matched ordinary hinge, spline,
degree-two polynomial, degree-three polynomial and grid. The original-model
online solver has no fitted artifact and remains in the complete menu. The
actual acquired models and their acquisition, fitting and finite-box audits
retain their preceding immutable identity. There is no new fitting or tuning
on the 32 evaluation states. The current scientific source and reporting
specification were committed before those states were drawn; the states were
committed before any policy result. Under the explicit independent-uniform-bit assumption for the entropy source,
the sampling law is the finite 40-bit dyadic law, as stated
precisely in Supplement, Section~\ref{sec:inference122}. The economic innovations
inside each Bellman operator remain continuously distributed.

Each actor receives three complete services: its original qualified control,
a \emph{recycled} service using the completed support payload, and an
\emph{adaptive} service that can purchase the fixed 256- and 512-cell support
refinements at that same anchor. Each preserves the same future controller.
There are 58 actor--risk cells, 174 complete service cells and 5,568 primary
policy decisions. Three separate serial processes evaluate the fixed first
16 states in rotated cell order, giving 8,352 additional timed decisions.
They are descriptive repeat executions of existing states, not additional
population observations. The six candidate fractions, realized-action rounding,
refinement ladder and tie rule were fixed in advance. No candidate action
receives a new original-risk value query from the improvement mechanism.
Proposals use the inner 30-bit capacity obtained by rounding the outward lower
capacity bound downward; qualification still uses the full original capacity.
Derivative recovery uses each feasible side length together with
$2^{-j}$, $j=2,\ldots,40$, and intersects with $[-7,7]$. These finite radii
produce valid enclosures; they are not asserted to attain the exact-infimum
sharpness or precision rate.

\subsection{Independent economics and population inference}
The independent implementation reads the original primitive model and uses
rational terminal optimization and outward continuous-risk integration. It
evaluates every distinct final, incumbent and available raw actor action.
A fixed independent 64/128/256/512-cell ladder checks accepted claims; every
raw bound and terminal witness is retained. It distinguishes an interval merely
compatible with a production certificate from an interval contained within it,
and distinguishes a positive gain from confirmation of the entire claimed
lower bound. Unresolved checks remain unresolved.

The independently evaluated $q$ is the optimal-future original objective.
Its complete-policy interval adds the same separately proved allowance
$0\le J-q\le15/16777216$. This checks the original-policy account for the
specified future-controller class; it is not an independent replay of every
rounded last-date controller action. Policy regret and action error are
reported as justified enclosures or upper bounds, not exact errors inferred
from a residual.

The frozen inference family includes all 116 treatment-versus-own-control
cells, 232 positive-gain threshold events and 348 signed comparisons against
the online control and the matching quadratic value control. The proof in the
supplement combines exact binomial inversion with bounded-variable bounds for
joint coverage of at least $95\%$. Numerical enclosures and theorem bounds
remain distinguishable from population uncertainty. The inference is conditional
on the pre-sample frozen algorithm and inherited information, not conditional
on the realized evaluation sample. There is no outcome-selected subset.

\subsection{Complete service work and computational adoption}
Let $A$ denote the complete allocated historical acquisition and fitting cost,
$H$ the measured process work outside its nonoverlapping complete cohorts,
$T$ one complete cohort of $n$ decisions, and $L$ its measured actor loading cost.
The declared scenario for $N$ uses of a stored service is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fullcost122}
 C(N)=A+H+L+\frac{N}{n}(T-L),
 \qquad N\in\{32,256,4096,65536\}.
\end{equation}
The calculation includes original information acquisition, tuning and fitting
where present, finite-training audits, qualification, candidate arithmetic,
rejected candidates, failed paid refinements, actor inference and bookkeeping.
Independent original-model validation, population inference, reporting and
publication are separately charged in the research ledger; they are not added
twice to a measured decision-service cohort. Shared parent work is allocated
in full once to each hypothetical standalone
service; those alternative allocations are not added together as realized
research time. The primary parent clock excludes its three timing children
exactly once. Each timed comparison uses the economic outcomes of the same
first 16 states as its clock; the 32-state primary outcomes remain separate.

For a nonnegative price $p$ per unit of measured computation, the complete menu
compares $N J+pC(N)$, with $J$ the sample mean cost per decision. The tables give exact strict-price domains using the
independent economic endpoints and recorded clocks, then intersect all
traditional competitors and all three timing processes. An additional
sensitivity uses the genuinely completed clock from the interrupted earlier
attempt and can only reduce that domain. Large $N$ are explicit extrapolation
scenarios, not observed throughput or population adoption estimates. Training
and certificate reuse therefore enter transparently, without treating an
amortization scenario as a new measurement.

The initial execution was interrupted without a terminal parent receipt.
Its saved complete cohorts, a complete first timing process and a partial
second timing process remain preserved. Before the subsequent complete run,
a restart protocol fixed unchanged sources, states and order, required exact
decision-field agreement, and forbade new statistical observations. All 9,808
old rows agree on the 16 declared decision fields. The interrupted execution
has a measured completed-work lower bound but an unknown total duration;
consequently a complete exact research-time total is not asserted. Later
schema/reporting corrections preserve their failed attempts, immutable source
identities and narrow disclosed changes. None changes a policy outcome.
