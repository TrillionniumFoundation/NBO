\title{Neural Bellman Operators}
\runtitle{Neural Bellman Operators}
\begin{aug}
\author[id=au1,addressref={add1}]{\fnms{Qian}~\snm{QI}\ead[label=e92ECTA]{qiqian@pku.edu.cn}}

\begin{abstract}
Neural Bellman Operators construct continuation values and feasible actors for a
specified recursive economic objective. We develop an action-level use of
information already purchased by an original Bellman certificate. A quadratic
minorant and a value enclosure imply a sharp derivative interval on a constrained
action domain. A robust feasible replacement then certifies original-policy
improvement, regret and objective width without a new value query at the
candidate action. The result distinguishes support accuracy, marginal accuracy,
implemented-future error and computational cost. We retain complete own-future
construction and recursive-risk foundations, with qualified links to stochastic
control and nonlinear recursive utility. The numerical application uses the
original two-date investment economy with continuous innovations and nested
average value at risk. Fifty-six previously acquired actors and an online solver use common original
objectives, feasible controls, qualification tolerances and improvement rules;
matched-budget representations share the acquired labels. A separately
frozen experiment compares neural, matched hinge, quadratic, polynomial,
spline and grid representations on 32 new states with three process clocks.
Independent rational evaluation, simultaneous population inference and complete
cost--value menus distinguish safe policy changes from neural-specific adoption.
