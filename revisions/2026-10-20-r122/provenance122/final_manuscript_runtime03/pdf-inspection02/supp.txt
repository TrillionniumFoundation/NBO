TECHNICAL SUPPLEMENT TO NEURAL BELLMAN OPERATORS
QIAN QI
Peking University
This supplement supplies complete proofs for the original Neural Bell-
man Operator, its feasible own-future construction, recursive risk and
action accounts, stochastic-control foundations and Brownian recursive-
utility bridge. The retained action, continuous-risk information and policy-
improvement statements are included with their full proofs. The R121
addition derives the original integrated-risk marginal, constrained tail
movement, outward finite-risk derivative labels, exact finite-box training
bounds, feature-aware action contrasts and recursive anchor propagation.
It states the precise information interface, numerical qualification and pop-
ulation assumptions. Earlier proofs and economic models retain their own
scope; a result for one objective is not transferred to another by notation.
The current addition proves the sharp paid-support derivative interval,
safe original-policy replacement, precision and stopping claims, a match-
ing fixed-information minimax bound, original-policy gain propagation
through the lower centered risk recursion, and finite-state simultaneous
inference. The two analytic extensions after the scientific freeze do not alter
the frozen experiment. Statements introduced here are numbered S.*.

torical experiments and additional retained models appear in the development

12.4. What interval compatibility does and does not establish
Let I be an independently justified interval for an original policy value and P0 a
recorded service or fine-reference interval. If they are disjoint, both cannot enclose
the same value: at least one enclosure argument, arithmetic step, input identity, or
model premise requires investigation. The case must be retained. If they intersect,

Meaning and limits of the statement.
The family covers fixed original-policy costs,
with numerical endpoint uncertainty retained. It neither proves a stronger claim
than the uniform qualification premises nor promotes empirical compatibility to
independent confirmation. The inherited actors are conditioned on; no result here
concerns their retraining on an unseen sample or their optimization error under a
different Bellman objective.
