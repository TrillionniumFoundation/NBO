\begin{abstract}This supplement supplies complete proofs for the original Neural Bellman Operator, its feasible own-future construction, recursive risk and action accounts, stochastic-control foundations and Brownian recursive-utility bridge. The retained action, continuous-risk information and policy-improvement statements are included with their full proofs. The R121 addition derives the original integrated-risk marginal, constrained tail movement, outward finite-risk derivative labels, exact finite-box training bounds, feature-aware action contrasts and recursive anchor propagation. It states the precise information interface, numerical qualification and population assumptions. Earlier proofs and economic models retain their own scope; a result for one objective is not transferred to another by notation. The current addition proves the sharp paid-support derivative interval, safe original-policy replacement, precision and stopping claims, and finite-state simultaneous inference. Statements introduced here are numbered S.*. \end{abstract}
