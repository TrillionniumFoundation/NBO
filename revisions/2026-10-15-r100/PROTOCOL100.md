This is a new author-revision experiment, not an R98 completion and not a reuse of the R96 population stream or R97 refitted actors. This protocol is committed before any R100 fitting or evaluation. It fixes a deterministic numerical experiment, not a random-sampling population study; finite-design means are not confidence intervals. No deployment-price calibration or neural superiority is assumed.

## Scientific questions

Can a verified marginal-action interval admit a policy without paying for an unnecessarily accurate absolute continuation level? Does gradient-informed acquisition improve original-objective regret, proposal acceptance, or complete work relative to value fitting when both receive identical information? What remains after including a semianalytic original-model solver, quadratic, coordinate spline, oblique hinge, and grid dynamic-programming comparators? The oblique ReLU and conventional hinge are mathematical twins; any equality is reported, not rebranded as neural specificity.

## Fixed economy and information

Use the exact original two-state, two-decision-date investment transition, state-dependent scalar investment bound, costs, beta=15/16, continuous uniform innovation on [-1/32,1/32], and separately alpha=1/2 and 3/4. The objective remains nested upper-tail AVaR, not expected cost or static AVaR. The implemented terminal controller uses the inherited uniform allowance 2^-20; its state observation and feasible action/error proof must be supplied. Continuation targets are V1 and its state gradient acquired from the same original primitives. Semianalytic access is available to every arm and is charged when used.

Training states: the complete 9 by 9 grid (i/8,j/8), i,j=0,...,8. Validation states: the complete 4 by 4 grid ((4i+3)/16,(4j+3)/16), i,j=0,...,3. Confirmation states: the complete 4 by 4 grid ((4i+1)/16,(4j+1)/16), i,j=0,...,3. These three finite designs are disjoint. No state may be deleted because an arm fails or loses. Values and both gradient coordinates are supplied to every fitted arm; complete label acquisition is charged to a standalone purchaser, with the shared-cost alternative shown separately.

## Fixed comparators and acquisition

Compare convex quadratic, coordinate hinge spline, and oblique ReLU continuations, each with value-only and gradient-informed readouts. Add a conventional oblique-hinge twin for each ReLU readout, a grid/triangulated-interpolation dynamic-programming continuation, and an online semianalytic original-model proposal. Affine coefficients are unrestricted; convex nonlinear readouts are nonnegative. Coordinate knots are 1/4,1/2,3/4. Oblique directions are (1,0),(0,1),(1,1),(1,-1), normalized consistently, with knots fixed at the same fractions of their range on [0,1]^2. Quadratic atoms are x1^2,x2^2,(x1+x2)^2,(x1-x2)^2. This is a disclosed unequal-dictionary strong-frontier comparison, not an equal-rank neural separation. ReLU/hinge twins have identical information, features, parameters, and algorithms.

Value readouts minimize squared value error. Gradient-informed readouts add (1/64) times the sum of squared errors in both gradient coordinates. No hyperparameter search is allowed. Use deterministic convex least squares with reported tolerances and residuals; record all solver exits and nonzero coefficients. No independence of a second implementation is claimed merely from rerunning the same fitting routine.

## Original-objective certification and stopping

All arms face the same first-date original-optimum target: tau in {2^-8,2^-10,2^-12}, with beta*2^-20 reserved for the terminal allowance. Initial proposals are feasible and are retained even when rejected. A representation-neutral original-law derivative enclosure is used for admission; compare its conclusions with a value-support enclosure where constructed. Risk-tail localization and derivative integration must be outward, with endpoint, quadrature and inner-optimizer errors included. Refinement doubles the interval partition, starting at four cells, up to 4096. Scalar action refinement has at most 64 steps. Any limit produces a recorded unqualified outcome, not a silent success. Code corrections may repair violations of the stated mathematics; every failed run and reason remains in the evidence ledger. They cannot change states, comparators, tolerances, loss weights, or the economic model.

Select a service on validation by minimum total counted original-model oracle work, ties by declared lexicographic name, separately at each risk and tolerance. Confirmation is reported for every arm, not just the selected one. No finite-grid validation theorem is substituted for an independent-population theorem.

## Measurements and verification

Record training and fitting work; all candidate proposals; first-proposal rejection; final actions; original recursive objective intervals; regret upper certificates; action-distance bounds; tail and integration levels; fallback; point and interval primitive calls; elapsed and process CPU times; environment and memory scope. Operation counts are primary. Full 16-state service timing is descriptive. Up to five fresh-process repetitions of the primary-tolerance workload are planned; unfinished repetitions must be labeled unfinished, with completed runs preserved. No timing range is a confidence interval.

Independently check the terminal AVaR identity and action derivatives using exact rational primitives and finite differences or separately written polynomial formulas. Recompute decision bounds independently and mutation-test certificate rejection. Exact mechanism/information examples require a separate rational implementation. Retain all failed validations. Produce source hashes, logs, raw rows, summaries, English theorem/proof text, a point-by-point response and a standalone rebuilding package. Unexecuted horizon/control/innovation-dimensional expansion and externally calibrated adoption remain explicit outstanding claims; this finite original-model study cannot stand in for them.
