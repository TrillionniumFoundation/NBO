\section{Overview and review target}
This response addresses all 18 numbered findings and 35 technical comments in the second-pass R47 report at commit \path{a535a466ec4975dc14e2d46f73a690c72e11671e}, which reviews manuscript commit \path{1987a1f91ff783cd6f24fa7a9ea6a8118f64c56c}. We have also consulted the first-pass report and preserved the complete earlier article and supplement. The new branch is based on the second-pass review commit, so the review itself remains part of the auditable ancestry.

The revision replaces the lower-plug-in workflow by a raw-input, witness-only pipeline; characterizes the exact occupation relaxation represented by prices; proves a shared-budget completion; implements target-dependent arithmetic; and proves a paired Borel-state restriction--extension theorem for finite observed initial laws under controlled finite-atomic transitions. The source-frozen evaluation has twelve new primary environments and two separate observed-fleet contracts. Each of two independently checked formulations meets four primary targets; both fleet targets are met. The eight open primary rows and the thirty old uniform-initial-law intervals are not described as solved.

The responses below separate completed changes from numerical or modeling requirements that the current evidence does not establish. In particular, no external SCIP lower proof, expensive learned operating oracle, empirical calibration, or new strong-branching ablation is claimed. These are not replaced by counts of proof objects.
