## M1 / B1. Identify the contribution without changing the paper's subject

We retain the original NBO construction and do not infer a neural advantage from a method-neutral certificate. The revised introduction distinguishes three uses of a neural object: a constructed continuation, a proposed action query and a proposed record for retrieval. In each case the numerical object and its incremental work have different roles.

The new section **Frozen neural continuations and policy-cost precision** goes beyond letting a predictor choose an interior query. A fitted continuation enters the actual cost observation through its conditional expectation. The theorem states the terminal correction, residual-oscillation range, implemented greedy-gap requirement and original-optimum policy bound. The exact ReLU moment proposition makes this calculation executable in the original economic law without substituting an optimal-value oracle. Its uniform qualification account explicitly charges state and action covers and does not assume a small achieved error merely because a network has been fitted.

The second new section, **Transporting purchased Bellman witnesses**, is deliberately available to records generated by either the neural backend or the conventional verifier. Its contribution is a valid state transfer of both a lower original-value certificate and a capacity-feasible upper witness. Neither nearest-neighbor retrieval nor a fitted endpoint is accepted as a certificate by itself. This extends the original operator's implementation and resource account, rather than renaming the project or asserting that a shared correctness argument identifies neural superiority.

## M4 / B3. Reuse, the first fill and stable workload coverage

R69 extends the earlier twenty-four-block study to sixty-four predetermined blocks, includes fitting, serialization, loading, identity checks, stale-model rejection, fallback and shutdown, and fixes a stable crossing criterion before execution. Those results remain in the Numerical and Development Appendix: small-task crossings occur for several representations, while the eight-state task does not show a learned reuse winner.

The new catalogue asks a different question: can an already paid original-Bellman certificate avoid a later descendant tree at a nearby state? It fixes five scalar and vector tasks, three reuse rules, the complete repetition schedule, a 128-record capacity, nearby-state displacements, an invalidation point and a distant query before execution. The rules are no cache, exact-key cache and geometric witness transport. The last rule repairs capacity and checks exact rational bounds before every hit. Every miss invokes the unchanged original-law recursive verifier. Every block is serialized and its cache reloaded and identity checked.

The finite-cover proposition is not inferred from those localized states. It requires a tighter fresh-fill tolerance, positive reuse radius, retention of all records and checking all candidates. It exposes dimension dependence and the cost of rejected retrievals. The executed cache instead uses a bounded store and the requested fill tolerance, so its measured counts are reported as observations. Invalidation destroys paid coverage and requires a new fill. The study does not assume a persistent crossing for unrelated future states or claim that its root cache accelerates the descendants of a miss.

## M5 / B4. Inference at a declared margin and the price of qualification

R70 already closes the precision-design deficiency at the stated normalized-loss margin. All twelve centered intervals meet the predeclared equivalence margin $1/256$ and the declared numerical and sampling allocations. Every interval still contains zero. We retain both the positive equivalence conclusion and the absence of a strict ranking, together with the earlier wide cash-cost intervals.

The referee's concern about complete cost remains important: optimal-advantage evaluation itself purchases expensive Bellman descendants. R72's frozen-continuation theorem provides an alternative whose range depends on verified residual oscillation and greedy gaps. It retains the terminal correction when $h_T\ne g$ and the common initial-law requirement. Exact conditional moments evaluate a shallow ReLU continuation without an optimal-value tree. A finite state-and-action-cover argument provides a nonrecursive route to uniform residual qualification, without applying true-value convexity to an arbitrary fitted critic.

This is a proved alternative, not an assertion that a new trained critic has already met the $1/256$ margin more cheaply. The paper specifies what must be fitted, integrated and verified before that empirical claim can be made. The R69 inference observations and clocks remain unchanged, and the R72 query records add no independent expected-cost sample size.

## M7 / B7. Independent numerical implementation and its exact boundary

The retained R70 audit independently reconstructs full sampled state and cash-cost bins and final inference arithmetic. It preserves the earlier cumulative-buffer aliasing counterexample, corrected catalogue and numerical portability diagnostic. Those checks are not replaced by the new source hashes.

The new independent checker imports no production cache, interval-query or ReLU moment routine. It reconstructs every new accepted transfer from its paid source record using exact rational state and action distances and independently coded primitive moduli. It checks feasibility, lattice alignment, context, endpoint direction and tolerance. Every conditional neuron expectation is recomputed by clipping a rectangle and integrating its affine function through polygon area and first moments, rather than using the production positive-part divided-power expression.

The checker independently verifies the new operations. It does not independently regenerate the internal descendants of every inherited optimal-value fill. That premise remains visible in both the manuscript and audit. Thus a source-bound return and an independently recomputed transport are not misdescribed as an independent reimplementation of the historical 8.1-million-query tree. The mathematical record includes the primitive proof supporting the moduli and the inherited query contract supporting each paid source witness.

The unfavorable all-in qualification cases are preserved. Normalized loss, processor seconds and monetary prices are not silently equated.

The new fitted-continuation theorem and paid-witness transport reduce two well-defined potential costs within that same decision problem. These remain empirical economic claims distinct from the completed mathematical and computational contributions. Keeping those distinctions is necessary to make the original NBO objective testable, rather than changing the subject or manufacturing a favorable economic conclusion.
