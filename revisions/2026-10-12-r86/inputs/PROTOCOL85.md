## Prospective original-law validation (fixed before execution)

The actors are the nine already frozen two-date investment controllers in the R80 factorial study: zero, and legacy/calibrated ReLU/Bernstein at acquisition sizes 8 and 16. All coefficients, complete acquired action tables, laws, tie conventions, evaluation kernels and economic primitives are inherited unchanged. No refit, screening-out of actors, sample-dependent precision, or post-result extension is permitted.

Use disjoint new calibration and evaluation streams of 8192 and 131072 original-law dyadic bins, respectively; 40 bits per coordinate, four independent coordinates per bin, seeds 2026101208501 and 2026101208502 with NumPy PCG64. Existing actors are frozen before these streams are generated. Generate no pilot outcomes. The ideal statistical model is independent uniform bin indices and conditionally uniform within-bin coordinates; PCG64 is the disclosed reproducible numerical implementation.

Use both inherited independent full-bin propagation implementations and the exact rational midpoint fixtures. Infer all 36 unordered paired original-law cost differences jointly on each stream, with the inherited empirical-Bernstein construction and log upper bound 11 (144*exp(-11) < .005 per stream). Retain unconditional and analytically conditioned costs, and every unresolved sign. Calibration selects the controller with the smallest empirical conditioned upper endpoint, with lexical ties. Test the frozen selector against every comparator on the distinct evaluation stream. All pairwise bounds remain valid after this selection because the evaluation event is simultaneous. The margin is 1/256 in original normalized cost units, interpreted only as a declared purchaser tolerance, not a monetary estimate. Report sampling and enclosure components separately. Count paired rows once per stream, not once per actor or comparison.

Record complete fresh-process time, CPU, peak memory, exact actor/stream identities, and both kernel checks. Do not interpret inherited hardware timings as a same-source cross-machine validation. Include all fitting warnings from inherited records as historical diagnostics and do not count zero new fits as evidence of training reliability.
