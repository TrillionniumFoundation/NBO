\section*{Response to the 4 October 2026 Report on R12}
We thank the referee for identifying both the incomplete submitted object and the substantive questions that a completed comparison must answer. This revision continues the original Neural Bellman Operators paper. It preserves the reviewed development snapshot and every earlier application, and adds an executable fixed-work study, independent mechanism diagnostics and a finite-observation transfer account. The numerical summaries in this response are generated from the accompanying evidence rather than entered as anticipated results.

\subsection*{B1--B3 and M1--M3: A complete paper and the actual direct contrasts}
The authoritative revision consists of integrated root manuscripts, the complete response, generated tables, fitted weights, raw path arrays, source and table manifests, test records, execution logs and compiled documents. Publication to the new evidence and referee branches is conditional on the numerical, identity, preservation and compilation checks. The development branch and its incomplete or failed executions remain separate. A development smoke is never included among the final ten streams.

The announced-time experiment carries the complete inherited design through dimensions 10, 20 and 50, with ten fixed streams and all three methods. Both origin and finite-population evaluations use the declared final path count and time grid. Every NBO-minus-direct-policy and NBO-minus-affine comparison is formed directly on common paths; the shared reference trajectory and its transfer contribution cancel. The supplement prints each primary NBO-minus-direct-policy mean and two-sided endpoint before any conclusion about the aggregate pattern. Positive, negative and inconclusive results remain distinct. Descriptive variation across ten fixed streams is not promoted to a sampling theorem over unspecified future training procedures.

The inherited README failure is resolved by evaluating the original assertions on the exact archived historical root view, including the original README, then restoring all bytes. An independent full-tree audit checks preservation relative to the latest review commit. No historical assertion is weakened to make the current root pass an obsolete layout test. Typography is checked on the actual generated paper, not a source-only surrogate; unresolved references, duplicate labels and overfull boxes still fail the build.

\subsection*{B4 and M4: Fixed work and total work at a common endpoint}
The announced-time design is retained as one comparison rather than treated as the complete numerical benchmark. An additional ten-stream study trains each method for a fixed 160 iterations and evaluates validation-selected prefixes at 80 and 160 iterations. Rollout states, time cells, validation frequency and validation path counts match across methods. Critic and actor updates, differentiated work, measured time and memory do not necessarily match and are exposed separately.

We report direct paired method endpoints for these fixed-work objects and the tested work needed to reach prespecified improvement and absolute-regret endpoints. Work to a target includes the consumed fitting prefix and every certificate inspected up to the first attainment, including earlier unsuccessful checks. Unattained targets are censored at the maximum tested budget, not omitted or assigned zero cost. The results are finite-grid work-to-target comparisons, not an asserted asymptotic complexity advantage. A second operating-system environment repeats the fixed-work comparison for seed 7919 in dimensions 10 and 50; selected checkpoints, actor tensors and endpoints are compared rather than assuming timing invariance.

\subsection*{B5 and M5: Testing the critic mechanism rather than assuming it}
The strong-concavity and conditional-projection statements remain mechanism results with explicit hypotheses. We add independent held-out frozen-policy rollout banks. Cross-bank products estimate regression error relative to the conditional rollout costate without simply counting reference-sample noise as critic error. The estimate is allowed to be negative. Shared fine increments produce nested time-grid costates and paired discretization-sensitivity diagnostics. The record separately contains target variance, noisy-reference error, cross-bank error, actor and critic-greedy Hamiltonian loss, the deterministic action-loss inequality, paired payoff and work for the same weight file.

The diagnostics cover value-only, costate-only, raw-costate, update-count and width variants as well as the fixed-work fits. A direct-policy method's unused initialized critic is explicitly not called a trained competing estimator. The reference costates are still finite-rollout Monte Carlo quantities, not the true continuous-time costate. Accordingly, the independent economic payoff endpoint remains the performance result; target MSE or a descriptive correlation cannot replace it. This makes the proposed mechanism falsifiable in the saved computation without claiming an optimizer theorem that has not been proved.

\subsection*{B6 and M6--M7: A separate finite-measurement implementation}
The exact continuous-observation proposition remains a mathematical information benchmark and is no longer the only implementation discussed. A new controller uses only the current finite-date measurement, including a causal sensor-error term. Its physical capital state follows the original diffusion; no observed drift integral or latent Brownian vector is required. The protected implementation uses the saved weights and an explicitly controlled activation kernel. Global weight-based Lipschitz bounds, activation and dot-product errors, recurrence roundoff, clipped-innovation tails and measurement noise enter a proved payoff-transfer recursion.

Every sensor descendant has a separately evaluated protected innovation parent at the same time grid. We do not reuse a certificate for different arithmetic or a different cell count. Shared-noise fine-Euler differences are reported as diagnostics alongside, not instead of, the deterministic transfer allowance. A large allowance remains in the table; an observed small payoff difference does not justify deleting an error term. The admissible filtration and the role of independent randomization are stated in the main text. The sensing result is a finite-grid account with stated conditions, not a claim that arbitrary noisy observation preserves all earlier guarantees.

\subsection*{B7 and M8: Absolute accuracy and the independent scalar reference}
The scalar HJB solve remains independent of the neural critic. In addition to the nested grids and wider domain, the revised audit reports policy losses at five initial states, maximum losses over the central interval, adjacent-grid value and action changes, fixed-policy residuals and action-bound counts. The largest-grid policy-loss diagnostic is not extrapolated to a 50-dimensional problem.

The high-dimensional record continues to report the actual $G_d-L_a$ upper endpoint and now evaluates total tested work to prespecified values of that endpoint. Improvement over the schedule, direct method superiority and proximity to the unknown optimum are separate estimands. None is inferred merely because another one has a favorable sign. This retains the original numerical ambition while specifying exactly which part the present computation establishes.
