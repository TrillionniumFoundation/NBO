\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:r22conclusion}
The new evidence separates what the parameterization contributes from what the optimizer and certifier contribute. Under matched initialization and Adam gradient budgets, neural parameterization produces a uniformly better regional policy than direct coefficients. The pair-moment argument proves this ordering and initial-to-final improvement for independently initialized experts. Direct L-BFGS-B nevertheless remains the stronger overall regional configuration. These findings replace an unidentified representation claim with an identified conditional comparison without suppressing the adverse benchmark.

The acceptance gate now has executed rejection, complete restoration, a diagnosed financing-root failure, and a documented supervisory recovery. The global current-state actor and witness also have a new, independently checked complete-domain refinement. None of these facts turns the regional stochastic mixture into a globally accurate learned state map. The full-domain $.01$ objective, continuation-witness problem, and matched global classical frontier remain the paper's original targets. The correction-cone formulation and cover accounting specify mathematical requirements for advancing them; they do not assert that an unexecuted high-dimensional or incomplete-market result has already been obtained.
