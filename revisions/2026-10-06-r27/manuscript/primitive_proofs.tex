\section{Proof of the primitive-only training schedule}
\label{sec:r27primitiveproof}
\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:r27primitive}]
Work backward. The terminal coefficient is $P_T=Q_f$. Suppose the future has
been finalized and $Q_j\preceq P_j\preceq\overline P_j$ for the next
nonterminal date. Then $m_jI\preceq M_j:=dP_j\preceq L_jI$. The isotropic
iteration of Proposition~\ref{prop:r25isotropic} preserves
$c_jI\preceq W'W\preceq M_j$ and contracts the Frobenius error by the factor
$1-2\alpha_jc_j$. The initial error is at most $\sqrt d L_j$, so
\eqref{eq:r27cap} gives $\|W'W-M_j\|_F\leq e_j$.

Write $\widehat P_j=W'W/d$. The actor solves
$(R_t+\beta B_t'\widehat P_jB_t)K_t=\beta B_t'\widehat P_jA_t$.
Since $0\preceq\widehat P_j\preceq P_j$,
\[
 \|K_t\|_2\leq\frac{\beta b_t L_j a_t}{dr_t},\qquad
 \|A_t-B_tK_t\|_2\leq
 a_t\left(1+\frac{\beta b_t^2 L_j}{dr_t}\right).
\]
The own-policy defect is
$D_t=\beta B_t'(\widehat P_j-P_j)(A_t-B_tK_t)$, up to an irrelevant sign.
Hence $\|D_t\|_F\leq\chi_te_j/d\leq\sqrt{\eta q_tr_t}$.
Completion of squares and $H_t\succeq r_tI$ give
\[
 0\leq g_t(x)=x'D_t'H_t^{-1}D_tx
 \leq\eta q_t\|x\|^2\leq\eta x'Q_tx.
\]
The coefficient of the minimum current-action objective is at most the
coefficient obtained with zero current control. Therefore
\[
 Q_t\preceq P_t\preceq(1+\eta)Q_t+\beta A_t'P_jA_t
 \preceq\overline P_t.
\]
Noise constants cancel from this local comparison and are evaluated under the
same policy. This proves both induction claims. The coercive comparison
lemma then gives $J^\pi\leq(1+\eta)J^*$ and
$J^\pi-J^*\leq\eta J^{\rm ref}=\epsilon_0$.

A square-factor gradient update uses a fixed number of dense matrix products,
so it costs $O(d^3)$ real operations. Own-policy evaluation, reference-envelope
construction, and the $T$ actor solves add $O(Td^3)$. Keeping one factor,
gain, and value matrix at each date uses $O(Td^2)$ entries. These counts include
neither precision-dependent bit costs nor a comparison lower bound.
\end{proof}
