13   economic questions. We now connect the policies returned by a construction frontier      13

14   to direct cost inference and then to a choice of implementation resources. The           14

15   construction data select the policies; independently generated evaluation paths          15

16   assess them. This separation permits a valid comparison of first-crossing policies       16

17   with different resolutions.                                                              17
