22                                         1. INTRODUCTION                                      22

23     A continuation approximation matters because it helps produce an economic                23

24   decision. Prediction error, the loss of the implemented policy and the work needed to      24

25   certify that loss are different objects. This paper connects them within a constructive    25

26   Neural Bellman Operator (NBO). The economic target is a feasible complete policy           26

27   under the original law, not an action that is optimal only for a fitted surrogate.         27

28     The first construction evaluates each continuation against its own fitted future         28

29   and retains feasible action witnesses. Its affine–ReLU realization and primitive           29

30   error account do not require a successful nonconvex optimizer. The second imple- 30

1    mentation queries the original Bellman problem recursively, only at states needed          1

2    by its current action search. A trained selector proposes where to query; outward          2

3    lower and upper bounds determine whether information is sufficient. These are two          3

4    implementations within the same NBO development, not two encodings counted                 4

5    as independent methods.                                                                    5

6        The nonlinear investment economy has state-dependent capacity and continuous           6

7    innovations. Its primitive curvature controls the convexity defect of the bilinear         7

8    transition. This yields Lipschitz and semiconcavity bounds for the true Bellman            8

9    value, and hence continuous-action certificates without a tensor state table. The          9

10   trained continuation or selector is not assumed convex. Boundary queries, protected        10

11   refinement, acquired-state feasibility and an explicitly costed rational recovery          11

12   determine the complete policy’s original-optimum guarantee.                                12

13       The new operational question is whether a proposed action can be licensed              13

14   before purchasing its Bellman value. Strong convexity gives an upper chord from            14

15   two existing endpoint bounds. Subtracting the global action-search lower bound             15

16   produces a computable regret certificate. If that certificate closes, the actual rounded   16

17   proposal can be returned without another query. If it does not close, protected            17

18   conventional refinement remains available. This criterion replaces an unobserved           18

19   distance to the minimizing action by information present in the numerical transcript. 19
20       A corresponding margin loss measures unresolved verification work. Its achieved        20

21   value bounds additional endpoint queries on a specified context catalogue. A               21

22   separate validation result states the distributional conclusion and the required           22

23   independence assumptions. We also give a fixed-feature ReLU readout whose                  23

24   certificate-aware objective is convex and whose optimization error is bounded              24

25   by a computable box dual gap. This constructive training result does not assert            25

26   convergence of unrestricted hidden-layer fitting. The numerical comparison retains         26

27   its separately frozen L-BFGS, quadratic and other selectors, and evaluates their           27

28   actual certificate progress rather than equating action-label fit with verification        28

29   savings.                                                                                   29

30       The empirical design tests the complete procedure. Ten selectors receive identical     30

31   kernels and screens, including linear, spline, nearest-action, derivative-based and        31

32   simple analytic controls. Eight predetermined fitting seeds separate training vari- 32

15     The resulting evidence distinguishes three conclusions. The query procedure            15

16   returns certified policies without a stored state lattice. Learning can change the       16

17   amount and location of verification work, and a saved selector can be assessed           17

18   under actual reuse rather than an assumed amortization ratio. Neither conclusion         18

19   alone identifies lower expected economic cost or representation-specific superiority. 19
20   The purchase decision therefore uses observed work, direct policy-cost intervals         20

21   and the declared price of computation as separate inputs.                                21

                                              12. CONCLUSION
8                                                                                                            8

9      Neural Bellman Operators connect continuation construction to feasible decisions                      9

10   under the original economic law. The witness construction supplies an explicit                          10

11   own-future loss account. Primitive curvature also supports a recursive query imple- 11
12   mentation without a state lattice. A computable upper chord can license a predicted                     12

13   action before another Bellman evaluation, while the lower certificate retains author- 13
14   ity over accuracy.                                                                                      14

15     The operational loss measures verification work rather than distance to an un- 15
16   known optimum. Its fixed-feature ReLU readout has a certified convex optimization                       16

17   account. The two-control query extends the same policy guarantee to the previously                      17

18   specified coupled-capacity economy. These are constructive statements, distinct                         18

19   from a claim that a particular fitted network wins a computational comparison.                          19

20     The full study makes that comparison observable. It charges training, loading, in- 20
21   valid reuse, queries, path enclosures and durable records; evaluates actual complete                    21

22   policies under the continuous law; and records the growth of a two-innovation                           22

23   continuation tree. The current gain intervals do not identify a representation-specific                 23

24   economic advantage. Keeping that finding alongside the new positive certificates                        24

25   preserves the paper’s original objective: a useful numerical decision must be feasible,                 25

26   accurate and worth its complete cost.                                                                   26
