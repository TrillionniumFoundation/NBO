## B4 and M5: Direct cost inference at a meaningful declared precision

The new estimator addresses the width problem without increasing sample size after inspecting results. For a frozen feasible policy, the discounted sum of optimal Bellman advantages along its actual trajectory has expectation equal to its policy loss. Subtracting two such sums cancels the expected initial optimum under the common initial law. Their expected difference is therefore the original expected policy-cost difference. Local regret bounds control the observation range.

The **Bellman-centered cost comparison** theorem proves this identity, its range and a simultaneous endpoint confidence interval. Its proof does not assert a pathwise cash-flow identity, rely on an assumed-correct fitted critic, or compare two loss upper bounds as though they were policy costs. The actual returned action is fixed before a separately paid tighter optimal-value evaluation. Continuous-bin uncertainty, all compatible acquired actions, quadrature, action coverage and outward arithmetic remain in that calculation.

The frozen design uses 1,024 new common-path rows for each scalar task, evaluation tolerance equal to the policy target divided by 32, and a loss margin of $1/256$. Numerical and sampling half-width allocations are each $1/512$. All twelve paired contrasts and both allocations are reported. The four methods include the unchanged original R67 ReLU controller loaded from its pinned model, not a retrained replacement.

All twelve intervals lie within the declared equivalence margin, while all still contain zero. This establishes two-sided equivalence at that normalized-loss margin, not equality or strict superiority. For adaptive-minus-hat–ReLU, the centered intervals are approximately $[-0.00068263,0.00070764]$ and $[-0.00160291,0.00160253]$, rounded outward. Their widths are about 0.00139 and 0.00321, compared with about 0.423 and 0.529 for ordinary cash-cost intervals on the same new rows. The target has not changed.

The inference service is not free: the complete shared processes take approximately 9.03 and 408.63 seconds. Conditional Bellman integration dominates the numerical width; bin propagation is much smaller. Raw pre-intersection components are displayed as diagnostics and not misrepresented as an additive decomposition of the final intersected width. The two new tasks contain 2,048 common-path rows, not that number multiplied by methods, pairs or publication replays. Combining centered and ordinary cash-cost statistical families requires the corresponding union bound.
