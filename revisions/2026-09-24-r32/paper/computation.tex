\section{What the costs include}
Each neural proposal is fitted in a fresh subprocess. Its elapsed time includes interpreter/framework initialization and coefficient serialization; coefficient loading is charged at compilation. Subsequent costs are separated into witness construction, own-policy evaluation, eligible-set construction, dynamic minimum-cost computation, final evaluation, internal verification, and serialization. The independent final checker is timed separately and added in the main timing table. Reference solves, pointwise comparisons, sensitivity-envelope generation, and supplementary audit experiments are reported separately, not silently charged as necessary optimizer work.

The class-based pipeline is executed even when the installed proposal eventually qualifies for zero intervention, so that conservative over-revision is visible. The independent audit additionally evaluates the incumbent and writes a distinct zero-intervention certificate when the upper-witness test passes. The incremental own-evaluation and zero-certificate checking time is recorded. A production implementation can perform this test before cost optimization; the paper does not retroactively replace its measured class-pipeline timing by that hypothetical shortcut.

Times are single executions on the recorded machine. The same witness is reused across proposals in the study, but its full measured construction cost is charged to each cold solution for a standalone accounting. The exact dynamic-programming reference and the certified greedy baseline use the same primitives; they optimize operating return rather than the added revision-cost objective. Library memory is included in process peak resident memory. Portable uncompressed certificate bytes and mathematical piece/bit counts are reported separately.

\section{Audit trail and limitations of verification}
The checker recomputes whole-cell inequalities using exact affine overlays and point values. It does not invoke the maximizing/minimizing envelope routine or train a model. It shares representation and primitive affine operations with construction, so it is not a fully independent software stack or a machine-checked proof assistant. The supplementary mathematical proofs identify the properties these operations must satisfy. Tiny exhaustive scenario-tree checks and malformed certificates supplement, but do not replace, that proof.

The scientific guarantee is for exact compiled deployment. No hardware floating-point neural execution is covered. Economic sensitivity is over named computed policies with normalized implementation units. The original stopped-economy full-domain target and the neural-specific speedup question remain separate from the certified coupled computation. Those boundaries are part of the record and are not converted into experimental successes by a successful build.
