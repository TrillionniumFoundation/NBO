\begin{abstract}
This supplement gives the complete proofs for the original numerical contracts, attained continuation readouts, predictable original-law costs and direct acquired-policy qualification. It retains optimal-advantage centering and all preceding proof sections. The new displacement and endpoint-inference arguments explicitly cover discontinuous acquired actors, continuous innovation bins and the original investment moduli. Detailed experiments and work records are in the Numerical and Development Appendix.
\end{abstract}
