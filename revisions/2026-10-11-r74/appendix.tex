\begin{abstract}
This appendix retains the preceding economic-choice, localization, deployment, training, reuse and numerical evidence under its original contracts. It adds all thirteen direct-cost estimands for the frozen attained controllers, the precision and width accounts, and complete independent reconstruction and qualification-work records. Historical unfavorable comparisons and incomplete precision targets remain visible.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Scope of the reading set.}
The preceding sections retain the original experiments and their own observation counts. In particular, the continuation-construction account reports no new policy-cost rows in that construction experiment. The separate direct-cost experiment at the end of this appendix evaluates its frozen returned controllers and has its own prospectively declared observations, support and inference family. No historical result is relabeled as an observation of the new study.
