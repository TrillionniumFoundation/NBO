# R16 additional predeclared experiments

These experiments do not replace, select, or suppress any primary original-economy neural checkpoint.

## Fresh price continuum
Start with exact prices 1/2 and 8. Generate each 16-slab consumption/preference policy from constant initialization using the retained R14 SLSQP proposal algorithm, and fit each dual from deterministic starts using its retained closed-normal proposal algorithm. No historical actor, pilot, or chosen-node file is read. Re-evaluate the original stopped payoff and dual independently, with the R14 rational-Taylor arithmetic. Select the exact worst current price-envelope breakpoint, rounded to spacing 1/1024. If it duplicates a node, bisect the widest current price interval. Stop at a proved uniform gap below .01 or 30 generated nodes. Preserve all optimizer attempts, failures and target misses. The separate MPFR experiment checks the complete historical 18-node calculation; it must not be relabeled as a fresh MPFR library.

## State-space comparators
Use monotone wide-jump controlled Markov chains and a semi-Lagrangian weak Euler scheme, each on 9, 17 and 25 state nodes per dimension, and 64, 128 and 256 time steps. Control-head grids span the entire consumption/preference intervals and the pre-distance portfolio interval, with (3,3,4), (5,5,6), (7,7,8) nodes. Both produce continuous bilinear state interpolants of bounded action heads, piecewise constant in time; multiply the portfolio head by (2-x)/1.5. Their policies are feasible in the unchanged economy. All six candidates are certified with the same stored primary neural critic and the original continuous action upper oracle; discrete values are not certified continuous-time values. Report complete generation and verification costs and every target failure. This comparison does not claim state-of-the-art implementation optimality.

## Coupled inventory economy
Let dX=(AX+a)dt+.1dW, A=-.2I+.3M, M=11'/d, cost X'(I+.5M)X+|a|^2, terminal cost .5|X|^2, horizon one. Use two neural Riccati gains for the mean and orthogonal modes; the terminal gain .5 and its positive lower bound are imposed architecturally. Train on differential Riccati residuals, never exact-solution labels. Seeds 16200--16209; two tanh layers, width 8 on even seeds and 16 on odd seeds; Adam .005, batch 128, checkpoints 250 and 1000, float64 and one thread. Certify every retained checkpoint on 512 time cells; recheck seed 16200 final checkpoint on 128 and 2048 cells. A quadratic performance-difference identity and a Lyapunov moment inequality cover all states with |x0|^2/d<=1 for dimensions 4,8,16,32,64,128. One trained gain pair is reused across dimensions, not counted as six independent training runs. The scalar Riccati comparison is a structure-aware classical benchmark, not a neural training target. Report per-coordinate and total certified regret. This structured extension does not resolve the original nonlinear economy's full-horizon accuracy requirement.
