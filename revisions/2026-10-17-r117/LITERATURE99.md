# R99 literature and novelty boundary (checked 2026-10-14)

The paper does not claim that decision-aware learning, gradient-sensitive policy
bounds, conditional risk duality, convex quadrature, finite-menu concentration or
information-based adversarial constructions originate here.

- Elmachtoub and Grigas (2022), *Smart “Predict, then Optimize”*, Management Science
  68(1), 9–26, DOI 10.1287/mnsc.2020.3922; arXiv:1710.08005.
  Decision loss and an optimization-aware surrogate precede this revision.
- Liu and Grigas (2021), *Risk Bounds and Calibration for a Smart Predict-then-Optimize
  Method*, arXiv:2108.08887. Surrogate-to-decision-risk calibration is prior work.
- Cai and Judd (2015), *Dynamic Programming with Hermite Approximation*, Mathematical
  Methods of Operations Research 81(3), 245–267; NBER Working Paper 18540.
  Derivative information in Bellman approximation is prior work.
- Jones and Peet (2026), *Bounding the Error of Value Functions in Sobolev Norm Yields
  Bounds on Suboptimality of Controller Performance*, IEEE TAC early access,
  DOI 10.1109/TAC.2026.3727165; arXiv:2502.15421.
- Jun Liu (2026), *Verifiable Error Bounds for Physics-Informed Neural Network
  Solutions of Lyapunov and Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman Equations*, arXiv:2603.19545.
  These HJB papers preclude claiming the first residual/derivative-to-policy bound.
- Novak (2015 version), *Some Results on the Complexity of Numerical Integration*,
  arXiv:1409.6714. Deterministic indistinguishability and randomized bump arguments
  are classical methods; R99 specializes them with convex AVaR-tail constructions.

The R99 increment is explicitly delimited: a supporting certificate with imperfect
original-risk witnesses and all feasible actions, an action/work selection account
for complete NBO services, an exact decision realization of the existing oblique
continuation, and law/quantifier-specific information constants. Equivalent
conventional geometry is not excluded from the same mathematical result.

The official Econometric Society support points to the `econsocart` authoring
class retained here. Its paper-length guidance encourages shorter articles but
states no universal page cap. The R99 response reports actual lengths and does not
claim that preserving a long development companion resolves every editorial
length concern. No journal submission or acceptance is represented.
