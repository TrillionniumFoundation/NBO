## Four fully completed services

Legacy; compiled-oracle; legacy plus safeguarded improvement; compiled-oracle plus safeguarded improvement. The same backend is offered to every representation, including the online comparator. The compiler performs real-arithmetic cubic inversion only as a proposal, rounds to the existing dyadic quantum, and applies the unchanged outward original-law witness. If its terminal certificate fails, retain and charge the failed attempt and the original terminal oracle fallback. Do not change investment94.terminal_proposal or the deployed terminal policy.

Each service first completes the existing root rule: 128-bin certificate, paid online proposal when rejected, 256/512/1024/2048/4096 refinement and exact rational fallback. Fail closed if either original target remains unmet. Record the completed incumbent before improvement.

Safeguarded services then use exactly three trial rounds with fractions 1, 1/2, 1/4. At each round recompute the original-law 128-bin support at the current incumbent, clip a-s/2 to the original capacity and apply the stated fraction, round downward to 30 bits, and compute a fresh 128-bin original-policy certificate. A valid previous original-optimum lower bound may be reused, but not a different action's policy interval. Accept only if the candidate differs, its policy upper endpoint is strictly below the incumbent lower endpoint, and both original targets pass. Otherwise retain the incumbent and charge the attempted trial. Improvements are relative to the completed incumbent of the SAME backend; compilation by itself is not claimed to dominate a different completed service.
