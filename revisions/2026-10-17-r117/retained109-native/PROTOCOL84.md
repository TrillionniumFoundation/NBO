## Joint-event design

Each model has at most 512 additional two-cell winner-intersection problems.
If at most 128 alternatives survive full-menu qualification at 1/4096, all of
them seed pair selection. Otherwise, seed the minimum-lower and maximum-upper
conditional-cost alternative in each cell, retaining both when distinct.
Remove same-cell pairs, sort the rest by decreasing product of the two
maximum absolute cost endpoints, break ties lexicographically, and take the
first 512. Record every selected pair, including failures and inconclusive
certificates. This finite cost-directed rule uses the same simultaneous event;
it does not require or assert independent validation observations.

For each model evaluate excess objective 0, 1/65536, 1/32768, 1/16384, 1/8192
and 1/4096. Report the old actionwise interval, the full-menu interval and the
common-readout compatibility interval. A strict lower qualification bound
excludes an event only when it exceeds the attained objective plus the stated
allowance. Equality remains possible. Tied minimizers are included in weak
winner polyhedra; the enclosure is valid for the original deterministic tie
rule without pretending that every weak winner is actually selected.

## Numerical qualification

HiGHS proposes multipliers. Rational simplex normalization, derivative-budget
scaling and coefficient-box support verify every lower bound. An infeasible
solver status alone excludes nothing: a positive exact slack-support
separation must independently certify infeasibility. Otherwise the retained
valid lower bound supplies conservative fallback.

The economic assignment relaxation has one choice per observation cell and
all verified selected pair conflicts. Its recovered nonnegative edge duals
and cellwise maximum residuals give an exact feasible upper certificate. The
negative lower endpoint is verified separately. Every final interval is
intersected with its valid full-menu bound. The independent checker imports
neither the LP solver nor the production qualification module; it reconstructs
feature integrals from the independent antiderivative implementation, menus,
support duals, conflicts, assignment inequalities and all 36 pairwise class
transfers.

## Development disclosure and costs

fine ReLU and fine Bernstein. It tested implementation feasibility and showed
that full-menu barriers can be stronger than reference-only barriers. This
was not a preregistered statistical test and is not counted as an independent
scientific observation. The complete design above and exact source/input

Record singleton, joint-event and assignment phases, solver statuses, peak

new observations. Independent replay and document production have separate
