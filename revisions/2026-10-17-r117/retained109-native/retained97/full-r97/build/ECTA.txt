5        Our first contribution is constructive. Each dated NBO is formed against its own   5

6    already attained future, using primitive state and action covers, integration al- 6
7    lowances, and feasible action owners. The affine–ReLU realization does not assume      7

8    successful unrestricted nonconvex training. Original-owner compilation preserves       8

9    the continuation and its feasible witness while changing evaluation work. Joint- 9
10   domain and common-readout extensions retain the coupling between decisions at          10

11   different acquired states. Their hypotheses and complete statements remain in the      11

12   accompanying material.                                                                 12

13       Our second contribution is an original-law account for complete policies under     13

14   recursive risk. Centering the original conditional dual at the stored continuation     14

15   retains the penalty for changing the maximizing risk density. State-dependent          15

16   subsolutions and supersolutions then compare the implemented policy with the           16

17   original optimum. The continuous investment construction derives curvature, value      17

18   supports, and all-action inequalities from the original technology. Feasible risk      18

19   witnesses minorize the lower action problem; full-law envelopes dominate the           19

20   policy value. Representation quality, certificate validity, and computational gain     20

21   therefore have distinct, testable meanings.                                            21

22       Our third contribution joins information resolution to neural acquisition. For     22

23   convex Lipschitz continuations, the continuous-law AVaR bracket has second-order       23

24   innovation resolution. We establish a matching exponent against all deterministic      24

25   adaptive function-evaluation procedures, rather than only sharpness of the dis- 25
26   played quadrature pair. An exact oblique-ridge comparison isolates a representation    26

27   advantage over additive approximations and quadratics; it does not imply an ad- 27
28   vantage over a conventional method supplied with the same hinge geometry. A            28

29   certificate-oriented readout program gives an implementable construction whose         29

30   retained whole-domain residual span cannot be worse than that of its least-squares     30

31   control. Numerical optimizer success is not used as a certificate.                     31

4      Neural Bellman Operators connect an attained own-future continuation to fea- 4
5    sible dynamic decisions under the original economic law. Constructive neural             5

6    realization, original-owner compilation, and the complete-policy sandwich supply         6

7    the foundation. Recursive-risk qualification retains the original objective and the      7

8    all-original-action lower obligation. The investment technology supplies primitive       8

9    conditions under which these inequalities are executable.                                9

10     The new information result distinguishes quadrature-rule sharpness from a              10

11   lower bound against deterministic adaptive value queries. At fixed risk level the        11

12   continuous-law convex-Lipschitz problem has a matching second-order information          12

13   exponent. The representation comparison identifies a precise oblique geometry            13

14   that a ReLU represents efficiently, while recognizing that a conventional encoding       14

15   with that same geometry is equivalent. The verified readout construction turns a         15

16   certificate objective into an executable acquisition step, without using optimizer       16

17   termination as mathematical validation.                                                  17

18     The numerical evidence keeps separate what these results imply. The R96                18

19   quadratic winner and all simultaneous policy contrasts are retained. The new rank– 19
20   label campaign reports residual spans, rejected proposals, paid repair, and complete     20

21   acquisition brackets for every declared arm. A valid residual improvement is not         21

22   automatically a better decision, and a storage benefit is not automatically neural- 22
23   specific. These distinctions are empirical consequences of the NBO construction          23

24   problem, not reasons to replace it with a different topic.                               24

25     An economic purchase is a comparison of complete policies and complete costs. 25
26   Its interval frontier must use the same workload units for policy loss and computa- 26
27   tion, include acquisition uncertainty on both sides, and state the external prices on    27

28   which adoption depends. The resulting conditions allow favorable, unfavorable,           28

29   and unresolved comparisons to coexist without altering the economic law or con- 29
