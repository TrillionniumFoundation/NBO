\hypertarget{neural-bellman-operators-revision-r77}{%
\subsection{Neural Bellman Operators --- Revision
R77}\label{neural-bellman-operators-revision-r77}}

We thank the referee for separating the correctness of a numerical
certificate from the contribution of a fitted continuation and the
economic performance of its implemented policy. We revise the original
Neural Bellman Operators paper. Its title, author, investment
primitives, original Bellman optimum and broader historical applications
are retained. The controlling report is the owner-commissioned advisory
report on R67, dated 11 October 2026, at commit
\texttt{4b27b56f4514f6c1d77b69fe707f7202f8b06b19}, reviewing manuscript
\texttt{eacd3e217ae1e2e9ea6e154b48da7d103159d051}. It is not an official
Econometrica decision.

The revision incorporates the intervening R69/R70 execution, R72
fitted-continuation and witnessed-reuse results, and R73
qualification-optimal backward constructions. The present base is
\texttt{2d7d9c4e62abc2230087f3a092a5c493b8ca6cb0}. Later source-only
commitments are preserved but not counted as executed science. We do not
restart the paper under a different title or relabel the common verifier
as an intrinsically neural algorithm.

The central new link is the original-law evaluation of the same complete
controllers returned by the attained NBO continuation fits. A
prospectively fixed study evaluates all four constructed actors and an
admissible installed reference, with both full cash observations and
exact final-innovation integration. Two independent interval
implementations recompute every new bin and acquisition rectangle. An
explicit simultaneous-coverage and width account separates numerical
uncertainty, observed endpoint dispersion and the finite-support
penalty. The priced-choice result converts the resulting intervals into
controller-selection and replacement-fee regions. All outcome statements
and flags are generated from the fixed records, including failed
precision targets and unresolved or adverse comparisons.

\hypertarget{m1-b1.-the-original-nbo-contribution}{%
\subsection{M1 / B1. The original NBO
contribution}\label{m1-b1.-the-original-nbo-contribution}}

The original constructed continuation remains the main mathematical
object. It is evaluated against its own fitted future, produces a
feasible actor, and has an original-optimum account. R73 supplied the
missing attained fixed-feature fit: a finite residual-qualification
problem, an exact primal--dual gap, and a backward construction using
the continuation actually returned at the next date. R77 evaluates the
complete actors returned by those constructions. Thus continuation
construction, achieved qualification, implementation and independent
original-law policy cost now refer to the same numerical objects.

The optional query predictor and the witnessed-reuse mechanism remain
separate parts of the paper. Their common certificate is method-neutral;
no neural advantage follows from their validity. The new experiment
instead compares a ReLU continuation construction with a conventional
Bernstein construction under the same feature count and acquisition
contract at each resolution. The reported paired costs, not a
representation identity or a reduced fitting objective, determine their
finite-catalogue comparison. This extends the original NBO evaluation
and improvement problem without substituting a generic query-search
paper.

\hypertarget{m3-b5.-prospective-work-validation-and-fixed-targets}{%
\subsection{M3 / B5. Prospective work validation and fixed

The retained action-readout experiment uses predictor-independent
validation contexts disjoint from fitting and executes the declared
recovery after a failed screen. Its IID work-loss statement is not
assigned to recursively visited on-policy contexts. The later
predictable qualification theorem keeps conditional deployment targets
distinct from a fixed complete controller's cost.

For the new cost experiment, the complete actors, initial law, bin
precision, estimands, sample count and comparison margin are fixed
before the new rows. The conditional estimator integrates only the last
original innovation; it does not alter either action. Consequently, it
has exactly the same expected cost as the complete cash observation. The
fixed-sample empirical-Bernstein analysis is not described as an
optional-stopping experiment, and no additional observations are
authorized after a comparison is inspected. This preserves the
distinction between the earlier stopping-valid theorem and the present
fixed-design execution.

The sixty-four-block R69 catalogue, its predetermined invalidation and

R77 adds a complete policy-qualification service, not a newly optimized

\hypertarget{m5-b4.-target-precision-and-economically-interpretable-comparisons}{%
\subsection{M5 / B4. Target precision and economically interpretable
comparisons}\label{m5-b4.-target-precision-and-economically-interpretable-comparisons}}

The completed R70 centered-cost study already identifies equivalence for
twelve contrasts at its declared margin while identifying no strict
ordering; the earlier wide R67 intervals remain preserved. R73 then
constructed actual qualified continuations but added no independent cost
rows. R77 directly evaluates these constructed controllers rather than
borrowing intervals from the different callable recursive policies.

The new protocol fixes 524,288 rows, forty-bit original-law bins, a
full-width target of 1/256 and a separate two-sided equivalence margin
of 1/256. All five marginal costs and all ten paired differences are
reported for each of two estimators. The joint family contains 30
estimands, not 30 independent datasets. With log allowance ten, the
endpoint union bound is below one percent. Every interval is recomputed
from exact integer sums and sums of squares with upward rational square
roots.

The width identity separates the mean retained numerical enclosure, the
two empirical-variance terms and the finite-support penalty. Acquisition
ambiguity and arithmetic expansion are included in the numerical term
and accompanied by diagnostic counts; we do not pretend that their
interacting effects are separately additive. The complete table reports
all failed and passed width targets. Precision is not equivalence: an
interval can be narrow and identify a difference larger than the chosen
margin. Neither midpoint estimates nor subtraction of two marginal
confidence intervals replaces the paired comparison.

The exact final-innovation identity is proved under the unchanged
investment law. Conditional expectation preserves each cost estimand and
weakly reduces the variance of the exact common-path difference. It does
not guarantee that every realized numerical confidence interval is
narrower, so both estimator outcomes are retained. No optimal-value
descendant tree is needed to evaluate the frozen acquired actors.

\hypertarget{m7-b7.-independent-endpoint-reimplementation}{%
\subsection{M7 / B7. Independent endpoint
reimplementation}\label{m7-b7.-independent-endpoint-reimplementation}}

The new production evaluator uses outward binary64 operations on
centered primitive expressions. The independent evaluator uses
Python-integer endpoints on a 48-bit dyadic grid, explicit directed
integer multiplication and division, expanded cost and transition
polynomials, and independently enumerated acquisition rectangles. It
imports none of the production interval, economic or actor routines.
Both implementations evaluate every new original-law bin for every
controller. The published interval is their hull intersected with the
proved primitive support, not their potentially more optimistic
intersection.

The technical proof establishes containment by induction through
elementary interval operations and exhaustive acquisition choices.
Agreement alone is not called a proof. A separate exact-Fraction
midpoint trajectory checks 512 predetermined rows per controller, while
boundary regressions exercise state and innovation extremes. The
publication replay then re-evaluates the full new arrays with both
implementations and recomputes every statistical interval exactly.
Historical clocks and source files are not altered by this replay.

This fully independently reimplements the critical new two-date cost
calculation. It does not independently recreate every descendant of the
historical eight-million-query vector tree, and the manuscript does not
claim that it does. The R67 aliasing error, exact counterexample and
corrected execution remain first-class historical evidence.

\subsection{M8 / B8. Return, fitting and timing

A safe verifier's return does not demonstrate reliable neural fitting.
The attained R73 fixed-feature optimization gaps and original fitting
messages therefore remain distinct from service completion. The new
study has no hidden-layer fitting and does not infer a population
training probability from a fixed catalogue. It checks the original
controller identities, numerical endpoint validity and statistical
calculations separately.

The work records distinguish inherited construction, production interval
evaluation, independent integer evaluation, exact postprocessing and
complete parent service. CPU affinity and available frequency
information are recorded; frequency is not described as locked when it
is not. One new inference service is not a timing-distribution
experiment, and small differences between numerical phases are not
promoted to a cross-hardware speed claim. Operation counts,
acquired-boundary cases and peak memory are reported alongside seconds.
All earlier warning and adverse runtime records remain unchanged.

\hypertarget{m9-b9.-the-economic-choice}{%
\subsection{M9 / B9. The economic
choice}\label{m9-b9.-the-economic-choice}}

The evaluated object is a feasible investment controller constructed
from an attained continuation fit. The installed zero-investment
controller gives an explicit replacement reference within the same
economy. It is not used to replace the stronger conventional query
baselines or to manufacture a specifically neural dominance claim.
Matched ReLU-versus-Bernstein comparisons and gains against the
installed reference are reported separately.

The new priced-selection corollary holds on the same simultaneous event
for every assignment of nonnegative resource charges. A controller
minimizing the upper qualified cost plus its charge is compared with
every alternative using that alternative's interval width. For a
replacement gain in {[}l,u{]}, a declared deployment count m, monetary
scale v, qualification cost q and replacement fee f give net benefit in
{[}mvl-q-f,mvu-q-f{]}. Positive lower gain produces an entire certified
deployment and fee region; unresolved gain remains unresolved rather
than being converted into a purchase recommendation by choosing a
favorable fee.

The loss units are normalized model units. We do not estimate monetary
installation costs, infer currency from processor seconds, or present an
uncalibrated model comparison as an empirical welfare estimate. The
result makes the economic decision conditional on explicit prices and
usage, while the new observations evaluate actual complete-policy gains.
This is a substantive extension of the original
construction-to-policy-cost chain, not a substitution of certificate
sharpness for policy performance.
