\section*{Blocking concerns B1--B10}
\paragraph{B1. Neural specificity remains unestablished.}
The same acquired oblique geometry is now evaluated by a conventional hinge implementation with identical information and the same complete services. All policy and trial records match ReLU exactly in all twelve new risk-by-service comparisons. The compiler, strict safeguard and stopping rule are available to every arm. Sections~\ref{sec:tail109}, \ref{sec:budget110} and \ref{sec:study110} state what is action-aligned and what is representation neutral. We do not report a neural-exclusive region or manufacture adoption over quadratic. That substantive scientific requirement remains open, while the new original-policy work trade-off is independently established.

\paragraph{B2. Information complexity is too narrow.}
The retained nonuniform-law and randomized-quantifier statements in Section~\ref{sec:information99} retain their exact oracle restrictions. Formula access in the compiler is explicitly different from point-value information. The new Theorem~\ref{thm:budget110} proves the sharp scope of a remaining-gain certificate interface; Proposition~\ref{prop:compose110} states its recursive propagation. The paid positive-gap resolution argument remains in Section~\ref{sec:improvement109}. These additions do not claim optimal general randomized rates, multivariate tractability or a universal end-to-end Bellman lower bound.

\paragraph{B3. Representation is not decision-theoretic.}
The R99 exact decision mechanism and its proof remain, including the conventional-hinge equivalence. The current investment comparison reports actual original-policy cost intervals, regret brackets and action-distance bounds, not only function norms. Tables~\ref{tab:budget110}--\ref{tab:quality110} expose both strict gains over completed plain incumbents and positive foregone improvement against full safeguards. This directly ties computation to decisions without misattributing ordinary geometric approximation to neural networks.

\paragraph{B4. Residual-oriented fitting fails the intended service connection.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:tail109} proves why pointwise directional matching can also fail when AVaR changes its maximizing density. It adds a centered quantile-band term and a complete constrained action-loss proof. The exact diagnostic has zero directional error but original loss $1/4$. The executed stopping rule uses an original-optimum certificate rather than a global training residual. Its actual work saving is accompanied by a verified foregone-gain bound. The frozen acquisition remains least squares; it is not relabeled a prospective training test of the new tail-aware objective.

\paragraph{B5. Complete work favors conventional methods.}
We retain that conclusion where it holds and strengthen the conventional frontier. The original-model compiler is a semi-analytic oracle shared with the online comparator; full quadratic/cubic and grid controls are added beside equal-rank families. Acquisition, every candidate trial, fitting audit, serialization, certification, repair and refused improvement are charged. The primary budget omits 2,386 of 3,072 extra trial pairs across all arms/risks, but learned services are not declared uniformly cheaper than the accurate online control. The earlier quadratic winner is unchanged.

\paragraph{B6. Conditional purchase is not economic identification.}
The new result is a measured work-versus-original-policy-cost trade-off, not an invented adoption price. Theorem~\ref{thm:budget110} bounds the maximum gain an omitted computation could have delivered, and Section~\ref{sec:budget110} states the unit-consistent stopping dominance rule. The budget $2^{-16}$ is an engineering parameter with all prespecified sensitivities reported. An economically calibrated margin, workload and compute price remain necessary for a deployment decision. No new empirical adoption region is claimed.

\paragraph{B7. Newly fitted actors lack direct population inference.}
All current actors receive their own actual policy-cost intervals on the complete declared 64-state catalogue. No confidence interval from R96/R97 is transferred. This gives deterministic catalogue conclusions, not continuous-population inference. R98's independent random validation/test barrier and the population ordering of its actors are not satisfied here. The article now makes that distinction beside every numerical comparison rather than burying it in a protocol footnote.

\paragraph{B8. Independent verification stops before fitting.}
We independently assemble feature matrices, enumerate nonlinear active faces and solve free problems by pivoted QR, separately from the production constrained routine. Every nonexcluded candidate objective is compared. All six rank-six choices and rounded coefficient vectors agree exactly in this run; full polynomial fits are checked by a separate factorization, and serialization is replayed. Shared floating numerical libraries and tolerances remain explicit. Separate rational primitive integration verifies 384 original-kernel contexts, while an independent standard-library auditor reconstructs raw cost, feasibility, stopping and acceptance gates without importing fitting code.

\paragraph{B9. The active submission is cumulative.}
The new introduction and main numerical section focus on original decisions, action accuracy and paid improvement. Historical owner compilation, finite-menu selection, earlier studies and diagnostics are moved losslessly to the development companion. The source map proves every split reconstructs the original bytes. Complete current proofs remain in the supplement, not hidden in a workflow log. Historical preservation does not dictate the active narrative or imply that every archived experiment was rerun successfully.

\paragraph{B10. The negative finding is stronger than the platform claim.}
The residual-to-decision failure motivates the current theorem and algorithm. The 644-to-757 rejection increase, all family-specific reversals and zero certified learned adoption over quadratic remain in the main article. \section*{Major requested changes M1--M10}
\paragraph{M1. One central economic question.}
The revised question asks when acquired continuations improve feasible original-objective decisions at a justified complete cost. Neural construction and learning remain the paper's research object. The original investment model and recursive objective are not replaced by a different easier application; conventional controls and certificate authority are separated from the representation.

\paragraph{M2. Precise novelty.}

The stopping ceiling's interface sharpness is not claimed as a general information-complexity breakthrough. Static trajectory CVaR is explicitly distinguished from nested AVaR, including in the comparison with recent quantile-gradient work.

\paragraph{M3. Action-aligned acquisition.}
The tail theorem supplies a quantitative action-loss target with centered value error, directional error, tail-gradient oscillation and constrained stationarity. The current allocation algorithm is driven by certified remaining original gain; its actual policy and work effects are prospectively tested. \paragraph{M4. Conventional geometry frontier.}

\paragraph{M5. Preserve the adverse evidence.}

\paragraph{M6. Consequential application.}

\section*{Minor comments 1--10}
\paragraph{1. Error-versus-information terminology.}
The retained deterministic rate is described as $N^{-2}$ error versus point-value information, not as a broad information exponent independent of the oracle. Formula access and numerical bit precision have their own accounting.
\paragraph{2. Adjacent lower-bound qualifiers.}
Deterministic, point-value, law and dimension restrictions remain attached to each retained information result. Random tapes with pathwise sound intervals are not confused with ordinary high-probability coverage.
\paragraph{3. Tail constants.}
The opposite dependence of the deterministic lower and upper constants as the tail shrinks is retained. No fixed-risk exponent is relabeled a tail-uniform rate.
\paragraph{4. Readout restrictions.}
The constraints are acquisition choices, not the best approximation in the entire family. Full polynomial and grid comparators expand the frontier. A new unexecuted coefficient-bound sensitivity is not claimed.
\paragraph{5. Distinct metrics.}
The revised terminology separates continuation residual span, action gap, original-policy regret, interval width, first rejection, remaining gain and actual cost contrast. The stopping budget is not a new qualification tolerance.
\paragraph{6. Rejection beside span.}
All historical family-specific rejection changes accompany the span result. Current omission of a paid trial is not counted as improvement in the original first-proposal rejection rate.
\paragraph{7. Engineering ranges.}
All clock extrema and acquisition timings are descriptive measurements. Neither an observed maximum nor the deterministic catalogue is assigned a confidence-interval interpretation.
\paragraph{8. Zero adoption over quadratic.}
That historical fact is stated in the main text. A valid new work saving does not erase it or create a calibrated neural adoption claim.
\paragraph{9. Verified approximation and HJB residuals.}
The current supporting inequalities and the continuous-time bridge give explicit all-action and implemented-action premises, computational graph, boundary/terminal errors and proofs. The bridge does not infer uniform residual bounds from collocation samples or convergence of a trained network from BSDE existence.
\paragraph{10. Title and content.}
The original title is retained because this is its author revision. Neural construction, acquired actors, common original-law authority and conventional equivalence are stated explicitly; the title is not evidence of neural exclusivity.
