## Scientific changes and demonstrated scope

For convex Lipschitz continuations under scalar uniform innovations, the new
paid tail-localization construction matches the retained deterministic
point-information lower bound, of order `K R (1-alpha) N^-2`, uniformly in the
risk level for `N >= 32`. The supplement supplies complete localization,
quadrature, endpoint and lower-bound proofs. The economic implementation turns
an outward tail-location defect into a global all-action lower support and an
upper bound on the actual complete policy, including its unchanged future
controller. A bounded prelude and packed evaluation reduce parts of the
observed service work before the original complete fallback.

The revision also completes explicit Bellman existence/measurability/stability,
constrained-gradient, full-computation-graph, coupled policy comparison,
signed-recursion and stopped recursive-utility proofs under their stated
assumptions. General NDU/FBSDE identification, optimization convergence and
neural-exclusive superiority are not inferred from those conditional results.

Three separately frozen deterministic campaigns retain 32,256 primary and
50,176 timing decisions, every failed attempt and all contemporaneous strong
controls. The packed service improves 10 of 16 median cells against geometric
evaluation; its six losses remain visible. All services return the same
complete policies in these within-arm comparisons, so those service savings
are not lower economic policy costs.

Independent validation D reconstructs all 983 original C policy keys with
exact rational primitives and a separate implementation. The actual execution
completed 126,807 terminal contexts and 652,774 checks; its read-only reporter
has a separate 590,818-check receipt. Both risk levels give a strictly positive
ReLU-minus-quadratic mean original-cost interval. Quadratic remains the stronger
complete-cost/economic control, and the equal-feature conventional hinge is
exactly equivalent to ReLU. D validates original-policy enclosures, not a new
date-zero global optimum or every finer reference endpoint. These limitations
are scientific obligations for the continuing NBO research programme.
