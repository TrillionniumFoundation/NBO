## Sixth service: fused-tail

The five retained services execute their unchanged sources: compiled,
geometric, active-tail, masked-tail and budgeted-tail. The sixth service,
`fused-tail`, uses exactly the two-level(2,4) budgeted schedule, same cached
cut, outward endpoint intervals and original-risk defect kappa, same global
action witness, same terminal-policy allowance and unchanged complete128-bin
fallback. Whenever fallback is invoked, its original gate determines repair,
further refinement and rational fallback. Early acceptance can in principle
retain a different proposal, so no same-policy outcome is presumed.

At each attempted resolution, form the exact shared dyadic tail geometry and
mark the positive-weight cells. Pack all nonempty midpoints and endpoints,
together with their original state and action identities, into ONE call to
the unchanged original terminal compiler. Every duplicate requested payload
is still paid; no cross-state or cross-service free caching is introduced.
Scatter the returned lower values, upper values and support slopes to their
original row and column. Empty-weight positions are initialized to exact
zero. Aggregate with the same termwise-outward interval arithmetic, fixed
original density, selected-tail secants and constrained quadratic minimum.
The zero terms carry no original model observation. Outward rounding or batch
fallback can produce different numerical endpoints; qualification must still
satisfy the original unchanged thresholds.

This operation preserves the bound of at most30 outward point/support
payloads and16 floating proposal values before acceptance or complete fallback;
at a boundary the outward cap is16. The new additional count is exactly one
packed compiler invocation per attempted resolution, containing all and only
its positive-mass midpoint and endpoint payloads. Compiler-internal arithmetic,
any original compiler fallback, array packing/scattering, empty-term interval
arithmetic, failed candidates, inference, records and complete service costs
are measured rather than treated as free. The existing bound is a payload
budget, not a universal runtime bound or a new neural approximation theorem.

If a failure occurs before aggregation, preserve the completed original
point payloads and their inverse mapping in the error trace. If the original
kernel fails before producing them, preserve the requested mapping, stage and
actual partial counters; do not invent completed payloads. The stored values
and slopes are internal execution evidence, never replacement raw outcomes.
