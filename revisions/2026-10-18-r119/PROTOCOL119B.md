# R119 phase B: prospectively frozen algorithm development after phase A

## Timing of this design and retained adverse evidence

This protocol is written AFTER the complete first R119 experiment, whose actual
source/protocol commit is `1f33c2667e0dff89699c6d6a01d03eacfecf3f4e`. The first
experiment and all its raw observations remain unchanged in `results119/primary119`.
Its complete record audit passed 444,053 exact arithmetic/identity checks on
17,664 decisions and 25,161 certificate attempts. It used 6,912 primary and
10,752 timing decisions. All three services returned the same within-arm
complete policies; original economic-cost differences were exactly zero.

The first active-tail service was slower than the geometric service in 15/16
median 32-state timing cells. ReLU was slower than both controls at both risks;
43 ReLU proposals per risk exhausted the tail ladder and needed the same
original conventional repair. Its six levels paid for unusable refinement,
while its vector kernel also evaluated empty tails and continued floating
bisection for boundary-selected proposals. These findings motivate the two
algorithmic interventions below. They are preserved as negative evidence.

The two-level cutoff and the decision to implement masking are informed by
those SAME deterministic first-phase states and results. Phase B is a declared
development experiment. Its repeated catalogue is not an unseen validation
sample, an independently drawn population, or a retrospectively preregistered
part of phase A. This file, both new algorithms, all comparator source and the
adapted independent audit are committed before any phase-B numerical observation.

## Five fully paid services and the new work bound

Every proposal is passed to all five services:

1. `compiled`: unchanged frozen 128-bin complete service.
2. `geometric`: unchanged frozen R117 8,16,32,64,128 initial schedule.
3. `active-tail`: unchanged first-phase 2,4,8,16,32,64 per-tail schedule, full
   vectorized tail evaluations, and complete fallback.
4. `masked-tail`: new sparse tail implementation with that same six-level
   schedule. Endpoint floating comparisons select a boundary proposal when
   indicated. Six bisection updates are evaluated only for the remaining
   interior proposal rows. Only nonzero-weight tails are sent to the original
   terminal compiler. The same outward endpoint defect kappa certifies every
   cut; a floating endpoint sign alone certifies nothing. This ablation measures
   sparse geometry without shortening the refinement schedule.
5. `budgeted-tail`: the same sparse implementation tries exactly 2 and 4 cells
   per nonempty tail, then enters the unchanged frozen 128-bin complete service
   for any unqualified proposal. It does not change either loss/width target.
   Whenever fallback is invoked, its unchanged 128-bin gate determines
   conventional repair, further refinement and rational fallback. Early tail
   acceptance may retain a proposal that another service repairs; its actual
   original-policy cost is therefore evaluated in the new reference comparison.

The original feasible density and full-action lower witness are unchanged.
Omitting a zero-probability integral term is exact. The six interior floating
updates merely propose a cut, whose original-law deficit is always paid. The
budgeted prelude acquires at most 30 outward terminal point values per proposed
action: two cut endpoints and at most10+18 quadrature points for the two tail
resolutions. A boundary cut uses at most 16. At most16 floating terminal values
are evaluated for cut proposals (four endpoint-comparison values and at most
twelve interior values). This is a terminal-query/work bound, not a claimed
wall-clock factor or a promise of economic improvement. Array-grouping, sorting,
masking, recording, all rejected attempts and the complete fallback are charged.

The default budgeted schedule is fixed now. No further cap, state, parameter,
actor, target, result-selection or evaluation rule will be changed after phase-B
outcomes. A source defect creates a separately identified failed attempt and
amended source if necessary; no clocks are replaced for looking unfavorable.
