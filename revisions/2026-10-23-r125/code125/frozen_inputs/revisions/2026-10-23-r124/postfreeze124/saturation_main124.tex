\subsection{When joint interpolation cannot improve the paid certificate}
\label{sec:saturation124}

The first frozen R124 experiment motivates an additional mathematical
diagnosis. This subsection was developed after that experiment; it was not
used to choose its states, omit a solve, or reduce its recorded costs.
The relevant distinction is between optimizing a certificate more accurately
and obtaining information that can change its best attainable value.

Suppose the incumbent $a\in[0,A]$ is the sole distinct paid anchor, with
finite information
\[
 W\le q(a)\le U,\qquad \ell\le q'(a)\le u,
 \qquad 0<\mu\le L.
\]
At repeated copies of this same anchor, intersect the level intervals and
derivative intervals first. Assume their intersections are nonempty.
Let $\mathcal Q_1$ be the class of continuously differentiable,
$\mu$-strongly convex functions with $L$-Lipschitz derivative satisfying
exactly these intervals. No additional global derivative cap, raw supporting
quadratic, or original primitive formula is included in this definition.
For $d=b-a$, put
\begin{align}
 m_-(d)&=\min\{\ell d,ud\}+\frac\mu2d^2,\notag\\
 m_+(d)&=\max\{\ell d,ud\}+\frac L2d^2.
 \label{eq:saturationmodels124}
\end{align}

\begin{proposition}[Single-anchor saturation]
\label{prop:saturation124}
For every feasible $b$, the exact joint-information extrema are
\begin{align}
 \min_{q\in\mathcal Q_1}\{q(b)-q(a)\}&=m_-(b-a),&
 \max_{q\in\mathcal Q_1}\{q(b)-q(a)\}&=m_+(b-a),\notag\\
 \min_{q\in\mathcal Q_1}q(b)&=W+m_-(b-a),&
 \max_{q\in\mathcal Q_1}q(b)&=U+m_+(b-a).
 \label{eq:saturationextrema124}
\end{align}
Every extremum is attained by a quadratic, and these are precisely the
integrated derivative-cone and transported-level bounds. Adding unobserved
nodes, exact adjacent interpolation constraints and their implied finite
boxes cannot tighten them. The assertion includes an endpoint incumbent,
an endpoint candidate, $b=a$, and $\mu=L$.

For $b\ne a$, write $g_*=u$ if $b>a$ and $g_*=\ell$ if $b<a$.
A stronger upper-bound witness has derivative
\begin{equation}\label{eq:localcurvaturewitness124}
 q_+'(t)=g_*+\mu(t-a)+(L-\mu)
       \{\operatorname{clip}_{[\min(a,b),\max(a,b)]}(t)-a\},
 \qquad q_+(a)=U.
\end{equation}
It has curvature $L$ only between the two actions and curvature $\mu$
elsewhere. It attains the same upper contrast and absolute upper value.
If $q_+'(0)\ge-M$ and $q_+'(A)\le M$, the upper equalities remain
true even after adding the global restriction $|q'|\le M$.
\end{proposition}

The conclusion concerns the stated information interface. In particular,
finite-radius recovery of a derivative interval from a raw support can be
conservative. Reintroducing that raw support as a constraint can shrink
the class further; the proposition does not rule out improvement from
that stronger calculation or from the complete investment formulas.

\paragraph{A verified switch that avoids redundant solves.}
The proposition yields a certificate-selection rule. After constructing
the common candidate menu, if the only distinct paid anchor is the
incumbent and the interpolation program contains only the stated data
and derived boxes, retain $m_+$ instead of calling a numerical
interpolation optimizer. If the optional global cap is imposed, first
check the two endpoint inequalities in
\eqref{eq:localcurvaturewitness124}. This requires rational arithmetic
and no original-risk query. Existing raw-support lower bounds and
original-optimum lower bounds remain in the qualification test.

\begin{corollary}[Certificate equivalence and computation allocation]
\label{cor:saturationbypass124}
Suppose a joint stage would only intersect its sound dual upper contrast
$D_b$ with the fused upper $m_+(b-a)$ and would replace the candidate
policy upper by the smaller of its existing upper and
$U+\min\{m_+(b-a),D_b\}+\delta$.
Under Proposition~\ref{prop:saturation124}, $D_b\ge m_+(b-a)$.
The switch therefore preserves every candidate contrast, absolute
upper, gain, regret and width test. With the same lower bounds,
rounding and tie convention, it returns the same policy as a completed
sound joint stage. It removes one numerical optimization and its
associated exact dual-bound evaluation for each switched candidate.

Holding $[\ell,u]$, $\mu,L$ and the action pair fixed, narrowing only
$[W,U]$ cannot improve the exact contrast in
\eqref{eq:saturationextrema124}. For $b>a$, lowering $u$ by $e$ lowers
the exact upper contrast by $e(b-a)$; for $b<a$, raising $\ell$ by $e$
lowers it by $e(a-b)$, whenever the updated information remains feasible.
Thus arithmetic refinement of an already saturated program has no
certificate benefit, whereas tighter marginal information can have a
quantified benefit. Any positive cost of that redundant arithmetic is
dominated by the switch for this calculation.
\end{corollary}

This is a stopping rule for a specified certificate computation, not a
reason to stop the economic decision algorithm, abandon new information,
or ignore absolute-value precision. A narrower level interval can improve
objective width; when it also tightens the recovered derivative interval,
the marginal effect must be included. Equivalence is asserted for completed
sound reference calculations. Avoiding a reference implementation's
numerical failure can change its fail-closed outcome while preserving
economic safety, so arbitrary failure paths are not claimed identical.

\paragraph{The saved experiment and the scope of the diagnosis.}
The independent post-execution exact reader verifies all 404 solve-bearing
candidates in the first R124 campaign. They belong to 202 rows, each with
one paid incumbent anchor. It supplies feasible primal vectors attaining
the fused bound in every saved interpolation program. All 404 also satisfy
the endpoint conditions for the cap $M=7$ using
\eqref{eq:localcurvaturewitness124}. Hence greater optimizer accuracy, or
merely adding this optional cap, could not have tightened these particular
contrasts. The 404 recorded solves and their actual costs remain charged.
The audit makes no claim of a newly measured runtime saving.

\paragraph{An empty fused menu is a different phenomenon.}
The other 98 two-anchor rows have interior incumbents. Every individually
transported derivative interval contains zero at the incumbent, and all
18 recorded proposal entries equal that action. They supplied no distinct
candidate to the joint stage. This does not imply that all joint information
was uninformative. For example, take any $A,\mu>0$, $L\ge\mu$, and the
exact levels $q(0)=q(A)=0$ with derivative intervals
$q'(0)\in[-\mu A,\mu A]$, $q'(A)\in[0,2\mu A]$.
The fused cones are $[\mu(t-A),\mu(t+A)]$. At incumbent zero every
fused and single-anchor robust proposal remains zero. Nevertheless,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:menustarvation124}
 \max_q\{q(A/2)-q(0)\}=-\frac{\mu A^2}{8}<0.
\end{equation}
The quadratic $q(t)=\mu(t^2-At)/2$ attains equality. For $\mu=2$ and
$A=1/4$ the guaranteed original-q gain is $1/64$. This is an exact
information example, not an evaluated investment state or a claim about
what the 98 observed states would achieve. It motivates a separately
frozen experiment in which paid values also generate candidate actions.
