## Frozen catalogue

Before any full numerical execution, commit ordinary executable sources,
run the declared smoke tests and create `audit/SOURCE_FREEZE69.json`.
The manifest records the entire catalogue, every new scientific Python source,
the source workflow SHA and inherited Python dependencies. Preparation may
materialize `vector_base69.py` from the pinned R67 ordinary source with the
explicit witness/split modifications; the generated ordinary file is committed
and hashed before experiments. No opaque executable bundle is used.

Scalar tasks are `(d,T,target,N)=(2,2,1/128,32)` and `(8,3,1/64,8)`.
All policies acquire 24 bits per coordinate and round actions downward to
32 bits, exactly as in the R67 query contract. The two fitting seeds are 6901
and 6902. They are finite catalogue choices, not a population training theorem.

There are 80 cold comparison services: adaptive and upper-chord minimizer
controls, plus hat-ReLU, degree-four Bernstein, label-loss ReLU and quadratic
proposals; the learned arms have two seeds, and every task/arm/seed has four
separate process repetitions. All learned arms purchase the same 48 training
states and endpoint information per nonterminal date. Hat-ReLU and Bernstein
have exactly `5*d` coefficients and minimize the same convex certificate loss
with the same box, penalty and 160-iteration optimizer cap. Their returned
quantized weights receive an independent exact-rational objective and dual-gap
calculation. Label-ReLU isolates the loss on the same hat features. Quadratic
retains its declared different parameter count. No unused training is charged
to the prediction-free controls.

There are 16 validation services: every learned arm, seed and scalar task,
with 256 new uniform acquired-state contexts at every nonterminal date. The
stream is predictor-independent and disjoint from fitting. The recorded
pre-query score and clipped margin loss bound a declared complete-action mesh
recovery after a failed screen. Every actual recovery is executed, including
its descendant queries. This recovery is distinct from the adaptive policy
service: its work-loss result is not advertised as an IID theorem for
recursively visited policy states. The log allowance is 12 and the validation
family error is at most 0.01. Context acquisition and validation costs are paid.

There are 56 reuse services: adaptive, hat-ReLU, Bernstein and quadratic,
with the same two seeds for learned arms and four process repetitions on each
scalar task. Each process trains and serializes once and then processes 64
predetermined new blocks of eight trajectories. Block 16 presents a stale
acquisition identifier and must fall back to adaptive search. Loading, hashing,
validation of identity, fallback, policy queries, path enclosures and durable
block files are charged. A stable crossing means cumulative observed work is
below the matched adaptive prefix from some block at most 48 through block 63.
A late or nonpersistent crossing does not satisfy that criterion.

Two inference services use 1,024 new common-path rows per scalar task.
Their arms are adaptive, newly trained hat-ReLU, newly trained Bernstein and
the original R67 ReLU controller loaded from the pinned R67 inference-model
file (seed 6603). The latter is not retrained or relabeled. Initial states and
shocks have the original continuous uniform law represented by 48-bit bins.
The fixed evaluator tolerance is `target/32`. The predeclared two-sided loss
margin is `1/256` in normalized cost units; this is a design margin, not an
estimated monetary installation cost. The numerical and sampling half-width
allocations are each `1/512`; achieved allocations, equivalence and
noninferiority are reported whether or not they pass. No sample-size increase
or tolerance revision is permitted after inspecting the new contrast signs.
All six pairwise contrasts are retained per task. The centered interval uses
a Hoeffding log upper bound of 12, with family error at most 0.01; same-path
ordinary cash-cost intervals use the retained empirical-Bernstein account and
its own declared tail budget. The union bound must be used to combine families.

There are 34 vector services. For dimensions 2 and 8 and horizons 2 and 3,
simplicial, uniform largest-edge, two-output hat-ReLU and two-output quadratic
proposals each receive two process repetitions, with eight continuous-bin
paths and target 1/4. Both learned proposals use the same 12 paid teacher
states per nonterminal date and fitting seed 6901. The vector training loss
is action-label loss; it is not the scalar certificate-aware training theorem.
Two additional dimension-two, horizon-four services (simplicial and hat-ReLU)
use one path each. The latter transfers its three-date fitted readout to the
four-date proposal; the unchanged certificate remains authoritative. This is
explicitly a transfer comparison, not four-date supervised fitting.

The total planned catalogue is **188 services**. A return failure, timeout,
optimizer warning, precision failure or independent disagreement is retained.
A scientific rerun after a source correction must have a new freeze and a new
attempt directory; the earlier execution is not overwritten.

## Measurement and independent checks

Parent clocks include launch, imports, training or loading, all queries,
path evaluation, serialization, child shutdown and log flushing. Parent
receipt bookkeeping is timed at the cohort level. Each worker pins one allowed
CPU, fixes numerical-library thread counts to one, and records available CPU
frequency/governor information before and after execution. Frequency is not
claimed locked unless the recorded platform actually supports it. Four
repetitions are descriptive paired timing evidence, not a hardware-invariant
performance probability. Query, descendant, proposal and training counts
remain separate from seconds.

Every new sampled trajectory is checked at its rational mid-bin point by a
separate implementation of acquisition, feasibility, stage cost, transition
and terminal cost. Exact scalar and two-control final-innovation formulas and
small full two-date value problems have separate regression checks. Full-bin
outward evaluation remains the scientific enclosure method. These checks are
not claimed to independently re-integrate the inherited eight-million-query
vector tree or to constitute formal proof checking.

The release preserves every R67 active statement and all historical sources,
including adverse timings, unresolved original cost intervals and the R67
aliasing counterexample. New methods, new samples, repeated streams and
historical records have separate identities. Stronger precision or safe return
is never, by itself, described as neural computational superiority.
