\hypertarget{neural-bellman-operators-revision-r84}{%
\subsection{Neural Bellman Operators --- Revision
R84}\label{neural-bellman-operators-revision-r84}}

We thank the referee for the detailed assessment of the distinction
between a correct safeguarded computation and an economically
informative Neural Bellman Operator. We revise the original paper,
retaining its title, economic primitives, constructive own-future
continuation, feasible action witnesses and original Bellman optimum.
The controlling advisory report is dated 11 October 2026, on review
commit \nolinkurl{4b27b56f4514f6c1d77b69fe707f7202f8b06b19}, and reviews
R67 at \nolinkurl{eacd3e217ae1e2e9ea6e154b48da7d103159d051}. It is not
represented as an editorial decision of the Econometric Society.

The report predates the subsequent attained-controller and original-law
cost developments. R69--R73 execute qualification and protected reuse;
R77--R81 connect complete constructed controllers, higher-precision cost
inference, affine calibration and exact optimization barriers. R82--R83
price the possible acquired-action changes and derive economic stopping
regions. This revision starts from the completed source-bound R83
recovery at \nolinkurl{0f807677ac6f2a47455f49f061ddbc9710b28738}, not from
an obsolete manuscript wrapper. Those sources, their complete records,
the interrupted R82 calculation and every unfavorable result remain
preserved.

R84 adds a further substantive restriction: a qualified output has one
readout vector. An alternative must win its complete feasible menu, and
choices at different cells must be compatible with that same vector. The
new main theorem transfers exact certificates for these restrictions
into original-law policy-cost bounds. Its proof is in the article. The
Numerical and Development Appendix derives the rational qualification,
separation and assignment duals, gives strict-improvement and
relaxation-gap examples, and reports every declared numerical
comparison. This extends the original NBO
optimization-to-economic-decision chain rather than substituting a
different paper on optional search proposals.

\hypertarget{m1-b1-what-belongs-to-the-nbo-construction}{%
\subsection{M1 / B1: What belongs to the NBO
construction?}\label{m1-b1-what-belongs-to-the-nbo-construction}}

The paper separates the constructive operator, the
representation-neutral safeguard and the qualification of the attained
continuation. Correctness of the safeguard is not made artificially
neural. The NBO nevertheless remains a substantive constructed object:
its own-future continuation, feasible witnesses, affine--ReLU
realization and primitive error account determine an implementable
policy. The latest calculations concern the policy cost of classes of
approximately optimized attained continuations, not merely the location
of an optional extra Bellman probe.

The new winner constraints use the actual feature representation and one
shared readout. They can be applied to both ReLU and conventional
Bernstein continuations, which is necessary for a meaningful comparison.
A stronger common theorem is not presented as evidence of exclusive
neural superiority. The original title and model are retained because
the result directly strengthens the economic qualification of the
original construction.

\hypertarget{m2-b2-matched-conventional-comparisons-and-full-menu-qualification}{%
\subsection{M2 / B2: Matched conventional comparisons and full-menu
qualification}\label{m2-b2-matched-conventional-comparisons-and-full-menu-qualification}}

The retained factorial experiment compares both bases, both
qualification rules and both acquisition resolutions under their
declared information and implementation contracts. It retains the
earlier ReLU advantage under legacy qualification and the reversed
matched ordering under affine-calibrated qualification. The latter is
not hidden by selecting a different baseline. Construction clocks with
different fitting or ablation boundaries are not asserted to be a
universal matched speed comparison.

R84 gives every one of the 976 previously admitted alternatives a
qualification problem against all 32 competing actions in its unchanged
menu. The prior reference-only barrier remains a valid lower bound and
is intersected with the new bound. Thus the new calculation cannot
obtain a favorable comparison by deleting feasible actions or altering
the fitted labels. An action that beats the reference but loses to
another feasible menu member need not be retained as a possible
implemented choice.

The shared-readout calculation then qualifies 1,572 predetermined
cross-cell pairs. It produces a stricter economic interval than
full-menu qualification in fifteen of the 24 model--tolerance records.
The four models and six tolerances are all reported, including unchanged
and unresolved cases. The exact finite design, cost-directed pair
ordering and development pilot are disclosed; this is not portrayed as a
prospective random benchmark.

\hypertarget{m3-b3-predictable-validation-and-post-selection}{%
\subsection{M3 / B3: Predictable validation and
post-selection}\label{m3-b3-predictable-validation-and-post-selection}}

The predictable-evaluation and protected-query theorems remain in their
original sections with their filtration, context-law and validation
requirements. They are not replaced by an assumption that recursively
produced contexts are independent. The intervening qualification and
readout experiments also remain in the compiled record.

The new theorem addresses a different selection issue. Conditional on
the original simultaneous attained-policy cost event, every valid
winner, joint-event and assignment inequality is deterministic. The
resulting comparison therefore holds for every readout in the stated
objective sublevel and for all allowances simultaneously. Inspecting
these valid bounds does not require a union bound over an uncountable
coefficient class or a new independent cost sample. Changing the labels,
future actor, initial law or estimand is outside that implication unless
separately qualified.

This establishes an optimization-to-decision guarantee for outputs of
the declared fixed-feature problem. It does not substitute that
guarantee for prospective work-loss validation of a newly fitted
predictor under a different context distribution.

\hypertarget{m4-b4-reuse-complete-work-and-prospective-evidence}{%
\subsection{M4 / B4: Reuse, complete work and prospective
evidence}\label{m4-b4-reuse-complete-work-and-prospective-evidence}}

The longer protected-reuse catalogues already deposited after R67 remain
available, together with failed admissions, unfavorable timing
comparisons and their exact charging boundaries. The new calculation
does not reclassify their observations or infer a stable neural
amortization advantage from an isolated late crossing.

R84 adds a separately measured use of existing evidence. Once attained
controllers, actionwise integrals and their cost intervals have been
purchased, new full-menu and common-readout certificates can improve the
economic decision without another fit or simulation. The scientific
process records 976 singleton qualification problems, 1,572 joint-event
problems, the assignment problems at every allowance, all solver
statuses, phase times and peak memory. Its recorded complete interpreter
time is approximately 104.699 seconds on the stated ARM runner,
including imports, input reads, computations, durable records and
shutdown. That is an incremental qualification cost, not the complete
cold-start cost of producing all prerequisite evidence.

The previous fitting, integration and statistical services retain their
original clocks. Independent checking and publication have separate
clocks. One recorded execution is not treated as a timing distribution,
and differences in these qualification times are not used to claim an
optimizer or representation speed ranking.

\hypertarget{m5-b5-useful-policy-cost-precision-and-an-economic-decision}{%
\subsection{M5 / B5: Useful policy-cost precision and an economic
decision}\label{m5-b5-useful-policy-cost-precision-and-an-economic-decision}}

The original-law R80 experiment already increases the direct cost family
to 524,288 common bin rows and 94 declared estimands, with conditional
integration, sampling uncertainty, continuous-bin uncertainty and
arithmetic enclosure accounted for separately. R84 does not add those
rows again. It refines the deterministic optimizer-class transfer
applied to that already covered family.

At excess objective 1/4096, coarse-minus-fine Bernstein has an inherited
actionwise interval crossing zero. The new compatible interval is
strictly positive. Consequently the finer controller has lower
original-law cost for every pair of readouts in the two qualified
classes, even though the readouts need not reproduce the attained action
arrays exactly. The article reports the outward interval and the entire
supported refinement-fee range. Because smaller objective sublevels are
contained in the largest one, the conclusion also applies to every
smaller allowance, not just the six displayed tolerances.

The matched fine ReLU-minus-Bernstein comparison at 1/65536 remains
strictly positive and has a narrower compatible interval. This preserves
the calibrated Bernstein advantage rather than inventing a new neural
ranking. Coarse-ReLU comparisons that remain inconclusive and the
larger-tolerance unresolved fine-basis cases remain in the complete
tables. The declared equivalence margin is tested using exact endpoints,
not rounded decimal appearances. A tighter certificate is distinguished
from a newly observed improvement of the attained controller itself.

\hypertarget{m6-b6-vector-controls-and-the-horizon-problem}{%
\subsection{M6 / B6: Vector controls and the horizon
problem}\label{m6-b6-vector-controls-and-the-horizon-problem}}

The vector-control construction, derivative and simplex results,
two-shock experiments and large descendant-tree growth are retained
under their existing assumptions. The new theorem applies whenever the
fixed-continuation conditional contrasts can be enclosed and the
first-date acquired action has the declared finite menu. Its proof does
not depend on a one-dimensional readout coefficient vector or on a
scalar cost approximation.

The newly executed study remains the original two-state, two-date
investment economy with a fixed terminal actor. It adds neither a
trained two-output vector benchmark nor an independent regeneration of
the largest vector tree. The theorem is not used to disguise that
distinction. Jointly refitting future controllers or claiming favorable
high-dimensional horizon scaling would require additional work beyond
these fixed-continuation certificates. The original vector results and
their adverse runtime evidence remain compiled, rather than being
deleted to simplify the new claim.

\hypertarget{m7-b7-independent-arithmetic-and-preserved-failures}{%
\subsection{M7 / B7: Independent arithmetic and preserved
failures}\label{m7-b7-independent-arithmetic-and-preserved-failures}}

The full R83 recovery and the interrupted R82 execution are preserved
separately. The old identity-lookup exception and the earlier
path-enclosure aliasing counterexample remain part of the development
record. The publication verification reruns the inherited relevant
suites and the independently implemented original-law integrations;
original service clocks are not replaced by replay clocks.

For the new calculation, the independent checker imports neither the
production optimizer nor its qualification module. It reconstructs exact
feature means from the separate antiderivative implementation, all
winner-menu inequalities, the coefficient-box support dual, every
joint-event separation, all conflict rosters and the final cost
transfers. It verifies 2,548 qualification certificates and 16,548
assignment-dual inequalities. All 36 pairwise class comparisons are
checked.

There are 104 numerical infeasibility statuses. Each is accompanied by a
strictly positive rational box-separation certificate; an infeasible
solver flag alone is never sufficient. If such a certificate were
absent, the event would retain its independently valid lower
qualification bound and would not be removed merely because optimization
failed. Regression tests exercise this fallback explicitly, along with
forged economic-dual values, duplicate sparse indices, negative
multipliers, strict boundaries and missing assignment coverage.

Full independent qualification of these new objects is not described as
full independent reexecution of the largest historical vector
computation. The respective audit scopes are kept distinct.

\hypertarget{m8-b8-what-is-guaranteed-about-fitting}{%
\subsection{M8 / B8: What is guaranteed about
fitting?}\label{m8-b8-what-is-guaranteed-about-fitting}}

Verifier return, numerical fitting performance and population
reliability remain separate objects. All historical fitting warnings and
seed-level observations are retained. The current study adds zero
fitting attempts and makes no claim about unrestricted hidden-layer
optimization success.

The new deterministic implication is stronger than an empirical
success-frequency assertion for the declared readout problem. If a
feasible output has primal value F(theta), an independently valid dual
value d no larger than the optimum, and primal--dual gap at most
epsilon, then it belongs to the covered objective class. The classwide
policy-cost interval and any supported economic decision apply to that
output regardless of which admissible coefficient vector the fitting
algorithm returned. The shared-readout restrictions improve the decision
by ruling out impossible combinations, not by assuming that an
unobserved optimizer follows a favorable path.

\hypertarget{m9-b9-economic-interpretation-refinement-charges-and-basis-reversal}{%
\subsection{M9 / B9: Economic interpretation, refinement charges and
basis
reversal}\label{m9-b9-economic-interpretation-refinement-charges-and-basis-reversal}}

The added positive result is a robust investment-controller refinement
decision. The fine Bernstein class is cheaper than the coarse Bernstein
class over the stated nonzero optimization allowance. Subtracting a
supplied additional refinement charge from both difference endpoints
gives the supported, unresolved and opposite-choice regions. The exact
normalized fee ceiling is generated from the verified lower endpoint and
printed with outward rounding.

This conclusion uses the original investment law and actual
implemented-policy cost. It is not a comparison between two all-state
error bounds, and it does not use simulated evaluation rows as economic
deployments. The inherited priced-selection and deployment-allocation
results retain separate loss units, information prices, compute charges
and deployment counts. No dollar calibration, estimated installation
charge or universal neural purchase recommendation is invented.

The observed reversal between legacy and calibrated basis rankings
remains scientifically relevant: qualification can change which
representation is economically preferable. The paper develops that
positive mechanism and its optimizer-class robustness rather than
attaching an unconditional superiority claim to ReLU.

\hypertarget{m10-b10-exposition-scope-and-substantive-preservation}{%
\subsection{M10 / B10: Exposition, scope and substantive
preservation}\label{m10-b10-exposition-scope-and-substantive-preservation}}

The main text now proceeds from attained NBO continuations and their
original-optimum accuracy through actionwise qualification, economic
stopping and common-readout compatibility. The new theorem includes its
complete proof in the article. Exact dual construction, complexity
accounting, counterexamples and the exhaustive new catalogue are in the
Numerical and Development Appendix. Core technical-supplement sections
stay in their existing locations.

Every inherited active section is retained byte-identically, compiled
once and in its original document. Original wrappers and historical PDFs
are preserved separately and never relabeled as new compilations. The
title, author information, economic primitives, historical records and
older remote branches are unchanged. The active manuscript is extended
without arbitrary deletion to meet an obsolete internal page target; its
actual page count is reported by the new release audit.

The historical diffusion, recursive-preference, endogenous-preference,
temporal-self and game results retain their own assumptions in the
companion material. The present fixed-continuation expected-cost theorem
is not silently applied to nonlinear recursive preferences. Maintaining
this hypothesis discipline preserves the original research program
without changing the topic or claiming an unproved extension.

\hypertarget{verification-and-submission-package}{%
\subsection{Verification and submission
package}\label{verification-and-submission-package}}

The R84 release contains ordinary main, supplement, numerical appendix
and response sources; the full-menu and common-readout theorem; all
exact dual and economic records; the frozen study design; inherited
evidence and failures; and the complete verification logs. The release
requires the inherited unskipped suites, the new exact regressions,
independent certificate checking, relevant full-bin replay,
four-document compilation and a clean archive rebuild. Visual inspection
is a separately recorded check of the actual PDF hashes, not an
implication of a successful compiler.

The principal addition is a constructive common-readout restriction that
sharpens economic accuracy and identifies a new classwide refinement
decision in the original NBO economy. It is supported by a complete
deterministic catalogue and independent rational verification. Universal
neural superiority, unqualified fitting reliability, empirically
calibrated fees and unexecuted large-vector validation are not inferred
from that result.
