22                                          1. INTRODUCTION                                       22

23     A continuation approximation matters through the economic decision it produces. 23
24   Prediction error, original-optimum policy loss and verification work are different           24

25   objects. This paper connects them through a constructive Neural Bellman Operator             25

26   (NBO), its attained own-future continuation and the complete feasible controller             26

27   implemented under the original law.                                                          27

28     The operator fits each date against its own attained future and retains feasible           28

29   action witnesses. Its affine–ReLU construction and primitive error budget do not             29

30   require unrestricted nonconvex fitting to succeed. A complementary recursive                 30

1    implementation uses verified curvature and protected conventional refinement;           1

2    trained proposals change optional query locations. Residual qualification permits an    2

3    attained shallow continuation to replace recursive value queries under a stated error   3

4    account. Affine calibration separates its represented derivative from a conservative    4

5    coefficientwise penalty.                                                                5

6        Our next question is how much optimization accuracy an economic decision            6

7    needs. Rational switch barriers identify every acquired action possible throughout      7

8    a qualification sublevel. Original-law conditional contrasts price those actions,       8

9    instead of charging the full cost range on each unresolved cell. Exact thresholds       9

10   determine the entire region of tolerances supporting an ordering, cost-equivalence      10

11   or replacement conclusion. A verified primal–dual gap places a returned readout in      11

12   that region without a fitting-success model or additional sample. Labels and the        12

13   continuation policy remain fixed.                                                       13

14       The prospective investment experiment compares both bases, both qualification       14

15   rules and both observation resolutions. Calibrated qualification improves both          15

16   ReLU and Bernstein controllers and reverses their earlier matched cost ordering. 16
17   Independent arithmetic identifies original-law gains; actionwise transfer preserves     17

18   some comparisons at nonzero optimization errors without identical actions. The          18

19   full catalogue retains adverse and unresolved cases. Conditional economic choices       19

20   require separate charges and deployment counts, not an invented conversion of           20

21   runtime or simulation rows into welfare.                                                21

23       Relation to existing methods. The construction draws on Lipschitz extensions        23

24   and distance transforms (McShane, 1934, Felzenszwalb and Huttenlocher, 2012). 24
25   Approximate policy iteration, safe improvement and convex dynamic programming           25

26   have established analyses (Munos, 2003, Pirotta et al., 2013, Scherrer et al., 2015,    26

27   Yang, 2020); learned warm starts and optimization-based control provide related         27

28   numerical methods (Sakaue and Oki, 2023, Sambharya, Hall, Amos, and Stellato,           28

29   2023, Agrawal, Barratt, Boyd, and Stellato, 2020). A common verifier does not           29

30   establish the incremental value of its neural proposal.                                 30

31       Control variates, telescoping evaluation and time-uniform concentration are         31

32   established tools (Munos, 2006, Jiang and Li, 2016, Howard, Ramdas, McAuliffe,          32
                                 NEURAL BELLMAN OPERATORS                                3

1    and Sekhon, 2020, Karampatziakis, Mineiro, and Ramdas, 2021), as are minimax- 1
2    regret decisions and shortest-path difference constraints (Manski, 2004, Floyd, 1962). 2
3    The new link is from exact qualification barriers and original-law action effects to     3

4    economic stopping regions for a constructed continuation, not priority for these         4

5    underlying principles.                                                                   5

6      The same readout coefficients determine actions in every observation cell. Sepa- 6
7    rate switch tests can therefore admit an alternative that never wins its full menu, or   7

8    combinations of alternatives that no common readout can select. Section 10 retains       8

9    both restrictions. An exact support dual certifies each winner event and selected        9

10   joint events; a second, cell-assignment dual transfers the resulting compatibility       10

11   information to original-law cost. This strengthens the existing NBO optimization- 11
12   to-policy account without changing a fitted label, future controller or economic         12

13   primitive.                                                                               13

14     The additional calculation is deterministic qualification of previously purchased      14

15   evidence. It reports the incremental work of full-menu and shared-readout checks         15

16   separately from construction, integration and policy-cost inference. The economic        16

17   enclosure remains valid when a numerical solver is inconclusive, because only            17

18   independently verified multipliers may exclude an event. Standard convex duality         18

19   supplies the lower-bound principle (Boyd and Vandenberghe, 2004); the contribu- 19
20   tion here is its connection to acquired NBO actions, common coefficients and the         20

21   paid attained-policy cost event.                                                         21
