9                      1.2. M1 / B1: What belongs to the NBO construction?                 9

10                                                                                         10
         The paper separates the constructive operator, the representation-neutral safe-
11                                                                                         11
     guard and the qualification of the attained continuation. Correctness of the safe-
12                                                                                         12
     guard is not made artificially neural. The NBO nevertheless remains a substantive
13                                                                                         13
     constructed object: its own-future continuation, feasible witnesses, affine–ReLU
14                                                                                         14
     realization and primitive error account determine an implementable policy. The
15                                                                                         15
     latest calculations concern the policy cost of classes of approximately optimized
16                                                                                         16
     attained continuations, not merely the location of an optional extra Bellman probe.
17                                                                                         17
         The new winner constraints use the actual feature representation and one shared
18                                                                                         18
     readout. They can be applied to both ReLU and conventional Bernstein contin-
19                                                                                         19
     uations, which is necessary for a meaningful comparison. A stronger common
20                                                                                         20
     theorem is not presented as evidence of exclusive neural superiority. The original
21                                                                                         21
     title and model are retained because the result directly strengthens the economic
22                                                                                         22
     qualification of the original construction.

                     1.4. M3 / B3: Predictable validation and post-selection
17                                                                                             17

18     The predictable-evaluation and protected-query theorems remain in their original        18

19   sections with their filtration, context-law and validation requirements. They are         19

20   not replaced by an assumption that recursively produced contexts are independent. 20
21   The intervening qualification and readout experiments also remain in the compiled         21

22   record.                                                                                   22

23     The new theorem addresses a different selection issue. Conditional on the orig- 23
24   inal simultaneous attained-policy cost event, every valid winner, joint-event and         24

25   assignment inequality is deterministic. The resulting comparison therefore holds for      25

26   every readout in the stated objective sublevel and for all allowances simultaneously. 26
27   Inspecting these valid bounds does not require a union bound over an uncountable          27

28   coefficient class or a new independent cost sample. Changing the labels, future           28

29   actor, initial law or estimand is outside that implication unless separately qualified. 29
30     This establishes an optimization-to-decision guarantee for outputs of the declared      30

31   fixed-feature problem. It does not substitute that guarantee for prospective work- 31
32   loss validation of a newly fitted predictor under a different context distribution.       32

                          1.9. M8 / B8: What is guaranteed about fitting?
21                                                                                            21

22       Verifier return, numerical fitting performance and population reliability remain     22

23   separate objects. All historical fitting warnings and seed-level observations are        23

24   retained. The current study adds zero fitting attempts and makes no claim about          24

25   unrestricted hidden-layer optimization success.                                          25

26       The new deterministic implication is stronger than an empirical success-frequency    26

27   assertion for the declared readout problem. If a feasible output has primal value        27

28   F(theta), an independently valid dual value d no larger than the optimum, and            28

29   primal–dual gap at most epsilon, then it belongs to the covered objective class. 29
30   The classwide policy-cost interval and any supported economic decision apply to          30

31   that output regardless of which admissible coefficient vector the fitting algorithm      31

32   returned. The shared-readout restrictions improve the decision by ruling out im- 32
