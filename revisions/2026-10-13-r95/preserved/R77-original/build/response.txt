             1.6. M5 / B4. Target precision and economically interpretable comparisons
15                                                                                             15

16       The completed R70 centered-cost study already identifies equivalence for twelve       16

17   contrasts at its declared margin while identifying no strict ordering; the earlier wide   17

18   R67 intervals remain preserved. R73 then constructed actual qualified continuations       18

19   but added no independent cost rows. R77 directly evaluates these constructed              19

20   controllers rather than borrowing intervals from the different callable recursive         20

21   policies.                                                                                 21

22       The new protocol fixes 524,288 rows, forty-bit original-law bins, a full-width        22

23   target of 1/256 and a separate two-sided equivalence margin of 1/256. All five            23

24   marginal costs and all ten paired differences are reported for each of two estimators. 24
25   The joint family contains 30 estimands, not 30 independent datasets. With log             25

26   allowance ten, the endpoint union bound is below one percent. Every interval is           26

27   recomputed from exact integer sums and sums of squares with upward rational               27

28   square roots.                                                                             28

29       The width identity separates the mean retained numerical enclosure, the two           29

30   empirical-variance terms and the finite-support penalty. Acquisition ambiguity            30

31   and arithmetic expansion are included in the numerical term and accompanied by            31

32   diagnostic counts; we do not pretend that their interacting effects are separately        32

1    additive. The complete table reports all failed and passed width targets. Precision   1

2    is not equivalence: an interval can be narrow and identify a difference larger than   2

3    the chosen margin. Neither midpoint estimates nor subtraction of two marginal         3

4                             1.10. M9 / B9. The economic choice                              4

5                                                                                             5
       The evaluated object is a feasible investment controller constructed from an
6                                                                                             6
     attained continuation fit. The installed zero-investment controller gives an explicit
7                                                                                             7
     replacement reference within the same economy. It is not used to replace the stronger
8                                                                                             8
     conventional query baselines or to manufacture a specifically neural dominance
9                                                                                             9
     claim. Matched ReLU-versus-Bernstein comparisons and gains against the installed
10                                                                                            10
     reference are reported separately.
11                                                                                            11
       The new priced-selection corollary holds on the same simultaneous event for
12                                                                                            12
     every assignment of nonnegative resource charges. A controller minimizing the
13                                                                                            13
     upper qualified cost plus its charge is compared with every alternative using that
14                                                                                            14
     alternative’s interval width. For a replacement gain in [l,u], a declared deployment
15                                                                                            15
     count m, monetary scale v, qualification cost q and replacement fee f give net benefit
16                                                                                            16
     in [mvl-q-f,mvu-q-f]. Positive lower gain produces an entire certified deployment
17                                                                                            17
     and fee region; unresolved gain remains unresolved rather than being converted
18                                                                                            18
     into a purchase recommendation by choosing a favorable fee.
19                                                                                            19
       The loss units are normalized model units. We do not estimate monetary in-
20                                                                                            20
     stallation costs, infer currency from processor seconds, or present an uncalibrated
21                                                                                            21
     model comparison as an empirical welfare estimate. The result makes the economic
22                                                                                            22
     decision conditional on explicit prices and usage, while the new observations eval-
23                                                                                            23
     uate actual complete-policy gains. This is a substantive extension of the original
24                                                                                            24
     construction-to-policy-cost chain, not a substitution of certificate sharpness for
25                                                                                            25
     policy performance.
