\begin{abstract}
This supplement retains optimal-advantage centering and every preceding complete proof, including constructive qualification, original investment actors and original-law inference. It adds proofs of complete-policy occupation transfer, nonlinear recursive comparison, strategic deviation certificates, joint-domain neural construction, adaptive acquisition coverage, predictor work bounds, dated accuracy allocation, prospective menu regret and uniform price-fee sensitivity. Counterexamples isolate incorrect reference-policy weighting and unattainable shared-readout combinations. Historical numerical evidence remains in the Numerical and Development Appendix.
\end{abstract}
