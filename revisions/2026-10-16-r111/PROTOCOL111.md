## Six complete services

C0: compiled original-law completed service, with paid conventional repair, the inherited integration ladder through 4096, and rational fallback. C1: C0 plus three strict completed-incumbent support trials of fractions 1,1/2,1/4, each accepted only when its upper cost is below the incumbent lower cost and both original targets hold. C2: the same trials with the R110 remaining-gain stop at theta=2^-16 before each trial. These are newly attributed R111 implementations/reproductions, not old timing observations.

P1: the complete C1 trace followed by a paid-information comparison of previously queried actions. P2: the complete C1 trace followed by that comparison and new strong-convex secant actions. P3: the complete C2 trace followed by the same secant construction. The control trace is completed before postprocessing, so a postprocessor cannot change its own comparator or remove its rejected work. Corresponding controls must have identical pre-postprocessing action and certificate traces; mismatches abort and remain recorded.

Every original-law certificate retains its already-computed feasible risk witness intercept, slope interval, anchor, optimal-future value upper bound, actual-policy bounds and original optimum bound. A support query retains its witness but does not obtain a policy upper bound for free. For a scalar action interval [0,A], reduce a slope interval to its exact rational midpoint and subtract radius times max(anchor,A-anchor) from the intercept. This produces a valid curvature-two quadratic on the entire original action set. Maxima of these minorants can be minimized exactly by enumerating endpoints, line intersections and active quadratic vertices. Existing valid optimum bounds are retained as additional bounds.

Subtract a^2 from every queried optimal-future upper value and form the lower convex hull of those points. Adding a^2 back gives an upper enclosure of the optimal-future objective on the observed action hull. Add beta*2^-20 to enclose the unchanged implemented future policy. Every hull segment's quadratic minimizer, rounded downward to a feasible 30-bit action within that segment, is a candidate. Previously queried actions are also candidates. No unobserved action receives a free true objective or gradient. Qualification and strict comparison use rational arithmetic and outward converted endpoints. Retain the completed control unless a candidate passes both unchanged targets and its policy upper bound is strictly below the best valid lower bound at the completed control action. Among admissible candidates choose smallest upper bound, then smallest action. Every rejected constructed candidate and all rational-envelope work are charged and recorded. Exact same-action identity gives zero contrast. A smaller regret upper bound alone is never a policy improvement.
