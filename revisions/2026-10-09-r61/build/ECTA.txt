11       The trained backend supplies a second, linked route. Learned nonlinear features      11

12   propose actions, while verification determines which proposals can be deployed. 12
13   In the investment model, uniform-innovation integration turns a one-hidden-layer         13

14   ReLU continuation into a rational piecewise-polynomial action objective. Exact           14

15   fitted minimization is therefore a finite calculation rather than a second unconverged   15

16   training problem. The fitted-witness transfer theorem separates center optimization      16

17   error from acquisition, continuation, continuous-action covering and numerical           17

18   verification errors. An exact witness removes the first of these errors, not all of them. 18
19       We develop two exact implementations of that witness. Lossless interval screening    19

20   excludes only lattice actions that cannot attain the minimum, and exact comparison       20

21   preserves the original smallest-index tie rule. Conditional transcript invariance then   21

22   preserves the acquired policy, its certificates and a prospective controller’s stop- 22
23   ping decisions. The additional finite-difference construction in Section 10 avoids       23

24   a favorable-separation premise. On each nonnegative scalar-action piece, the ob- 24
25   jective is a quartic with a nonnegative fourth-order coefficient. Its second forward     25

26   difference is nondecreasing. A concave prefix and a convex tail can consequently be      26

27   searched by rational sign comparisons, including flat objectives, adjacent ties and      27

28   signed cancellation. No isolated real root or unique surviving action is required.       28

29       The action dimension and the state dimension play different roles. The scalar        29

30   finite-difference result does not become a multivariate theorem by increasing the        30

31   number of state coordinates. We therefore give a separate exact integration formula      31
