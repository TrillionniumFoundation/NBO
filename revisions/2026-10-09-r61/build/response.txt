           1.2. B1. Separate the generic exclusion rule from the model-specific contribution
9                                                                                               9

10       The revision explicitly separates interval exclusion, exact activation reduction,      10

11   the integrated objective’s discrete curvature and the prospective policy contract. 11
12   We do not claim the generic idea of discarding a candidate whose valid lower               12

13   bound exceeds an incumbent upper bound. That principle is used to implement the            13

14   original mathematical witness, including its smallest-index tie rule.                      14

15       The new root-free theorem exploits the integrated NBO objective directly. On           15

16   a scalar activation piece, the polynomial is d0 + d1 a + d2 a2 + c4 a4 with c4 ≥ 0. Its    16

17   second forward difference on a nonnegative lattice is nondecreasing, even if d2 < 0. 17
18   Exact rational sign comparisons locate the concave prefix and convex tail. At most         18

19   four retained indices per nonempty piece supply the original smallest minimizer. 19
20   The theorem therefore does not require one screening survivor, a unique optimum,           20

21   a favorable growth constant or a root-isolation subroutine. A signed or flat fitted        21

22   continuation is admitted under the stated polynomial structure.                            22

23       The arithmetic-operation count, stored rational objects and bit-length dependence      23

24   are given separately. The implementation is an exact C++ rational engine, not a            24

25   floating root finder. The corresponding lossless replacement preserves the prior           25

26   policy and stopping transcript under the same mathematical controller, while the           26

27   fitted-witness transfer theorem retains the remaining acquisition, continuation,           27

28   continuous-action and verification errors.                                                 28

29       The multi-action extension has its own theorem rather than borrowing the scalar        29

30   result. A finite mixture of independent box-uniform shocks has an exact signed- 30
31   vertex ReLU expectation formula. Rational subdivision on a polytope-constrained            31

32   action lattice preserves the original lexicographic optimum and terminates with an         32
                                    RESPONSE TO THE REFEREE                             3

1    exact fallback when a declared prefix budget is exhausted. The explicit 2q shock- 1
2    integration terms and worst-case product action-lattice size remain in its cost ac- 2
3    count. Sixteen fitted two-control queries per worker execute shared capacity and        3

4    one or two shocks. This is a substantive non-scalar exact-witness extension, distinct   4

5    from a complete multivariate-control welfare experiment.                                5

                1.4. B3. Isolate reduction, screening, language and algorithm effects
28                                                                                           28

29     The eight-method experiment includes unreduced and reduced symbolic search,           29

30   vector enclosure with exhaustive exact comparison, unreduced and reduced screens,       30

31   native exhaustive evaluation, native finite-difference search, and adaptive subdivi- 31
32   sion. These comparisons expose useful mechanisms, but source inspection identified      32
     4

1    a remaining confound: the original native exhaustive mode was unreduced, whereas          1

2    the native piecewise mode first reduced activation regions.                               2

3        R61 therefore adds a new, prespecified native two-by-two experiment. Its four         3

4    cells cross reduction off/on with exhaustive/finite-difference search. Every cell         4

5    uses the same C++ arbitrary-size rational engine, original objective, lattice, smallest- 5
6    index rule and exact original-value postcheck. Reduction work is included wherever        6

7    reduction is performed. The ninety original rational fixtures and seven-repeat subset     7

8    are fixed before the fresh timing observations; no favorable instance is selected using   8

9    those observations. Two newly allocated worker jobs execute all cells. Batch sizes        9

10   one, eight and thirty-two are also evaluated for the same complete ninety-query           10

11   catalogue.                                                                                11

12       This comparison identifies the search effect at a common reduced representation. 12
13   At lattice cardinality 513, the median recorded saving is approximately 61–73 per- 13
14   cent across the width/worker groups, and every instance in those groups is faster. 14
15   At width thirty-two and lattice cardinality thirty-three, the medians are approx- 15
16   imately minus eighteen and minus nineteen percent: native exhaustive search is            16

17   faster. The revision therefore reports the actual crossover in the tested catalogue       17

18   instead of claiming that the piecewise method always wins. All four repeated-query        18

19   medians, full ranges, random orders, batch bytes and exact answer arrays remain           19

20   available. The audit independently verifies 3,160 returned exact answers; that count      20

21   is not an economic sample size.                                                           21

28   accuracy comparison. Whole-state lower intervals cover all continuous feasible             28

29   actions. Upper candidates are feasible throughout their acquired cells. The original       29

30   continuous innovation laws are enclosed rather than replaced by midpoint transi- 30

32   final result.                                                                              32

2    the implemented-policy upper excess relative to the same zero-policy continuation. 2
3    Their difference cancels that reference at the same state. The centered ladder attains   3

              1.10. B9. Economic interpretation, replacement fees and numerical units
25                                                                                            25

26       The numerical units remain those of the original normalized investment model. 26
27   No installation-price estimate, electricity tariff, processor-frequency estimate or      27

28   external welfare calibration is inferred from hosted execution time. Instead, the        28

29   manuscript gives the full symbolic decision account in those units.                      29

30       For identical returned policies, an implementation change affects computation        30

31   cost. At work price λ, setup difference k1 − k0 , per-use work difference w1 − w0 ,      31

32   use count H and additional implementation fee I, the new-minus-old total cost is         32
                                     RESPONSE TO THE REFEREE                              9

1    λ[ H (w1 − w0 ) + (k1 − k0 )] + I. This yields the entire break-even use-count account    1

2    whenever a measured per-use saving exists, while a negative saving remains a              2

3    negative result.                                                                          3

4      For different policies, a gain interval [l, u], replacement fee τ and incremental       4

5    work charge λ∆w give net benefit [l − τ − λ∆w, u − τ − λ∆w] on the same coverage          5

6    event. The cases of supported replacement, ruled-out benefit and unresolved benefit       6

7    are stated separately. New fixed-policy Bellman bounds supply a different accuracy        7

8    contract and have their own paid verification cost; they are not treated as a reduction   8

9    in an earlier measured stopping time.                                                     9
