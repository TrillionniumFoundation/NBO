10                           1.2. Cross-platform moment verification                         10

11                                                                                           11
         Different numerical-library versions can differ in the final rounding of a sample
12                                                                                           12
     second moment even when their endpoint arrays are byte identical. The audit
13                                                                                           13
     therefore does not overwrite a scientific interval with a newly rounded interval
14                                                                                           14
     and call it the same record. Instead, it converts each binary64 endpoint to an exact
15                                                                                           15
     rational, computes its exact sample mean, second moment and unbiased variance,
16                                                                                           16
     and checks their containment in the originally stored outward moment account. It
17                                                                                           17
     reconstructs the rational empirical-Bernstein endpoints from those stored accounts.
18                                                                                           18
     Thus a valid original interval can be verified without assuming bit identity of a
19                                                                                           19
     library reduction on another platform.
20                                                                                           20
         Validation confidence boxes receive the corresponding exact-mean check, with
21                                                                                           21
     their stored confidence radius and numerical-bin allowance. All policy-cost end-
22                                                                                           22
     points retain signed path gains. Conditional expected-cost safety does not imply
23                                                                                           23
     that a common-shock pathwise gain is nonnegative; such paths are not clipped
24                                                                                           24
     away. The declared independent-bin statistical model remains distinct from the
25                                                                                           25
     reproducibility of a pseudorandom seed.

                               1.3. Residual cache and signed tubes
28                                                                                           28

29       A range query includes every closed leaf meeting the query box. This is conser- 29
30   vative at a boundary but preserves the half-open deployed observation rule. No          30

31   smoothness is imposed on the actor. The first-stage installed policy is globally zero. 31
32   Sensitivity is used only under that verified reference identity; a changed incumbent    32
                          NEURAL BELLMAN OPERATORS: SUPPLEMENT                             3

1    falls back to the generic cache. The forward zero-policy tube contains the entire         1

2    future innovation support and the backward tube uses the sparse Jacobian of the           2

3    original drift. Its first innovation is integrated over the declared bins with exact      3

4    probabilities. The algorithm does not recursively enumerate all q T future bins.          4

5      Each raw candidate and covering comparison retains its base-cache interval,             5

6    signed-tube interval and intersection. Empty intersections are errors, not oppor- 6
7    tunities to choose a midpoint. Table entries for U − C and C − L are maxima of            7

8    independently nonnegative cellwise quantities. Their separate maxima need not             8

9    sum to the displayed maximum of U − L. Refinement attribution is a comparison             9

10   of valid enclosures, not a causal decomposition of state discretization, action search,   10

11   stochastic integration and floating-point error.                                          11

13                               1.4. Exact neural action search                               13

14
       The new scalar-action solver reads the exact stored dyadic coefficients. It enumer- 14
15                                                                                             15
     ates rational hinge breakpoints, constructs the cubic derivative of each quartic piece,
16                                                                                             16
     isolates every real stationary root by rational intervals, and compares candidate
17                                                                                             17
     lattice actions by exact rational objective values. Multiple roots and zero innovation
18                                                                                             18
     projections have explicit tests. The retained saved-model regressions specify one
19
     acquired-cell center in each task before checking its entire finite lattice exhaustively. 19
20
     They create neither a replacement production policy nor a new economic observa- 20
21                                                                                             21
     tion. Root-isolation cost and coefficient bit lengths are not replaced by a unit-cost
22                                                                                             22
     count in a runtime claim.

                              6.1. Exact search and proposal identity
28                                                                                             28

29     For each saved critic, the replay reconstructs the integrated action objective          29

30   from the exact stored dyadic coefficients. The action-dependent objective drops           30

31   only a common additive constant. It recomputes rational hinge breakpoints, cubic          31

32   stationary-root isolation and exact rational comparisons on the action lattice. It        32
     18

1    checks the chosen index, objective value, nonterminal menu optimization loss and       1

2    root/piece counts. Different valid rational isolating brackets are not by themselves   2

3    different economic actions; the selected lattice index and exact objective are the     3

4    controlling solver identities.                                                         4

5      The ReLU-exact mode keeps every ReLU-menu candidate. The quadratic mode              5

6    has its own exact fitted-value search; the other modes retain their declared menu      6

7    construction. Every terminal proposal uses the same analytic terminal objective        7
