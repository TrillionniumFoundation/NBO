                      2. NUMERICAL LABELS AND THE SEPARATED ACCOUNT
18                                                                                            18

19       An objective query has lower and upper endpoints for its midpoint-sum expec- 19
20   tation, plus the deterministic continuous-law integration remainder. Labels are          20

21   rounded on the inherited dyadic lattice, and the largest distance to the objective       21

22   endpoints supplies the query allowance et . Selecting the least rounded label and        22

23   retaining its original action costs one allowance in the label comparison and another    23

24   in the complete selected-policy account. It does not require a second copy of the        24

25   action-cover term in main equation (7).                                                  25

11                   3. WHY THE COMPILED ACTOR IS THE ORIGINAL ACTOR                               11

12                                                                                                 12

13     At a tensor node the distance transform minimizes over original sites, not merely           13

14   neighboring transformed nodes. Each forward relaxation along a coordinate line                14

15   compares the current value–owner pair with the preceding pair plus the weighted               15

16   coordinate distance; the backward pass does the same from the opposite direction. 16
17   The invariant is the lexicographic minimum over all processed original sites in the           17

18   processed coordinates. Separability proves the invariant after the final coordinate. 18
19   Keeping only the winning value would lose the actor identity.                                 19

20     For an off-grid point, choose a cell corner coordinatewise between the point and            20

21   its lexicographically first minimizing site. Distances add exactly. At that corner an         21

22   owner giving a smaller value, or a tied value and smaller original index, contradicts         22

23   the minimizer at the original point. This proves the owner identity even for incon- 23
24   sistent labels, zero slope, boundary cells and ties. Nonuniform coordinate spacings           24

25   enter only the exact distances.                                                               25

26     In interval execution, value expressions are enclosed outward. Actor candidates             26

27   whose lower objective exceeds another candidate’s upper objective can be discarded;           27

28   the remaining possibilities must be retained. An exact point tie is resolved by               28

29   rational comparison and the original index rule. A genuine uncertain state box is             29

30   not an exact point: the hull of its surviving actions is propagated. Boxes crossing           30

31   cells use the union of all relevant corners. These operations may cost more than one          31

32   point query, and the direct records retain actor and measurement ambiguity counts. 32
