22                                         1. INTRODUCTION                                      22

23     A numerical continuation matters because it helps produce an economic decision. 23
24   The error of a fitted value, the loss of the implemented policy and the resources          24

25   required to verify that loss are different objects. A policy may improve without a         25

26   tighter numerical certificate; a tighter certificate may leave the policy unchanged. A     26

27   useful computational method must connect its approximation and resource choices            27

28   to a feasible decision under the original economic law.                                    28

29     This paper develops Neural Bellman Operators (NBOs) along that chain. The                29

30   constructive implementation evaluates a continuation against its own fitted future         30

31                                                                                              31

1    and retains feasible action witnesses. Its exact affine–ReLU realization and primitive       1

2    error budget do not require a theorem that unrestricted nonconvex training succeeds. 2
3    A complementary recursive implementation queries the original Bellman problem                3

4    only where its action search requires information. A trained readout proposes                4

5    actions; verified lower and upper bounds determine whether the actual policy                 5

6    meets its original-optimum contract.                                                         6

7        The nonlinear investment economy has state-dependent capacity, bilinear tran- 7
8    sitions and one or two independent continuous innovations. Primitive curvature               8

9    controls the convexity defect of the transition and supplies regularity of the true          9

10   Bellman value. The action certificate can therefore dispense with a stored state             10

11   lattice. A strong-convexity upper chord sometimes certifies a rounded action be- 11
12   fore another endpoint query. Protected conventional refinement remains available             12

13   when a proposal fails. The certificate-aware fixed-feature readout and its optimiza- 13
14   tion gap make the resulting training problem constructive. Neither a fitted neural           14

15   continuation nor an arbitrary proposed action is assumed convex.                             15

16       Removing a state lattice does not remove the cost of evaluating a future. A first        16

17   original-value query can generate a large descendant tree. Independently evaluating          17

18   optimal advantages for precise policy-cost inference can generate another such               18

19   tree. We address these two costs separately, while retaining the original operator,          19

20   economic law and optimum.                                                                    20

21       First, a frozen fitted NBO continuation can replace the optimal value in the cost        21

22   observation. Its Bellman-residual oscillations and implemented greedy gaps control           22

23   both the observation’s range and the actual policy loss. A terminal correction makes         23

24   the identity valid even when the fitted terminal value is inexact. State-independent         24

25   continuation shifts cancel. For shallow affine–ReLU continuations, the original              25

26   one- and two-innovation conditional expectations are finite positive-part moment             26

27   calculations. An explicit state-and-action-cover construction qualifies their residuals      27

28   without querying the optimal Bellman tree. This is an approximation, integration             28

29   and certification result, not a claim that a fitted critic is already accurate because its   29

30   training loss is small.                                                                      30

31       Second, a lower original-Bellman bound and its feasible upper witness can be             31

32   transported to a nearby state. The rule pays for state displacement, capacity repair         32

1    and action rounding before deciding whether the old certificate closes at the new         1

2    tolerance. A failed check invokes the original recursive verifier. The policy guarantee   2

3    holds after history-dependent selection among valid records, without assuming             3

4    equality with the fresh-search policy. A finite-cover theorem states the tighter fill     4

5    precision and retention conditions needed to bound the number of expensive fills. 5
6    The executed bounded cache is evaluated by its actual counts, rather than by              6

7    premises it does not satisfy.                                                             7

8      These additions distinguish three uses of a neural object: a constructed continua- 8
9    tion, an optional query proposal and a proposed record for retrieval. Their economic      9

10   value is not identified by calling a common correctness argument neural. The neural       10

11   continuation now enters a computable original-law evaluation identity; the pro- 11
12   posal and retrieval mechanisms can change work but cannot manufacture a value             12

13   bound. Exact ReLU encodings and conventional piecewise-linear interpretations             13

14   remain related representations, not independent algorithms with an automatic              14

15   ranking.                                                                                  15

16     The retained frozen study executes certificate-aware ReLU and equally sized             16

17   Bernstein readouts, independent-context validation, sixty-four-block reuse, direct        17

18   continuous-law policy-cost inference and trained two-control comparisons. Its             18

19   twelve scalar cost contrasts meet the prespecified equivalence margin, but none           19

20   identifies strict superiority. Its complete cold-start evidence does not support a        20

21   uniform neural work advantage. The new predeclared experiment compares fresh              21

22   calculation, exact-key reuse and geometrically transported witnesses on the same          22

23   localized query workloads, including a four-date, two-control stress case. It counts      23

24   every original-law descendant on a miss and every rejected lookup, invalidation           24

25   and durable record. The workload is not relabeled as independent policy-cost data. 25

22   controller.                                                                              22

27   and the explicit residual-cover account state what must be purchased to replace          27

28   optimal-value centering by a fitted continuation. The witness-transfer theorem           28

29   likewise supplements ordinary memoization with a capacity-feasible upper witness         29

30   and a lower bound on the original continuous-action infimum.                             30

                                        12. CONCLUSION
27                                                                                              27

28     Neural Bellman Operators connect the construction of a continuation to feasible          28

29   decisions under the original economic law. The witness-producing implementation            29

30   has an explicit own-future error account. Recursive action verification permits            30

31   trained proposals without allowing fitting error to alter the policy certificate. The      31

32   present additions place a frozen neural continuation directly in original-law policy- 32
     40

1    cost evaluation and permit purchased original-value witnesses to be reused after a        1

2    verified capacity repair.                                                                 2

3      The resulting resource distinctions are substantive. Exact conditional moments          3

4    remove optimal-value descendants from the evaluation of a qualified shallow               4

5    continuation, but fitting and uniform residual qualification remain paid operations. 5
6    Transported witnesses remove descendants on a certified cache hit, but the first fill     6

7    and every later miss retain their original horizon cost. A history-dependent reuse        7

8    controller retains a loss guarantee; it is not automatically the same policy used in an   8

9    earlier independent cost experiment.                                                      9

10     The retained cost-inference evidence identifies equivalence at a declared numeri- 10
11   cal margin, not strict superiority. The new query experiment tests localized reuse,       11

12   not universal neural speed or calibrated welfare. Together with the constructive          12

13   theorems, these results identify what can be certified, reused and economically           13

14   compared, and what resources each step requires. They extend the original NBO             14

15   policy-evaluation and improvement objective while preserving every historical             15

16   application, assumption and unfavorable comparison for renewed scrutiny. The              16

17   qualification-optimal readout gives a direct link from an attained fitting objective      17

18   to a uniform NBO cost and regret certificate. Its exact dual distinguishes verified       18

19   optimization accuracy from a solver’s status. Predictable original-law cost intervals     19

20   allow qualification to stop when a declared economic criterion is met, while keep- 20
21   ing fixed-policy and adaptive-deployment targets separate. Both additions use the         21

22   original investment primitives and pay for approximation, uniform qualification           22

23   and numerical evaluation; neither infers a population ranking from a better training      23

24   objective.                                                                                24

25     The final link is the cost of the actual constructed controller. Original-law cash      25

26   observations and exact conditional integration refer to the same frozen acquired          26

27   actors, rather than to a replacement search policy. Their simultaneous intervals          27

28   distinguish a precision target, equivalence at a declared loss band and strict im- 28
29   provement. Priced selection and replacement use those intervals on one coverage           29

30   event for all nonnegative resource prices. These are decisions within the original        30

31   NBO economy; they neither infer a universal neural ranking nor assign an empirical        31

32   monetary calibration to normalized losses. The complete interval and work records         32
                                          NEURAL BELLMAN OPERATORS                                            41

1    allow the attained continuation fit and its economic consequences to be assessed                                1

2    together.                                                                                                       2
