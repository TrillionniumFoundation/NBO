15     The same readout coefficients determine actions in every observation cell. Sepa- 15
16   rate switch tests can therefore admit an alternative that never wins its full menu, or   16

17   combinations of alternatives that no common readout can select. Section 10 retains       17

19   joint events; a second, cell-assignment dual transfers the resulting compatibility       19

20   information to original-law cost. This strengthens the existing NBO optimization- 20
21   to-policy account without changing a fitted label, future controller or economic         21

22   primitive.                                                                               22

24   evidence. It reports the incremental work of full-menu and shared-readout checks         24

25   separately from construction, integration and policy-cost inference. The economic        25

26   enclosure remains valid when a numerical solver is inconclusive, because only            26

27   independently verified multipliers may exclude an event. Standard convex duality         27

28   supplies the lower-bound principle (Boyd and Vandenberghe, 2004); the contribu- 28
