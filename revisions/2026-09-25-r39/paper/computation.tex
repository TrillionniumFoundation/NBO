\section{What is computed and independently checked}
\subsection{Finite common-policy searches}
Each proof file contains the rational economic model, exact operating value, common action probabilities, operating and revision values, all searched boxes, contraction results, split coordinates and cuts, terminal leaf classifications, and the final interval. The LP version also saves every accepted nonnegative rational multiplier vector and its residual bound. A floating solver can propose a multiplier or a candidate, but cannot certify feasibility, remove a box, or establish the lower endpoint on its own. Solver failures retain the interval Bellman bound.

The independent SymPy verifier imports neither constructor, policy repair, nor SciPy. It checks the model's reward/cost contract, transition stochasticity, the exact operating Bellman equation, every all-restart candidate inequality, and the initial-law cost. It reconstructs contractions and McCormick residual rows, verifies both child boxes and complete tree reachability, checks cost pruning against the final incumbent, and rebuilds the global minimum over leaves. All 16 complete finite trees are checked, not only a sample of successful cases.

\subsection{Whole-domain continuous-state objects}
Each of the 42 primary randomized files retains the original incumbent and operating values, the new common operating continuation, numerator and denominator functions for mixing, and directed dyadic cost grids. The operating checker covers all open pieces and isolated points. The independent cost calculation uses positive affine transition maps and one of the three stated absolutely continuous initial laws; it does not extend the almost-everywhere cost guarantee to a Dirac initial law.

The connected local lower file contains full support floors and every directed local grid. Independent checks reconstruct support supersolution inequalities and all feasible local pure and two-action vertices. Twenty-one horizon/incumbent support families are reused across the two tolerances, but each primary policy and local result is separately checked. Final endpoints can additionally use valid inherited bounds. In the horizon-8 neural31002, uniform-law, allowance-$1/20$ row, a retained R34 randomized endpoint exceeds the freshly checked local/support lower value. Its inherited provenance is specifically listed in \path{results/publication_audit.json}; it is not claimed to have received a fresh R39 independent derivation. Deterministic strict-separation comparisons similarly retain the source R38 certificate identity rather than inventing a new proof of an old bound.

\subsection{Nonlinear objects}
Twenty paired-model mesh/horizon constructions at allowance $1/2$ and four tighter-tolerance lower-only diagnostics use directed integer arrays at scale $2^{34}$. Global support supersolutions, operating lower/upper arrays, support floors, and connected restart bounds are stored. Array multiplication includes signed-integer overflow checks before a result is accepted. The upper policies remain the retained R38 candidates; no new upper-portfolio search is charged to this lower-bound-only improvement.

Full independent arithmetic covers exactly $(T,N)=(8,64)$ and $(32,64)$. That verifier uses SymPy rational corner geometry, explicitly split threshold regions, and Python arbitrary-precision integer scalar induction. NumPy only reads the archive arrays. It checks all global-support action inequalities and separately reconstructs candidate operating and cost enclosures. The finer mesh rows remain directed-construction results with structural and hash checks; they do not inherit an independent label from the two checked objects.

\section{Resource and termination accounting}
The finite search allowance is 4,096 evaluated nodes and 60 seconds per solver/case, with requested absolute gap $1/1000$. Binary expansion adds two children, so an exhausted allowance can leave 4,095 evaluated nodes. Time is checked between iterations, not by interrupting the production of a node certificate. Model construction and operating solve appear in the end-to-end time. LP startup, Python imports, proof serialization, independent verification, and document rendering are separate stages visible in the logs; no single solver time is called the cost of the entire publication.

The main finite table and supplemental work table use every run, including zero-cost cases, positive-gap termination, and remaining intervals. An absolute gap below $0.001$ can still be a material percentage of a very small cost. Both scales are reported. Peak resident memory includes Python and library overhead. The nonlinear payload $8N^2(T+1)(11+4)$ bytes counts stored arrays before temporary buffers and differs from process RSS. The nonlinear 60-second allowance is not borrowed from the finite protocol: these are separate declared experiments with separate resource records.

The primary cohort is a development calculation with a fixed 1,024-cell base partition plus exact knots. No out-of-sample claim is made for its methods or incumbent selection. The local cost enclosure has nonzero width; exact operating values do not make its randomized cost exact. The full-horizon local lower bound need not be exact either. The manuscript therefore reports intervals rather than converting additional decimal places into an optimality claim.

\section{Adversarial tests and the trusted boundary}
Nine finite-proof corruptions test policy probability, policy class, initial law, terminal value, revision stage cost, lower bound, dual sign, and root coverage. The seeded partition test adds 200 random discontinuous piecewise-affine pairs, 15,112 exact value comparisons in the first execution, and 400 malformed-partition rejections. Its seed is 390723. These tests expose specific bugs and boundary mistakes but do not estimate an all-input false-acceptance rate.

The mathematical specification remains a trusted boundary: an independently programmed arithmetic check can still share an incorrectly stated economic assumption. The paper-to-code contracts therefore duplicate primitive formulas and test their consequences, while the article proves why the constrained formulation is the relevant economic object. The reset approximation assumptions are not declared machine-checked for the atomic models. Package hashes certify byte identities, not theorem validity. Publication checks ensure that displayed numbers match checked data, not that every proposed application satisfies the theorem.
