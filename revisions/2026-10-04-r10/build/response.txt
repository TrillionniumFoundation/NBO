11     The title, economic topic, and original applications remain unchanged. The com-          11

12   plete R9 article and supplement are archived exactly; all inherited code, data,            12

13   unsuccessful runs, and earlier review materials remain unchanged on the new                13

14   branch. The root article is integrated rather than replaced by a detached research         14

15   protocol. The new material is a continuous-time policy-performance theorem for             15

16   the original dense nonlinear capital economy, its executable arithmetic verification,      16

17   and a complete neural and direct-method study through dimension fifty.                     17

18     The theorem compares each certified policy with all original admissible adapted          18

19   controls in the primitive box, not only with policies in a neural class. A schedule-       19

20   centred output map limits the neural approximation class and allows the perfor-            20

21   mance bound to be computed from utility curvature, the verified coupling norm,             21

22   and scalar quadrature. The comparison economy is not restricted to that output             22

23   tube. The feasible schedule remains an explicit, cheaper benchmark with a tighter          23

24   bound. This distinction prevents an architectural guarantee from being presented           24

25   as evidence that neural training itself created the guarantee.                             25

10       The source branch pins the entire program before remote execution. Its work-         10

11   flow reruns the new algebra, interval, gradient-routing, model-identity, and de-         11

12   ployment tests; replays the inherited test suites without changing historical evi-       12

13   dence; computes every declared certificate and fit; audits raw pathwise arrays and       13

14   weight hashes; generates the tables; and compiles the article, supplement, and this      14

15   response. Only the complete execution is committed as the evidence and referee           15

16   copy. Failed commands are retained in logs and do not become successful evidence         16

17   by omission.                                                                             17

18       The distinctions remaining after this addition are scientific rather than hidden     18

19   accounting conventions: a structural policy guarantee is not a training conver-          19

20   gence rate; a continuous-capital theorem is not a continuous-stopping theorem for        20

21   endogenous preferences; and a trained actor must earn any claimed advantage              21

22   over the feasible schedule in the measured comparison. The revised paper states          22

23   its positive results at those precise levels while preserving the original research      23

24   programme.                                                                               24
