\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
The objective of neural Bellman operators is an accurate state-contingent solution of the original economic decision problem. The method developed here makes the connection between training and that objective explicit. A complete-cover signed residual envelope guides neural proposals, independent directed arithmetic determines acceptance, and a quantitative envelope theorem states what the accepted object certifies. Stopping accessibility corrects a genuine inconsistency of all-face hard-settlement critics without changing the economy.

The revised evidence separates numerical progress from statements that the data cannot support. The complete-cover objective produces decreasing raw certificates at all prescribed budgets in ten original-economy trajectories, reversing the earlier sampled-objective anti-alignment. Matched boundary ablations, unrestricted classical candidates, method-specific witnesses, and a computable fixed-witness floor identify the remaining obstruction. The original $.01$ neural target is still not met, and monotone certificate descent is not presented as monotone true policy improvement.

The nonlinear multi-product application supplies a different, positive accuracy--work result: a full-state neural initialization with a provably contracting correction attains a state-uniform total-cost bound below $1.037\times10^{-6}$ at 128 coordinates. Strong classical alternatives remain competitive under the same certificates. A separately identified original-economy primal/dual library admits explicit budget-financed wealth compensation. These results preserve the neural-solver objective while making every theorem, numerical claim, comparison, and welfare interpretation refer to the exact object for which it is established.
