Every numerical bound is attached to a stored mathematical object. The original neural policies use the accessibility-preserving architecture in the main paper. \subsection{Original-economy training and complete certification}
Seeds 16100--16109 and both checkpoints are retained. Widths alternate between 16 and 32; both have two hidden tanh layers. Every actor update follows three critic residual updates. Critic and actor learning rates are $.0005$ and $.0002$, with 128 samples, double precision, and one Torch thread. The random sampling mixture includes a 25 percent boundary-layer component. The optimizer is a proposal procedure and has no certified convergence claim. The main paper reports the complete twenty-checkpoint table, including all target misses.

The MPFR certifier evaluates every interior box. There are no skipped or failed cells in the retained valid records. The four state boundaries and terminal trace are handled by the algebraic identities and inaccessibility proof, not by omitting an inconvenient face. At upper wealth the witness dominates settlement for the original-control upper comparison. Only the candidate policy's probability of hitting that face is zero. For a head perturbation, consumption and preference remain clipped to their bounds and the portfolio perturbation precedes the wealth-distance multiplication. An additive portfolio error after that multiplication is outside the certified contract.

A neural-jet diagnostic compares the independently coded value, time derivative, state gradient, and three state Hessian components with automatic differentiation at 24 test states, and checks 72 actor components. It is an implementation diagnostic; the whole-cell MPFR enclosures and the analytic boundary proof establish the certificate. The JSON records retain, separately, positive optimal residuals, negative policy residuals, unsigned evaluation bounds, action-gap allowances, and time-slab integration. They do not identify the unknown true residual maximum or the exact amount of interval wrapping.

\subsection{Two same-model state-space methods}
The controlled Markov-chain generator uses upwind drift and two wide-jump diffusion directions with positive weights. The correlation enters those directions, and the stay probability is checked. The semi-Lagrangian generator uses a four-point weak Euler shock rule and bilinear state interpolation. Both apply the original stopping payoff when an approximate jump first leaves the domain, using the straight segment to locate that crossing. Neither treats this discrete boundary approximation as an exact continuous-time stopping simulation.

The three refinements use 9, 17, and 25 state nodes per coordinate and 64, 128, and 256 time steps. Their consumption, preference, and portfolio-head counts are $(3,3,4)$, $(5,5,6)$, and $(7,7,8)$. Bounded bilinear state heads, held piecewise constant in time, define the transferred policies. These policies use the same invariant portfolio-distance factor as the neural class. The separate original-economy verifier encloses every interpolation cell and time-piece range against the continuous control maximum. Its common critic is fixed by the protocol, not selected after seeing the baseline outcomes. Discrete central values are stored as diagnostics and are never used as verified policy values.

The two implementations provide distinct state-space candidate generators. They are not claimed to be optimally tuned implementations or a complete comparison against the strongest possible classical approaches. The common critic is a limitation of the current matched verification exercise; all six resulting bounds miss $.01$. The paper therefore reports generation and verification costs without claiming a matched-accuracy winner.

\subsection{Established arithmetic and its remaining trusted components}
The R16 arithmetic kernel calls MPFR with 128-bit precision. Lower and upper endpoints use round-toward-minus-infinity and round-toward-plus-infinity for basic operations and elementary functions, including conversion to binary64. Scalar products and reductions propagate intervals instead of treating round-to-nearest BLAS output as exact. Rational constants are enclosed before use. The library rejects invalid logarithms, square roots, nonfinite endpoints, and division through zero. The remote build uses the installed MPFR headers; the source also contains a documented LP64 ABI fallback for the local development environment.

The complete eighteen-node audit retains every primal value interval, upper dual bound, expenditure interval, source-box count, and exact envelope breakpoint. Its C arithmetic and elementary functions differ materially from the retained rational--Taylor kernel. Its analytic conjugate decomposition, rational Gauss roots, derivative remainder argument, stopping corrections, and price-envelope mathematics are shared. Agreement therefore reduces arithmetic uncertainty but does not independently prove those mathematical premises. The 450 rational arithmetic checks, the neural automatic-differentiation comparisons, and the historical high-precision point comparisons are diagnostics, not formal interval proofs.

\subsection{Fresh model-to-price-envelope reproduction}
The fresh calculation initializes only the two endpoint prices. At every generated node, deterministic initializations produce a new feasible 16-slab policy and dual pilot; exact-decimal model constants are unchanged. The full original stopped payoff and adapted-control upper comparison are re-evaluated. The next node is the worst current exact envelope breakpoint rounded to spacing $1/1024$; a duplicate invokes the widest-interval midpoint. The declared termination rule is a proved uniform gap below $.01$ or thirty generated nodes. All intermediate node additions, optimizer statuses, costs, and certificates are retained, not only the final successful library.

The canonical result has \RFreshNodes\ newly generated nodes. It uses the rational--Taylor arithmetic inherited as an algorithm, not historical policy data or preselected dual pilots. Neither price-node refinement nor a denser quadrature eliminates the current dual relaxation's positive allowances; the retained 16/32/64-slab experiment records the corresponding floor.
