\section{The objective audit and numerical discrepancy}\label{supp:r18objective}
The new checker rejects any missing, duplicated, reordered, or unaccounted-for cell and any mismatch between a network and the hash in its certificate. It uses the immutable MPFR cell endpoints as inputs to the objective-bridge check, rather than claiming to regenerate all 60 interval certificates. The two designated full-cylinder replays are identified separately. For every object, its proposal and certified logits are retained in a compressed numerical array with its own hash.

The smooth-maximum computation subtracts the largest stored logit before exponentiation and includes one exact zero logit. Its temperature is the exact rational $3/20$. Directed discounted masses and pairwise reductions enclose every summed quantity. The correction in Theorem~\ref{main-thm:objectivebridge} is computed against the clipped proposal logits because both maxima already include zero. This avoids assigning a spurious discrepancy to a negative residual whose positive part is exactly zero. The ordinary proposal graph is still not itself a proof of enclosure.

Across all objects, the integrated correction ranges from approximately $1.11149\times10^{-14}$ to $1.19078\times10^{-13}$. The worst-case soft-maximum allowance is at most $1.631868$. All 60 directed reconstructions satisfy the envelope implication. This is a check of the sufficient inequality, not proof of a global minimum of the nonconvex training objective. The current witness floors are orders of magnitude larger than the numerical correction.

At the upper wealth column its portfolio is exactly zero. Clipping corrects only the action admissibility convention; it does not multiply interior portfolios by a wealth-distance factor. A bilinear interpolant is a convex combination of four nodal values. Taking their extrema over all time slices and applying monotone action clipping therefore gives a whole-domain enclosure. The new range audit verifies all six tensors and their upper-face portfolios.

The fresh-library selector chooses one of twenty completely enumerated actors. Taking extrema over every stored consumption and preference-adjustment slab therefore also bounds every policy the selector returns for any price in $[.5,8]$.
