# R42: Neural Bellman Operators — study protocol

## Identity and preservation

The revision starts from the R41 advisory review commit `d97959d6347464749111599a88a5f790f7aecc4c`, reviewing manuscript commit `06a104a8db06b76464165899d817b303cb2aaa01`. The subject, author, controlled-economy framework, original applications, preceding theorems and unfavorable comparisons are retained. No historical measurement is overwritten. The new work is a revision of Neural Bellman Operators, not a replacement paper on frozen-network verification.

## Primary estimand and service boundary

The primary object is a capped algorithm, given economic primitives, that returns either a stored neural continuation and certified feasible policy at a declared all-state loss target, or a recorded failure. A passing verifier is not a convergence theorem for a nonconvex optimizer. Report success fractions on the complete fixed task/seed catalogue, not an extrapolated population probability. Charge target generation from the algorithm's own future networks, every fit, every failed verification/refinement, policy extraction, independent queries where available, and result serialization through flush/fsync. Report unclassified software exceptions separately from mathematical target failures. Record process initialization separately; do not present in-process clocks as cold machine start times.

## Scalar from-primitives catalogue

Use the inherited four-date investment primitives, price in {1,4}, recursive risk in {0,1}, and seeds {104729,130363,155921}. Each neural service generates terminal targets from the primitive terminal cost, then works backward using only its own already fitted future network. No conventional spline values, frozen R39/R41 weights, conventional labels, or exact-ReLU spline fallback enter the neural service. A finite hidden-weight training schedule and finite verification ladder are fixed in source before publication runs. Retain all attempts.

All-state targets are 0.06 and 0.04. The verifier compares all continuous feasible controls and includes the terminal network. A conventional spline procedure starts from the same primitives and has its own resolution ladder and the same stopping targets. Report every target's first crossing, or failure at the cap, rather than comparing an arbitrary final neural resolution with a conventional final-resolution clock. The four inherited query states are reported separately. For newly executed neural-versus-conventional policy values, the descriptive economic margin is fixed here at 0.005 cost units; do not retrofit it to inherited results, call interval overlap equivalence, or infer population superiority from selected states.

## Coupled nonlinear construction

Add a two-state, two-control investment economy with a shared simplex investment constraint, coupled nonlinear transitions, and independent continuous uniform innovations. Keep the economic objective within the existing NBO investment program. Execute fresh backward training on own-future Bellman targets. A convex squared-ReLU realization permits a direct strong-convexity inner-optimization certificate; state coverage, terminal error and innovation integration must still be proved and paid, not sampled away. All-state targets are 0.25 and 0.10. Use prices {1,4}, the same three seeds, and a fixed capped source schedule. A convex quadratic projection comparator receives the same primitives, inner optimization, verifier, target and accounting boundary. This comparator is not described as the strongest possible sparse-grid or high-order projection method. Report actual state cover, parameter/storage and operation counts. A coarse successful two-dimensional experiment is not a high-dimensional scaling claim.

## Precision complete-work replication

Retain the R41 pressure catalogue and all adverse clocks. Repeat its twelve dimension/conditioning/target objects three times against implementable fixed32, fixed64 and adaptive policies, preserving precision attempts and failures. Report accepted/rejected operations by precision and complete service clocks; do not claim that activation alone establishes savings. A new precision controller may be analyzed separately, but its results must not be substituted for the original adaptive method.

## Exposition and release

Organize the main article around the economic problem, NBO map, training-to-certificate algorithm, theorem, matched evidence, and economic interpretation. Retain the complete preceding theory and application text in clearly linked companions rather than deleting it. Distinguish the neural critic, nearest-node deployed actor, original economic model, and numerical execution contract. Bind sources, results, failure records, PDFs and point-by-point responses to a release manifest. This protocol fixes targets and reporting rules; implementation development and debugging remain disclosed and are not characterized as independent preregistration or blinding.
