\hypertarget{principal-mathematical-and-empirical-changes}{%
\subsection{1. Principal mathematical and empirical
changes}\label{principal-mathematical-and-empirical-changes}}

The new section, \textbf{Policy-relevant continuation errors},
strengthens the signed-width finite-sweep theorem. Let e denote the
difference between the incumbent's true policy value and its fitted
continuation. Rather than charge its global range width w at every
action comparison, we certify

\[
 |(P_t^a-P_t^b)e_{t+1}^k(x)|\le R_t^k(x;a,b)\le\chi_t^k.
\]

The proposal is accepted only when its centered comparison upper bound
plus beta times the proposal--incumbent action-error certificate is
nonpositive. Otherwise the incumbent is retained exactly. The theorem
proves both all-state nonworsening and

\[
 E_t^{k+1}\le\beta_t E_{t+1}^k+
 \alpha_t^k+\zeta_t^k+2\beta_t\chi_t^k.
\]

The original finite-horizon unrolling therefore holds with chi in place
of w. The global-width rule remains a valid fallback. Exact action
contrasts, search and comparison imply optimality after at most T passes
even with nonzero, nonconstant global continuation errors. The
coefficient two remains sharp; its proof is retained and specialized to
the new certificate class. These statements concern the original Bellman
optimum, not a weakened objective.

A coupled-residual proposition constructs pairwise error bounds using
policy Bellman residuals and couplings with the correct marginals. It
does not assume access to the true policy value or success of an
unexecuted transport optimizer. The action-null corollary identifies
precisely which nonconstant continuation errors can be removed from
action comparisons. Membership must be proved, or any remaining contrast
must be bounded; it is never inferred from sampled residuals.

In the original investment dynamics the first two action coefficients
are one-half and one-quarter. Consequently the common-innovation
difference of the first coordinate and twice the second is independent
of the action. The nuisance \texttt{M\ ReLU(y1\ -\ 2\ y2\ +\ 1)} is
therefore action-null, although its global width is \texttt{2M}. This is
an explicit neural-realization robustness result. It is shared by a
native representation of the same function and is not relabeled as
neural superiority.

The additional experiment enumerates all 65,536 closed eight-bit
observation cells for each of the eight retained target-two policies.
Four amplitudes, zero, one, sixteen and 4096, were fixed before this
execution. The study retains whole-cell feasibility, the original
terminal action lattice and proposal, outward cost comparisons, and
every rejected proposal. It records all 524,288 policy--cell cases and
every amplitude gate. Its returned contrast-aware actions are
independently checked against the unmodified inherited terminal
implementation. All new counts and clocks are generated from these
records.

This is an exhaustive deterministic robustness ablation, not another
independent expected-cost sample. The continuous investment study still
executes a final-date improvement, not T full policy-iteration passes.
The latter are exercised separately by exact finite-state regression
programs. The manuscript does not interchange these two forms of
evidence.

\hypertarget{b2-and-m2-the-identified-contribution-and-neural-specificity}{%
\subsubsection{B2 and M2: The identified contribution and neural
specificity}\label{b2-and-m2-the-identified-contribution-and-neural-specificity}}

The native original-owner distance transform and its exact affine--ReLU
realization continue to compute the same continuation, owner and
feasible action. They are not two independent empirical methods. The
compiler's common saving is not attributed uniquely to a neural
architecture.

R52 adds a positive result about what continuation accuracy a Neural
Bellman Operator actually needs: an error is relevant through its
feasible action contrasts. This goes beyond removal of a datewise
constant and includes an explicit nonconstant single-ReLU component in
the original nonlinear investment model. The coupled-residual argument
supplies a constructive certificate rather than assuming the conclusion.
The property is appropriately shared by any other representation that
supplies the same verified objects. We retain the NBO subject while
making this distinction mathematically exact.

\hypertarget{b3b4-and-m3m4-actual-cost-and-the-source-of-conservatism}{%
\subsubsection{B3--B4 and M3--M4: Actual cost and the source of
conservatism}\label{b3b4-and-m3m4-actual-cost-and-the-source-of-conservatism}}

The 93 witness-higher, zero witness-lower and three unresolved R49 cost
comparisons are unchanged. They remain adverse evidence about those
actual policies. Tighter certificates are not substituted for this
ranking. The retained R51 direct study applies the same feasible
final-stage improvement to both methods and records each own-incumbent
gain and the remaining cross-method difference.

The identity

\[
J_W-J_F=(J_W-J_{W^+})+(J_{W^+}-J_{F^+})-(J_F-J_{F^+})
\]

continues to separate removable terminal-action loss from the remaining
gap. Earlier-date actions, terminal states, capacity repair, blocked
proposals and work remain in the mechanism records. We do not assert
that this decomposition identifies every earlier-date source of policy
loss.

R52 identifies a separate, precisely removable source of conservatism: a
global evaluation-width allowance can reject a beneficial action because
of an error whose action contrast is identically zero. The new theorem
removes that allowance without changing the policy optimum or weakening
the incumbent safety requirement. The exhaustive experiment holds the
economic policies fixed and varies only that nuisance. It therefore
establishes robustness, not a newly favorable witness-versus-FVI cost
ranking. The historical joint cost/work frontiers remain the empirical
economic comparison.

\hypertarget{interpretation-of-the-completed-revision}{%
\subsection{4. Interpretation of the completed
revision}\label{interpretation-of-the-completed-revision}}

R52 supplies a stronger proved accuracy account, an executable
residual-based certificate, a nonconstant neural action-null realization
in the original model, and a complete deterministic robustness test with
ordinary source and evidence. These additions directly address the
distinction between critic error, safe policy improvement and actual
economic cost. They do not reverse the inherited unfavorable method
comparisons. Ten new exact tests supplement the 44 inherited tests; the
executed test totals and publication outcomes are recorded by the build
rather than assumed in advance.

The remaining empirical scope is stated precisely: full-horizon
investment improvement, high-dimensional non-tensor performance,
comprehensive adaptive-method superiority and empirically estimated
sensor prices are not established by the new terminal ablation. These
are not substituted for the original theorem targets or used to abandon
the NBO paper. The revision provides its mathematical statements and
reproducible evidence for the referee to evaluate on their merits.
