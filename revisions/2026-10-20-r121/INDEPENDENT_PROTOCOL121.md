## Independent economic reconstruction

The independent evaluator uses Python standard-library rational arithmetic and
the preserved `frozen108/code/independent94.py` primitive source, SHA256
`a1a0e4ce5563d1105c3cea9eaad6678c609229446ba6794f7b8a7c03df01ebad`.
It imports no production risk, interval, fitting or quadrature implementation.
The R120 independent witness formulas are retained in `code121/exact121.py`;
they are executed on every distinct **new R121** final action and raw first
proposal at each sampled state and risk. An identical policy key can be
evaluated once within this independent validation, with every referring row
retained. This does not give an experimental service free shared information.

Every such key receives the same 64 original-innovation cells and all 129
midpoint/endpoint successor states. Every terminal context first checks the
two exact boundary derivative signs. A boundary optimum requires no bisection;
an interior optimum receives exactly 32 rational bisection steps. Actual
iteration counts are saved. The calculation uses feasible original risk
densities, exact polynomial integration and a saved primal/dual identity.
Midpoint state supports include
capacity multipliers and stationarity allowances. They produce a lower bound
over the entire original action interval. Adjacent endpoint atoms retain their
multiplicity in the independent upper risk distribution.

The actual last-date controller is bounded by the optimal continuation plus
`epsilon_1=2^-20`; `beta*epsilon_1` is paid exactly once in a policy upper value.
The evaluator does not assert exact evaluation of the rounded future actor.
At a common initial state/risk, the strongest independently purchased lower
support and the smallest independently purchased feasible upper value may be
used for the original optimum. These are research validation costs and are
never fed back into fitted actors or their experimental decisions.

Final-policy regret and squared error from the original optimal action are
bounded using original action strong convexity with modulus two. Raw proposals
are retained even when rejected or economically inferior. Their regret is not
clipped to the completed-service target. Compatibility of production and
independent policy intervals is required and reported; intersection or one
interval's inclusion in another does not independently certify the other
interval's narrower endpoints.

## Independent acquisition verification

The action-value model dictionaries, coefficient boxes, state/action designs,
probe locations, original value enclosures, slopes, precision charges, all
fitting constraints and stored rounded coefficients are retained. A separate
implementation reconstructs every finite feature and action derivative from
the declared algebraic parameters. ReLU and conventional equal-feature hinge
encodings must describe the same mathematical critic; their full standalone
acquisition costs remain charged.

The independent verifier uses exact rational primal and dual bounds for the
bounded marginal linear program. Nonnegative dual weights are rationalized and
normalized; any remaining stationarity mismatch is paid by minimizing the
linear residual over the declared coefficient box. Thus the verified global
training gap does not depend on a floating optimizer's success flag. For the

box minimization provide an independently evaluated optimization gap. These
bounds verify the acquired finite optimization problem; a finite-node fit is
not silently promoted to a continuum policy certificate.
