# Neural Bellman Operators: R121 author revision

This directory is an author revision of **Neural Bellman Operators** for the
Econometrica numerical-methods research programme. The original constrained,
two-decision-date investment problem and its continuous nested AVaR objective
remain the numerical target. The new experiment changes the learned root
continuation's training loss, with the same acquired information, feasible
actions, original-risk certification service, and conventional comparison menu.

## Reading the contribution

The main manuscript is `ECTA.tex` / `build/ECTA.pdf`. Its new mathematical
component is `new121/marginal_main.tex`, with complete proofs in
`new121/marginal_proofs.tex` and `supp.tex` / `build/supp.pdf`.
The new construction has five connected parts:

1. Derive explicit marginal and regularity bounds for the original continuous
   risk operator, including capacity and adverse-tail boundary cases.
2. Acquire outward action-marginal labels from paid original Bellman values,
   and fit an anchored continuation readout by a robust finite-box linear
   programme. The value-fitting control receives the same complete label
   bundle and uses the same features and coefficient box.
3. Independently check the actual rounded readouts and their finite-box
   optimization gaps with rational primal and dual/support witnesses.
4. Connect feature-aware marginal enclosures to action contrasts, constrained
   policy loss, and the levels that must be retained when a value is exported
   into an earlier recursive Bellman operator.
5. Compare the resulting decisions and complete costs under the unchanged
   original objective. Retain the initial proposal, every rejection, the
   eventual certified policy, and the strong conventional alternatives.

The finite-cover feature certificate is a proved construction. The numerical
campaign executes the separately specified original-risk production service;
it does **not** claim to have computed that global feature-cover certificate.
A finite-box training gap does not by itself certify off-design marginal
accuracy, policy improvement, or adoption.

The experiment trains 48 readouts and creates eight exact matched-hinge
controls, across seven representations, two independently acquired budgets,
two risk levels and two training losses. ReLU and its matched hinge encode the
same functions. The hinge's actual copy, identity, and parent-fit charges are
retained, but ordinary hinges can use the same batching and arithmetic as a
ReLU implementation. Their shared geometry or an implementation-specific cost
difference is not a neural-exclusive advantage.

## What the completed R121 experiment establishes

All nine original process stages exited successfully: the two acquisitions,
the two independent training audits, the new state draw, the primary service
including all five fresh clocks, independent economic reconstruction, frozen
statistics, and the deterministic complete report. There are 56 acquired
actors, 58 policy cells, 3,712 primary decisions and 9,280 timed decisions.
The independent reconstruction checks 1,301 distinct policy/proposal keys,
167,829 terminal contexts and 1,406,549 executed assertions. These are execution
and verification counts, not independent statistical sample sizes.

The robust marginal loss is lower at the acquired nodes in **all 28** matched
training comparisons. Nevertheless the independent 64-state mean original
cost gain, defined as VALUE cost minus MARGINAL cost, is strictly negative in
**all 28** sample enclosures. Every one of the prespecified simultaneous
population mean gain intervals contains zero. The data therefore establish
adverse sample means, without a population mean improvement or deterioration
claim under the fixed joint 95 percent procedure. The frozen threshold-event
bounds describe heterogeneous benefits and harms separately; no retrospective
selector is credited with an unexecuted economic gain.

All 3,712 deployed policies satisfy the common saved production qualification.
VALUE has 60 first rejections and MARGINAL has 294 out of 1,792 proposals per
loss. All 354 refusals incur a conventional fallback and change the final
action. MARGINAL is faster in 4 of 28 primary complete cohort comparisons and
in 40 of 140 corresponding timing comparisons; with the full declared
acquisition and audit allocation the counts are 6 of 28 and 38 of 140.
The online original-model baseline has a lower independently enclosed sample
cost than every learned cell and a lower allocated full cost at N=64. All
ReLU full-menu sufficient and upper not-ruled-out price regions are empty,
including every individual clock and the five-clock intersections.

These findings are a direct test of the original NBO learning and decision
problem. The new proved feature-cover, action-contrast and capacity-sensitive
policy bounds identify additional conditions that a finite-node training loss
does not supply. Their global feature-cover construction was not numerically
executed in this campaign. The current algorithm does not establish a
neural-exclusive computational advantage, an economically successful marginal
training rule, higher-dimensional scaling or calibrated application adoption.
The complete negative and positive statewise evidence is retained as a basis
for those still-open research obligations, without altering the original
objective, sample, prescribed comparisons or Econometrica research direction.

## Reproduce the saved scientific evidence

Use a clean checkout containing F, M, D and the final author-source commit,
and work from this native directory. Keep that checkout for saved-evidence
CI and reporting. Perform every new scientific replication in a separate
checkout, so that its additional successes or failures cannot enter the
original launch ledger. Python 3.12.14 and the pinned packages in
`requirements121.txt` are the recorded numerical environment. The original
launcher fixes the scientific thread environment to one thread and records
actual commands, versions, wall time, RSS, stdout and exit status. No GPU is
required by this experiment.

The final CI wrapper verifies the saved source/actor/state bytes and complete
record census, then replays the exact statistics and complete stable reporting
tables. It also rebuilds all four PDFs and requires byte equality with the
source build snapshot:

```bash
python verification121/ci121.py --output ci121/reader-check01
```

Run this command at S: later additive publication commits are separately
bound to S, and are not substituted for S's complete file census. A fresh
output path is required. `--skip-build` checks the saved evidence and tables
without TeX, but is explicitly not the full author-package check.

No original economic solver, learning procedure, independent entropy draw,
or timing campaign is invoked by this CI replay. Its success does not turn
historical data into a new scientific replication. The original actual runs
and their costs remain in their original directories.

To repeat the independent rational evaluation on the published policies,
use the separate replication checkout, choose unused output directories and
preserve the new execution separately:

```bash
python code121/launch121.py --output results121/launch-reader-independent01 -- \
  python code121/independent121.py \
  --source-commit 16bf21334efcbc719b83e2ccf10e8527f8b956f2 \
  --primary results121/primary-attempt01/records121.jsonl \
```

This is a new computational replication of the saved actions. It does not
replace the original independent evidence or add independent initial states.
The evaluator checks every distinct final action and every available raw
proposal. An unavailable proposal remains explicitly unavailable; it is not
replaced by a zero or a successful fallback observation.

## Build and verify the documents and transport

The strict local builder does not run scientific code:

```bash
python build121.py --receipt-dir build_receipts121/reader01
```

It compiles all four documents with fixed PDF time metadata and records the
actual TeX and bibliography commands, input dependency closure, diagnostics
and PDF hashes. A clean source build requires no unresolved references or
citations, multiply defined labels, overfull boxes, or integration markers.
Exact PDF equality across systems remains an actual check, not an assumption
from a successful TeX exit alone.

`verification121/publication121.py` supplies `bind`, `pack`, `verify` and
`describe-parts`. A final referee-ready archive contains every native file at
P, including source, mathematical development, raw evidence, failed prefixes,
cost logs, PDF hashes and the actual CI/publication records selected in its
manifest. The independently checkable archive manifest lists all members,
byte counts, SHA256 values and Git blobs. The final delivery uses an unchanged
no-split archive manifest plus a separate `VIRTUAL_ZIP_CHUNKS121.json` that
binds the ordered, at-most-8-MiB Git parts in V. The archive contains the
complete native P tree; the later request Q and delivery additions V remain
outside that archive to avoid a self-referential package.

After observing the actual immutable S, P and V commits and the two metadata
hashes, verify every remote archive byte and member without saving a complete
local ZIP:

```bash
python verification121/remote_verify121.py \
  --repository TrillionniumFoundation/NBO \
  --delivery-commit V_FULL_40_HEX \
  --source-commit S_FULL_40_HEX \
  --publication-commit P_FULL_40_HEX \
```

`publication121.py verify --archive` with the actual source/publication commits
and expected manifest hash. Its `--parts-dir` mode belongs to the earlier

`provenance121/development_support121/REMOTE_PUBLICATION_HANDOFF121.md`.

## Historical evidence and remaining scientific obligations

The controlling report is the committed R97 owner-commissioned advisory
report, whose exact reviewed source is recorded in the retention map. It is
not represented as an Econometric Society editorial decision. The baseline
is the fully materialized and independently bound R120 source/publication
package, not the highest branch label. The complete branch and report census
and the prior still-valid requirements are retained with source identities.

The historical negative results remain evidence: traditional baselines that
won, rejection increases despite smaller residual scores, failed runs,
unresolved neural adoption regions, and the limits of prior refinement
algorithms. R98 and R115 experiments with independent incomplete obligations
are not completed by relabelling R121 data. New theorem, learning, policy,
computational and economic statements are distinguished throughout the
response and machine-readable requirement map. The programme remains the
construction, learning, comparison and certification of recursive Neural
Bellman Operators for the original economic decisions.
