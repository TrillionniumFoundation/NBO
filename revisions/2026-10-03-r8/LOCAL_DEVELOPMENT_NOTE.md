# Local development and immutable execution

R8 development followed observation of the R7 evidence and report. It is not
represented as an ex ante experiment. The four completed neural pilots and all
principal runs are rerun from the immutable published source, with their full
raw outputs and failed economic targets retained.

Before the final 800,000-box slope-cover run, an exploratory 300,000-box,
depth-18 call was interrupted by the local tool timeout. It produced no final
certificate JSON or NPZ. Its temporary progress-log path was subsequently
reused; a complete raw log of that interrupted call is not available. No bound,
completed-run count, or independent verification claim is attributed to it.
The complete slope and subsequent cell-corner executions have separate files.

The source changed during local development. The remote immutable-source run
is the authoritative reproducible execution; its manifest, rather than an
unrecorded local intermediate source version, identifies the reported results.
