                                      11. CONCLUSION
28                                                                                          28

29     Neural Bellman Operators connect learned continuation operators to feasible          29

30   decisions and the economic value of their implemented future behavior. The con- 30
31   struction requires a fixed original recursion and a complete accounting of the         31

32   information used by its critic, actor and numerical certificate. The constrained       32
     40

1    investment model makes these requirements explicit while retaining continuous           1

2    innovations and nonlinear recursive risk.                                               2

3      The new action-comparison theory uses the geometry of the original Bellman            3

4    problem. A constrained state-gradient modulus yields an action-scaled continuation- 4
5    difference bound; convex integration and a constrained risk-defect transfer then        5

6    provide computable comparison intervals. Qualification and a strict original-policy     6

7    improvement bound jointly govern replacement of a completed incumbent. The              7

8    proof also identifies what additional precision can resolve and what remains hidden     8

9    behind a uniform future-policy allowance. This distinction gives a concrete stopping    9

10   criterion and a specified information problem for subsequent NBO algorithms.            10

11     The independent experiment certifies gains over the completed learned incum- 11
12   bents under the original investment objective. All 42 learned-incumbent refinement      12

13   groups have positive simultaneous population mean-gain lower bounds; the in- 13
14   dependent optimum supports also give much smaller upper bounds on original              14

15   policy regret and root-mean-square optimal-action error. These gains coexist with a     15

16   strong online incumbent that is already close to optimal. The new paired service        16

17   changes twelve returned actions relative to inherited safe improvement, but no cell- 17
18   level incremental mean advantage is resolved, and all sixteen primary costs and all     18

19   forty-eight matched clocks are higher. The mixed comparison authorizes one shared       19

20   candidate at a recorded resolution where the separate convex bound is inconclu- 20
21   sive. The direct conventional proposal has a strictly lower independently enclosed      21

22   sample mean cost than both L and P in five cells and is faster than P throughout the    22

23   matched clocks. ReLU and hinge policies are identical. ReLU has empty sufficient        23

24   full-menu adoption regions, and its upper-condition intersections across the three      24

25   timing scenarios are empty; individual-clock upper conditions leave some cases          25

26   unresolved. These findings preserve the independent neural contribution as an           26

27   explicit scientific problem.                                                            27

28     The remaining research priorities concern the joint design of continuation learning   28

29   and paid economic decisions. They include acquisition losses that reduce action- 29
30   comparison work, information about the actual future controller that resolves           30

31   gains below the present allowance, and matched experiments with longer horizons,        31

32   multiple controls and higher-dimensional continuous risk. The retained stochastic- 32
                                        NEURAL BELLMAN OPERATORS                                              41

1    control, recursive-utility and stopped-boundary results provide separate rigorous                               1

2    foundations for this program. Progress on these problems must preserve the original                             2

3    objective, give conventional methods the same usable information, and demonstrate                               3

4    the economic value of the resulting policies together with their full computational                             4
