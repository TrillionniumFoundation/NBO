# NBO R98: revision scope and evidence boundary

The revision responds to the R97 advisory report at review commit 9723c6cd2cc8b3e071422f3e522d4a843946db63. The reviewed article is R97 at f802fe64d91d9f5c1c8f86123b0fbe114e622279. This branch preserves both histories. The paper remains Neural Bellman Operators, in the original dynamic-policy construction problem and Econometrica style.

The principal mathematical task is to connect acquisition to action-relevant certification and complete service work, rather than claiming that residual-span reduction implies fewer rejected proposals. We will derive a decision-specific bound and an executable acquisition/selection rule, with explicit context-law and qualification assumptions. We will also investigate an oracle refinement and a decision-theoretic embedding of the retained oblique-continuation example. Every result must state its information class and economic target.

The existing R96 and R97 evidence is retained, including the quadratic winner, all 36 span/rejection comparisons, zero adoption over quadratic least squares, and unresolved policy-cost intervals. New fitted actors cannot inherit old actors' sampling intervals. Development tests, finite-catalogue diagnostics, and prospective population evidence will be separately labelled. No unexecuted campaign will be reported as executed.

Publication will include source, article, supplement, response, a precise claim-to-evidence map, and an executable dependency closure. No historical paper or review is deleted or overwritten. The first source-export workflow only makes the pinned scientific sources and fixtures available for inspection; it asserts no new scientific result.
