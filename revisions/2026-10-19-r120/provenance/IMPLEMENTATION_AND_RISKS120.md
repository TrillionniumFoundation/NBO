# R120 source inspection and prospective implementation decision

This is an author-side source audit and design record. It contains no new economic measurements, random states, fitted models or validation outcomes. The source/protocol hash receipts in this directory bind the actual prepared files; remote freezes and actual launches are root-owned.

The eight R119 actors are obtained by fitting terminal continuation values and minimizing the resulting recursive root objective. These are not directly action-supervised actors. ReLU and the conventional hinge control use the same learned parameters, and the tensor interpolant requires the complete ordered midpoint training grid. The native fixed future controller uses downward 40-bit state observations; describing this as forty optimizer steps is incorrect.

R108 already has three original-support policy-improvement attempts accepted by strict independent policy-interval separation. R110 already has gain-ceiling stopping and actual improved policies. Simply restoring those routines would not be a new R120 algorithm. R119 A/B/C instead changed service/certificate acquisition while keeping within-arm actions identical; their speed ratios cannot become policy gains in this revision.

## Additional implemented mechanism

The prepared R120 source retains ordinary terminal-value training and changes the economically authoritative comparison. The new original-economy mixed regularity theorem supplies a deterministic shock-Lipschitz modulus proportional to action separation. Paired midpoint differences have a valid action-dependent error bound even when their difference is not convex. The implementation intersects that interval with separately valid convex midpoint/secant bounds, pays both original tail-density slacks and the unchanged future-policy allowance, and only accepts a strictly certified original-policy improvement that also meets original qualification.

A proved tail-defect transfer is additionally intersected with the directly paid incumbent slack. Exact integer square roots and Fraction checks provide upward dyadic square-root bounds. This improves a lower comparison/rejection bound at the candidate's selected density; it is not attributed as an upper-acceptance or unpurchased-query advantage. Activity and operation counts are recorded so an unused bound cannot be credited for a result supplied by another bound.

The four services are completed, unchanged legacy-safe, paired with the same three historical proposal rules, and strong direct-refine at 512 root midpoint nodes and 24 bisections. The direct control receives the identical new paired information primitive. The online/direct arm is the standalone strong conventional control with no training charge. All eight representations run all four services, so fitted methods are not insulated from conventional wins.

## Prospective custody and sample

Artifact checks verify kind, exact source F, exact sixteen-model census, actual committed bytes and the distinct ancestor chain F to M to D. Git ancestry is not represented as a remote publication-time receipt.

The evaluation population is explicitly finite discrete uniform in each 40-bit coordinate; the economic innovation law remains continuous uniform on the original shock support. One initial state is passed to each complete service. This prevents the existing whole-batch terminal-compiler fallback from coupling distinct held-out observations. All 64 rows remain in every arm/risk/service comparison, including hypothetical repeated state draws. Validation can deduplicate identical economic keys after all decisions have been paid; population rows are never deduplicated.

The prepared catalogue has 4,096 singleton primary decisions and 6,144 additional descriptive timing decisions in three fresh serial processes. Every model is reloaded per service cohort. Individual service times, original query counters, cold loading, complete cohort time, process overhead and acquisition charges are separately identified. Gzip event streams preserve candidate values, slacks, point payloads, precision attempts, rejections and failed prefixes. Source-only review corrected a wrong-freeze artifact acceptance gap and an event-file creation timing exclusion before any scientific execution.

## Remaining risks and interpretation limits

The new modulus is proved for d=2. The retained d4/d8 original-model material remains available, but this prospective study does not claim a new higher-dimensional learned-policy tournament or apply unproved constants outside d2.

The paired method may not improve on the inherited control. The conservative mixed constant, terminal point intervals, tail mismatch and beta times 2^-20 future allowance may leave small gains unresolved, and the conventional direct solver may dominate. Such results are valid; all schemes have the same original objective and access to strong conventional computation.

Singleton calls cost more than vectorizing many initial states. The unchanged rational fallback is finite but expensive (1,025 root candidates, 256 shock cells and bounded terminal solves). No unconditional thirty-minute runtime promise is made; a sample may not be thinned if a declared run is slow. Independent all-key 64-cell/32-step Fraction validation can also dominate the scientific validation budget. Its actual expense must be reported separately from competitive decision service.

Population intervals are conditional on the declared ideal independent OS-byte acquisition law and the frozen actors. The root-owned inference protocol gives simultaneous conservative service-versus-own-incumbent gain bounds and a fixed threshold staircase. It does not establish cross-representation population superiority, neural adoption or population runtime dominance. Cross-method original economic contrasts and all neural/conventional negatives remain explicitly reported.

Any new neural-specific claim requires evidence beyond a representation-equivalent hinge, spline, polynomial or direct solver. This design is intended to establish actual safe original-policy changes and to measure their full economic/computational value. It does not assert their observed sign or competitiveness before execution.
