## Mathematical contribution

The new results construct a comparison certificate for the difference between
two original Bellman actions. The constrained investment model has continuous
innovations, nested average value at risk, convex adjustment costs and an
unchanged implemented terminal controller. A global constrained state-gradient
modulus yields a continuation-difference bound proportional to the action
distance. Its intersection with convex quadrature bounds can resolve the sign
of policy improvement before either full value is known to the same accuracy.

The supplement proves the constrained regularity bound, matching information
orders for an explicitly defined fixed-risk-density convex point-oracle
class, a constrained tail-defect transfer, original-policy safety and gain
telescoping, finite-resolution sufficient conditions, the future-policy
information floor, independent global optimum lower supports, and the exact
simultaneous population inference. The oracle lower bound is not asserted for
formula-informed algorithms in the fixed investment model. The retained Bellman,
actor/critic, gradients, stochastic control, recursive utility and boundary
results carry their own assumptions and proofs.
