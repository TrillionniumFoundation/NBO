\title{Neural Bellman Operators}
\runtitle{Neural Bellman Operators}
\begin{aug}
\author[id=au1,addressref={add1}]{\fnms{Qian}~\snm{QI}\ead[label=e92ECTA]{qiqian@pku.edu.cn}}
\address[id=add1]{Peking University}
\end{aug}
\begin{abstract}
Neural Bellman Operators construct and learn continuation operators for feasible
decisions under an original recursive economic objective. We derive an
action-scaled comparison modulus for the constrained investment economy with
continuous innovations and nested average value at risk. Intersecting paired
continuation differences with convex value bounds yields a computable
action-aligned certificate. Matching information bounds hold for a stated
fixed-risk-density point-oracle class; a constrained tail-defect transfer and
the implemented future-policy allowance extend the calculation to actual policy
changes. We prove uniformly safe replacement, finite-resolution sufficient
conditions and the remaining future-policy information floor. The stated Bellman,
actor--critic, recursive-utility and boundary propositions receive complete
proofs under their separate assumptions. Eight representations with common
access to the original primitives and four complete services are compared
under a separately frozen independent protocol, with all acquisition,
refusal, qualification and service costs. Independent rational reconstruction
supplies original-policy and global-optimum bounds. All 42 learned-incumbent
refinement groups obtain positive simultaneous population mean-gain lower
bounds. The new mixed bound changes one recorded acceptance decision, while
the paired service is slower than inherited safe improvement in every matched
clock. Strong online and direct conventional methods remain competitive;
no neural advantage is established against the complete conventional
menu. The results identify the joint learning and certification work required
for economically valuable NBO decisions.
\end{abstract}
