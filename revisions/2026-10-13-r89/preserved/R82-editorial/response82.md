## B8: A uniform guarantee at approximate optimization tolerances

Let U be the attained feasible readout's fixed-label objective. Any readout with objective at most U+epsilon can select only the reference action or an alternative whose switch lower bound is at most U+epsilon. Equality is retained so the proof covers ties. Since the unknown optimum is at most U, every epsilon-optimal readout is included.

The new theorem conditions on each initial acquisition cell and encloses the expected cost difference for every such retained action followed by the same continuation policy.
