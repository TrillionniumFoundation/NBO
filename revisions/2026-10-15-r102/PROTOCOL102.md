## Mathematical target

Construct an implementable original-uniform-law AVaR point-value algorithm for convex K-Lipschitz functions. Localize the excluded interval of original probability alpha by value-query comparisons, form lower and upper affine envelopes only on its complement, and importance-sample their gap. Establish finite-query bias, variance and confidence bounds, including rational finite-grid sampling. Investigate whether the randomized N^(-5/2) lower bound in R99 has a matching upper bound, and whether a tail-localized deterministic upper bound matches its (1-alpha) dependence. No theorem is presumed true before proof and verification. These are oracle results, not dimension-free Bellman complexity or neural-specific representation results.
