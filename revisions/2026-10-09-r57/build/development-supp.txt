13       An objective query has lower and upper endpoints for its midpoint-sum expec- 13
14   tation, plus the deterministic continuous-law integration remainder. Labels are               14

15   rounded on the inherited dyadic lattice, and the largest distance to the objective            15

16   endpoints supplies the query allowance et . Selecting the least rounded label and             16

17   retaining its original action costs one allowance in the label comparison and another         17

18   in the complete selected-policy account. It does not require a second copy of the             18

19   action-cover term in main equation (7).                                                       19

20       For a uniform scalar bin of width w, the mean absolute distance to its midpoint           20

21   is w/4. Here w = 1/(16M) and the transition’s shock modulus is d. Therefore the               21

22   numerical account decomposes into βdLt+1 /(64M) and the remaining arithmetic                  22

23   width. The recorded primitive coefficients in main equation (35) reconstruct each             23

24   witness bound as a/N + b/K + q/M + η with a nonnegative numerical remainder. 24
25   The release checks this decomposition at all witness rungs, as well as the full signed        25

26   formula for every conventional rung.                                                          26

                    3. WHY THE COMPILED ACTOR IS THE ORIGINAL ACTOR
4                                                                                            4

5
       At a tensor node the distance transform minimizes over original sites, not merely     5

6
     neighboring transformed nodes. Each forward relaxation along a coordinate line          6

7
     compares the current value–owner pair with the preceding pair plus the weighted         7

8
     coordinate distance; the backward pass does the same from the opposite direction. 8

9
     The invariant is the lexicographic minimum over all processed original sites in the     9

10
     processed coordinates. Separability proves the invariant after the final coordinate. 10

11
     Keeping only the winning value would lose the actor identity.                           11

12
       For an off-grid point, choose a cell corner coordinatewise between the point and      12

13
     its lexicographically first minimizing site. Distances add exactly. At that corner an   13

14
     owner giving a smaller value, or a tied value and smaller original index, contradicts   14

15
     the minimizer at the original point. This proves the owner identity even for incon- 15

16
     sistent labels, zero slope, boundary cells and ties. Nonuniform coordinate spacings     16

17
     enter only the exact distances.                                                         17

18
       In interval execution, value expressions are enclosed outward. Actor candidates       18

19
     whose lower objective exceeds another candidate’s upper objective can be discarded;     19

20
     the remaining possibilities must be retained. An exact point tie is resolved by         20

21
     rational comparison and the original index rule. A genuine uncertain state box is       21

22
     not an exact point: the hull of its surviving actions is propagated. Boxes crossing     22

23
     cells use the union of all relevant corners. These operations may cost more than one    23
