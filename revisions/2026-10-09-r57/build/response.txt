                        1.2. B1, M1: A specific trained-neural contribution
11                                                                                               11

12       The new neural continuation is genuinely fitted: all hidden and output coefficients     12

13   receive Adam updates before a final ridge output fit, and its stored dyadic coeffi- 13
14   cients are retained. It is not a relabeled native min-plus envelope. The integrated- 14
15   continuation lemma gives the exact scalar-uniform expectation of each learned               15

16   ridge feature, with outward numerical evaluation and explicit treatment of zero             16

17   innovation projection.                                                                      17

18       The new Algebraic neural action witnesses theorem then reduces the original             18

19   scalar investment objective to piecewise quartics. Rational hinge breakpoints and           19

20   cubic stationary-root isolation yield an exact minimizer on the deployed action lat- 20
21   tice using at most 22m+13 candidate indices for m hidden features, with coefficient         21

22   bit lengths and root-isolation cost separately charged. This supplies a terminating         22

23   fitted-critic action search rather than assuming another numerical optimizer suc- 23
24   ceeds. The code uses exact rational comparisons and handles ties, negative output           24

25   weights and zero projected noise.                                                           25

26       The solver agrees with exhaustive rational lattice minimization on the retained         26

27   saved-neural fixtures in dimensions two, four and eight. These are transparent              27

28   solver regressions, not new actual-cost observations. The R55 production design             28

29   used its frozen 17-action construction menu; we have not reassigned its economic            29

30   intervals or clocks to policies generated by the new search. The original constructive- 30
31   backend theorem and compiler remain in the active paper as a distinct route to              31

32   primitive Bellman accuracy.                                                                 32

            1.4. B3, M3, M4: Accuracy, enclosure width and the dominant obstruction
23                                                                                           23

24     The full-action directed theorem is retained with its statewise safety statement,     24

25   coefficient-one error recursion, finite-horizon expansion and sharpness example. 25
26   The new cache proposition makes its required intervals computable on a non- 26
27   tensor partition. It encloses residuals and values on each assigned observation cell,   27

28   retains every future leaf intersecting a query box, and intersects independently        28

29   valid residual and direct-value accounts. Discontinuous actors are not assigned a       29

30   fictitious derivative.                                                                  30

31     The primary cache-only cohort exposes the remaining obstruction: broad contin- 31
32   uation and actor ranges block nonterminal changes. The separately frozen signed- 32

                    1.5. B4, M2: A prospective from-primitives stopping service
19                                                                                            19

20       Each method/task/target combination starts from primitives in its own process. 20
21   The two resource stages, acquired leaves, training budgets, inference looks and          21

22   expected-cost targets are specified before that cohort’s production. At each stage       22

23   the complete new policy is frozen before its fresh inference stream. The service         23

24   returns at the first nonpositive upper bound for J(policy)-q\J(installed). A strictly    24

25   positive lower bound advances the resource stage; an unresolved interval advances        25

26   the declared look. No later stage or look is computed after a successful return.         26

27       The first-attainment theorem proves the economic validity of this return under a fi- 27
28   nite conditional confidence allocation. It also proves that every returned incumbent,    28

29   including a budget-exhausted one, retains deterministic statewise safety. All own        29

30   construction, verification, inference, serialization and durable output are charged by   30

31   the surrounding process clock. This is not the R54 retrospective complete-catalogue      31

32   frontier relabeled as a prospective algorithm.                                           32

                1.6. B5, M5: Non-tensor execution and explicit dimensional work
9                                                                                            9

10     The new acquired-state representation is a locally split binary partition with 32     10

11   or 128 leaves, rather than a Cartesian product of coordinate grids. The complete        11

12   services run the original nonlinear family at (d,T)=(2,2),(4,4),(8,6). This is a gen- 12
13   uinely multidimensional non-tensor implementation, not a nonuniform tensor grid         13

14   described as non-tensor.                                                                14

15     The work proposition charges the literal cell-membership calculation as O(T A         15

16   q Nˆ2 d) plus primitive and critic work, where N is the number of leaves and A          16

17   counts candidates and covering intervals. The signed smooth-reference calculation       17

18   costs O(A N q d Tˆ2) and can stream its innovation children. It needs neither           18

19   a full state-pair table nor enumeration of qˆT future innovation histories. The         19

20   primitive Jacobian gives the new dimension-uniform smooth-reference stability           20

21   bound 1920/91 for the original normalized family.                                       21

22     This bound and the executed eight-dimensional service are positive, specific          22

23   results. They do not imply that a fixed leaf count resolves arbitrary dimensional       23

24   models at a fixed all-state accuracy. The manuscript retains that separate approxima- 24
25   tion burden in its explicit work and error accounts rather than presenting non-tensor   25

26   storage as a universal elimination of dimensional complexity.                           26

                      1.8. B8, M8: Repetitions, hardware and statistical units
19                                                                                              19

20       Every declared service has three isolated sequential process runs, a fixed available   20

21   core and one numerical-library thread. Method order rotates across repetitions. 21
22   The tables report the minimum, median and maximum complete-process times,                  22

23   output bytes and peak resident memory. All own inference and durable records are           23

24   included.                                                                                  24

25       The repetitions deliberately keep training and inference seeds identical. Exact pol- 25
26   icy and confidence-interval identities are verified, so they are timing/reproducibility    26

27   repetitions, not a sample of independently trained critics. Clock frequency is not         27

28   controlled, and comparisons are within a task runner rather than a cross-runner            28

29   hardware-scaling regression. The new independently fitted direction observations           29

30   belong to the separate structural-uncertainty study and are not counted as inde- 30
31   pendent neural-policy fits. No optimizer success probability is inferred from these        31

32   records.                                                                                   32

2                                                                                             2

3      The original investment costs and transition laws are unchanged. The targets re- 3
4    quire two- and ten-percent expected-cost reductions from a specified installed zero- 4
5    investment controller. In the eight-dimensional, six-date neural service, changed        5

6    leaf counts are (26,24,24,20,14,0): the successful policy changes are nonterminal, not   6

7    a relabeled final-date repair. At the ten-percent return, the cumulative gain interval   7

8    is [0.21452658,0.29774911] with outward-rounded endpoints.                               8

9      The adoption corollary maps each gain interval into the entire replacement-fee         9

10   and computation-price plane: net gain lies in [l-tau-lambda W,u-tau-lambda               10

11   W]. It gives both the supported adoption region and the region where adop- 11
12   tion is ruled out, with the intermediate region left unidentified. This is an actual     12

13   implemented-policy decision with paid computation, not a conclusion about an             13

14   unchanged policy’s certificate. The quantities remain normalized model units rather      14

15   than a claimed external calibration.                                                     15
