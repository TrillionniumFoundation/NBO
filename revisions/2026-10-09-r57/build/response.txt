               1.2. M1 and B1: A specific neural computation and its policy meaning
14                                                                                             14

15       The original NBO subject is retained rather than replaced with a differently titled   15

16   paper. At the same time, the representation-neutral nature of safe improvement is         16

17   stated explicitly. The trained neural contribution is not the general idea of incumbent   17

18   protection and is not an exact encoding of a conventional min-plus policy.                18

19       For the stored trained ReLU continuation, exact scalar-uniform integration pro- 19
20   duces a piecewise-quartic investment objective. The inherited algebraic theorem           20

21   supplies a terminating action-lattice minimizer through rational breakpoints and          21

22   cubic-root isolation. R57 now executes that minimizer in prospective services. Nega- 22
23   tive output weights, zero innovation projection and ties are handled in exact rational    23

24   arithmetic. Quadratic fitted values receive the analogous exact-search opportunity,       24

25   and all modes receive the same exact terminal-action solver.                              25

26       The new fitted-witness transfer theorem explains what this exact computation          26

27   contributes to the original Bellman objective. Its local bound is the sum of center       27

28   optimization loss, within-cell action-contrast variation, action-relevant continuation    28

29   error, continuous-to-robust-lattice coverage and verification overshoot. Exact action     29

30   search eliminates the first term, not the others. The theorem carries that bound into     30

31   the coefficient-one finite-sweep recursion and includes a sharpness example. This         31

32   is a positive link between a trained action computation and full-action economic          32

4                 1.3. M2 and B4: A genuinely prospective stopping algorithm                  4

5                                                                                             5
       The new protocol fixes five modes, three tasks, three distinct training seeds, two
6                                                                                             6
     targets, two construction attempts and three inference looks before production.
7                                                                                             7
     Every mode/task/target/seed service starts from primitives in its own process.
8                                                                                             8
     No fitted object, validation interval or earlier construction rung is supplied free of
9                                                                                             9
     charge.
10                                                                                            10
       The targets require ten- and twenty-percent expected-cost reductions from in-
11                                                                                            11
     stalled zero investment. The stopping estimand is the signed difference J(candidate)
12                                                                                            12
     -q\J(installed), not a plug-in ratio. A nonpositive upper endpoint returns immedi-
13                                                                                            13
     ately; a positive lower endpoint advances the resource attempt; otherwise the next
14                                                                                            14
     declared look is drawn. A final unsuccessful attempt returns a budget-exhausted
15                                                                                            15
     safe candidate without claiming the target was attained or impossible. The complete
16                                                                                            16
     registry retains every attempt and return.
17                                                                                            17
       One distinction is made explicit. Intermediate refinement attempts are not de-
18                                                                                            18
     ployed, and every attempt is verified against the same smooth installed reference.
19                                                                                            19
     The procedure never differentiates a changed discontinuous acquired actor. Its
20                                                                                            20
     refinement attempts are therefore not relabeled as successive policy-iteration passes.
21                                                                                            21
     The original finite-sweep theorem remains available under its own stated premises.

          1.4. M3 and B2: Matched economic accuracy and actual returned-policy costs
24                                                                                            24

25     Each target has a separate from-primitives stopping service. Complete process          25

26   clocks include imports, construction, exact search, verification, all own inference,     26

27   serialized outputs and termination. We do not select an after-the-fact favorable         27

28   certificate threshold or charge only the final successful prefix operation.              28

29     After the five methods for a task, target and seed return, a new 65,536-path stream    29

30   evaluates ReLU-exact minus each of the four other returned policies. That stream         30

31   is separate from the stopping information under the independent-bin statistical          31

32   model. These comparisons are about the actual returned policies, even when their         32

              1.5. M4 and B3: Decomposition and an algorithmically removable term
13                                                                                            13

14       The new transfer theorem gives five mathematically defined terms rather than         14

15   calling every source of conservatism a training error. The center optimization loss      15

16   is computed exactly for the stored fitted objective. Its elimination is the controlled   16

17   intervention in the ReLU-exact mode. The common verifier and continuous-action           17

18   lower cover remain fixed.                                                                18

19       The new neural-modulus proposition gives an explicit acquisition allowance           19

20   from the stored feature weights, action projections and innovation radii. The affine     20

21   continuation component and exactly action-null ridges contribute zero to that            21

22   spatial action-contrast bound. Features with nonzero innovation radius receive the       22

23   corresponding clipped derivative factor. An explicit continuation-error allowance        23

24   is also given for the installed zero reference, and the continuous-action allowance      24

25   follows from robust capacity oscillation, action spacing and the original dimension- 25
26   uniform reference stability bound.                                                       26

27       These primitive accounts can be combined with the directly computed U-L bound        27

28   by taking the smaller valid allowance. They are not described as having been the         28

29   numerical stopping criterion when the execution used the direct lower cover. The         29

30   recorded U-C and C-L components still add cellwise; their separately printed             30

31   maxima need not add to the maximum total gap. We do not label an unexecuted              31

32   six-factor causal ablation as measured evidence.                                         32

                    1.6. M5 and B5: Non-tensor multidimensional execution
9                                                                                              9

10     The new services use the original nonlinear cyclic investment family at dimen- 10
11   sion/horizon pairs two/two, four/four and eight/six. Acquired observations use            11

12   a locally split binary partition with 32 or 64 leaves, not a nonuniform Cartesian         12

13   product described as non-tensor. Whole-cell capacities and downward-quantized             13

14   actions remain feasible for every state assigned to a leaf.                               14

15     The same signed reference procedure is used for all generators. Its proof and           15

16   dimension/horizon work account are retained from R56, including the dimension- 16
17   uniform smooth-reference Lipschitz bound and the absence of a full state-pair table       17

18   or exponential enumeration of future shock histories. The new exact-search records        18

19   add rational root and candidate-evaluation counts. These are actual multidimen- 19
20   sional executions, but not an asymptotic experiment showing that a fixed number           20

21   of leaves solves arbitrary high-dimensional models at fixed all-state accuracy.           21

22     The original Bellman target is not replaced by the initial-law cost-reduction target. 22
23   Every returned policy has a support-capped one-sweep all-state loss account that          23

24   includes the old-reference continuation term. The supplement does not substitute          24

25   its local greedy gap for final Bellman loss.                                              25

17              1.8. M7 and B7: Learned structure under uncertain action exposure              17

18                                                                                             18

19       The R56 sample-split learned-direction theorem and its complete R55 structural- 19
20   uncertainty execution are retained without changing their observations. Directions        20

21   are fitted from data; independent validation produces a coordinate confidence box         21

22   for unknown action exposure. The same box qualifies both the nuisance correction          22

23   and the remaining critic comparison. The corrected gate does not use the simulator’s      23

24   true exposure as a hidden oracle.                                                         24

25       The new action-sensitive feature moduli complement that result by making              25

26   the known-law neural projection terms explicit. They do not remove the need               26

27   for a confidence set when the primitive exposure is estimated. The maintained             27

28   unknown-exposure model has known nonlinear drift and action-invariant bounded             28

29   innovation structure; neither edition claims validity for an arbitrary unknown            29

30   transition kernel. The inherited zero count of certified harmful plug-in choices in       30

31   that particular learned-direction catalogue is retained, rather than replaced with the    31

32   much larger count from the different known-perturbation study.                            32

                 1.10. M9 and B9: The economic use of the returned computation
22                                                                                           22

23     The targets now require ten- and twenty-percent reductions from a specified           23

24   installed policy, not merely a decrease in its certificate. All methods are evaluated   24

25   against the same original continuous initial law and nonlinear investment costs. 25
26   The complete gain intervals determine the supported adoption-fee region relative        26

27   to installation.                                                                        27

28     Choosing between two returned methods is a further economic decision. The             28

29   new incremental-adoption account shifts the fresh paired policy-cost interval by the    29

30   replacement fee and computation price times the measured work difference. It states     30

31   both the region supporting adoption and the region ruling it out; the intermediate      31

32   region remains unresolved. This makes the computational procedure part of the           32

2    result.                                                                                   2
