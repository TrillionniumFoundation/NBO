                                            1. INTRODUCTION
24                                                                                                24

25     A continuation approximation matters because it changes an economic decision. 25
26   Its fitting error, the loss of its implemented policy, and the work needed to certify an     26

27   improvement are different objects. This paper develops Neural Bellman Operators              27

28   around that distinction. A construction uses its own fitted future values, returns           28

29   feasible action witnesses, and connects numerical resources to the cost of the policy        29

30   actually implemented.                                                                        30

1        There are two linked numerical contracts in the same controlled economy. Under         1

2    effective primitive moduli, the constructive backend returns a feasible policy with a      2

3    prescribed error account against the original Bellman optimum. Theorems 2 and 3            3

4    retain the construction and its exact continuous original-owner implementation. A          4

5    fitted backend instead proposes actions from learned features. Those proposals be- 5
6    come deployable only after a whole-cell policy certificate is satisfied. A prospective     6

7    service then stops when the complete policy attains a prespecified expected-cost           7

8    target relative to an installed controller. This additional contract neither changes the   8

9    Bellman optimum nor turns an empirical fitting error into an optimality guarantee. 9
10       The trained neural backend has an explicit computational structure. Under the          10

11   model’s scalar uniform innovation, the expectation of a one-hidden-layer ReLU              11

12   continuation is analytic. For the scalar investment action, the integrated objective       12

13   is piecewise quartic, and a finite collection of rationally isolated cubic stationary      13

14   points supplies exact action-lattice witnesses. This produces a terminating action         14

15   search for a fitted nonlinear continuation without invoking another unconverged            15

16   nonconvex optimizer. It is distinct from compiling a native min-plus envelope into         16

17   an identical neural circuit. The verification theorem nevertheless applies to both         17

18   neural and conventional candidates.                                                        18

19       The policy account is implemented on a locally split, non-tensor observation           19

20   partition. A one-state residual and value cache encloses the old policy’s contin- 20
21   uation without a full table of state pairs. A signed sensitivity calculation for the       21

22   smooth installed reference further retains economically useful continuation direc- 22
23   tion. We prove its validity and give explicit state-dimension, leaf-count and horizon      23

24   costs. The original nonlinear investment family has a dimension-uniform smooth- 24
25   reference stability bound, although the work needed for a chosen approximation             25

26   accuracy can still grow with dimension. Every later discontinuous acquired actor is        26

27   handled by the generic cache, not by differentiating away its jumps.                       27

28       The preceding prospective experiment compares trained ReLU, quadratic and              28

29   tree-based Bellman continuations in dimensions two, four and eight and horizons            29

30   two, four and six. The original compiled-witness and tensor-FVI frontends remain           30

31   in the two-dimensional comparison. Each method pays for its own construction,              31

32   verification, inference and durable output. Targets require two- or ten-percent            32
                                 NEURAL BELLMAN OPERATORS                                3

1    expected-cost reductions from installed zero investment. A separately frozen signed- 1
2    sensitivity extension attains both targets in every declared service; its preceding      2

3    cache-only failures remain in the record. The complete-policy costs, nonterminal         3

4    changes and all-in clocks determine what this means economically. They do not            4

5    establish uniform neural superiority: common verification actions often determine        5

6    the same returned policy for several generators, and quadratic FVI is an important       6

7    low-work comparator.                                                                     7

8      A separate sample-split calculation learns approximately null directions under         8

9    uncertain action exposure. The guarantee qualifies both the nuisance correction          9

10   and the remaining Bellman comparison with the same validation confidence box. 10
11   It therefore concerns inferred structure, rather than merely a hand-selected exact       11

12   invariant, while retaining the maintained nonlinear drift and innovation model.          12

13     The additional comparison in Section 12 executes the exact fitted action solver        13

14   inside the prospective service, rather than assigning old cost observations to new       14

15   policies. A nested-candidate design keeps the verifier and shared actions fixed, and     15

16   genuinely different training seeds produce matched fitted objects for the menu and       16

17   exact-search modes. Theorem 12 identifies how center optimization loss enters full- 17
18   action Bellman accuracy; Proposition 14 supplies explicit action-sensitive acquisition   18

19   and evaluation moduli. The experiment then separately asks whether the additional        19

20   witness changes a certified decision and whether its returned policy has a lower         20

21   actual economic cost.                                                                    21

                                       13. CONCLUSION
14                                                                                             14

15     Neural Bellman Operators connect an own-future continuation to a feasible               15

16   action and an economic cost account. The constructive backend retains the original        16

17   Bellman-accuracy objective. The trained backend now has analytic innovation               17

18   integration and a terminating exact action-lattice search, while its proposals remain     18

19   subject to whole-cell true-advantage verification. These are complementary parts          19

20   of the same numerical method, not interchangeable claims about the name of a              20

21   function class.                                                                           21

22     The non-tensor cache and signed reference calculation turn the policy account           22

23   into an executable prospective service. Their proofs retain the cost of leaf member- 23
24   ship, critic evaluation, state dimension and horizon. The declared expected-cost          24

25   targets are attained in the sensitivity experiment, with genuine nonterminal action       25

26   changes and complete own-service clocks. Conventional generators share the same           26

27   verification treatment, and policy identities make their role observable. The finite ex- 27
28   periments identify successful returns and their costs; they do not establish universal    28

29   neural dominance or calibrated welfare effects.                                           29

30     The full-action gap, the actual expected cost and the price of acquiring or verifying   30

31   a policy remain separate economic quantities. Keeping all three explicit permits a        31

32   decision maker to choose the appropriate contract without treating fitting accuracy,      32
                                      NEURAL BELLMAN OPERATORS                                    53

1    certificate sharpness or a favorable timing observation as a substitute for the cost of           1

2    the implemented policy.                                                                           2

3      The new action-search execution completes another part of that chain. Exact                     3

4    rational witnesses eliminate the stored critic’s lattice optimization loss, and the               4

5    transfer theorem identifies the remaining acquisition, evaluation, continuous-cover               5

6    and verification obligations. The nested-candidate comparison makes a stricter                    6

7    certified upper endpoint attributable to an added witness under a fixed verifier. 7
8    New prospective services and independent returned-policy contrasts determine                      8

9    the actual economic consequences. Neither exact fitted minimization nor a tighter                 9

10   upper endpoint is substituted for those contrasts. The complete records retain all                10

11   modes, seeds, targets, attempted refinements and computational costs.                             11
