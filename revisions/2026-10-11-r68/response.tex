We thank the referee for recognizing the state-lattice-free policy
certificate and for identifying the additional evidence needed to assess
the trained selector. We have revised the same Neural Bellman Operators
paper. The title, own-future continuation construction, feasible
witnesses, original Bellman benchmark and broader historical development
are retained. The active article now connects a computable query
decision to a certificate-aware training loss, and evaluates stronger
controls, prospective reuse, actual continuous-law policy costs and the
previously specified two-control extension.

The controlling report is the advisory review on branch
\nolinkurl{review/econometrica-numerical-methods-r65-2026-10-10-34a5ce1},
commit \nolinkurl{d548a97f461160296239ed6a973d4a91ef9d1fad}. It reviews R65
commit \nolinkurl{34a5ce17dc3ac4681b6d004eca00637f28104e28}. We also
reviewed the subsequently deposited R66 protocol and execution rather
than treating R65 as the last repository work. R66 is not relabeled as
an integrated paper or an external acceptance decision.

That review uncovered a numerical enclosure error in R66. The cumulative
path-cost interval was initialized with aliased lower and upper arrays.
In-place updates overwrote its running lower endpoint. An independent
rational trajectory falls below a saved lower endpoint, so the issue is
substantive even though it is small in magnitude. We retained the
original records, repaired the accumulator, committed and froze the
corrected sources, and reran the entire 212-process catalogue in R67.
The fixed random indices are unchanged to isolate the correction; their
repetition does not add independent observations. The active cost
intervals and complete timings come from the corrected run, with the
original R66 evidence and the counterexample preserved.

\hypertarget{b1-m1-the-nbo-contribution-and-the-role-of-learning}{%
\subsection{B1, M1: The NBO contribution and the role of
learning}\label{b1-m1-the-nbo-contribution-and-the-role-of-learning}}

The revised article distinguishes the constructive own-future ReLU
continuation from the trained query selector and from the method-neutral
verifier. It does not infer neural computational superiority from an
exact ReLU encoding. The original operator, construction,
witness-preserving compiler and acquired-policy statements remain in the
active theorem chain, with their arguments preserved in the supplement
and the complete earlier editions.

The positive new contribution is operational. A strong-convexity upper
chord and a global action-search lower bound can certify an actual
proposed action before a new Bellman query. A margin loss bounds the
endpoint work left unresolved by the proposal. A fixed-feature ReLU
readout can minimize a convex certificate-aware objective with a
directly computable optimization-gap certificate. These additions do not
rely on successful nonconvex hidden-layer training or on the unknown
location of the optimal action. They develop the existing NBO question
rather than substituting a different paper or claiming exclusivity for
generic safe improvement.

\hypertarget{b2-b3-m2-strong-conventional-controls-and-complete-cold-start-work}{%
\subsection{B2, B3, M2: Strong conventional controls and complete
cold-start
work}\label{b2-b3-m2-strong-conventional-controls-and-complete-cold-start-work}}

The source-frozen comparison contains ten selectors with the same
original-law kernel, pre-query screen, acquired action rule and accuracy
target. The fitted controls now include full quadratic, linear, additive
piecewise-linear spline and nearest-action predictors. Unfitted controls
include adaptive parabolic search, bisection, direct minimization of the
upper chord, an analytic terminal-derivative proposal and a midpoint
rule. The analytic derivative is only a proposal at earlier dates; it is
not treated as their true optimal action.

Every fitted selector receives the same ninety-six action-label states
for a given task and seed. Eight predetermined seeds and two clock
repetitions produce the complete comparison catalogue. Training labels,
fitting, imports, prediction, queries, path enclosures, output
durability and process shutdown are all charged. The main comparison
table reports complete process times rather than presenting an online
query saving as a cold-start win. The supplement retains every fitting
seed, optimizer warning and actual query count. Closely spaced clocks
are not interpreted as statistically identified runtime differences.

The new upper-chord screen is offered to the conventional methods as
well as the neural selector. It is therefore possible to assess
prediction against a stronger analytic control, not merely against a
deliberately weak initialization. Earlier conventional wins and the
twenty worse pure ReLU policies remain in their original files and
companion manuscripts. The current evidence is not used to rewrite those
results.

\hypertarget{b4-m3-a-computable-training-to-query-connection}{%
\subsection{B4, M3: A computable training-to-query
connection}\label{b4-m3-a-computable-training-to-query-connection}}

The earlier localization proposition contains an unknown action error
and a derivative at the unknown optimum. It is retained as an analytical
result, but the new section \textbf{Operational prediction certificates
and readout training} supplies a different, computable condition.

For consecutive queried actions \(l<u\), verified upper endpoints and a
primitive strong-convexity bound define a quadratic upper chord \(C\).
If the outward value of \(C\) at the actual rounded proposal minus the
existing global lower bound is at most the local tolerance, the proposal
is certified without a new endpoint query. Its proof uses only the
original objective's strong convexity and valid endpoints. A failed
screen leaves the protected-refinement and full-policy guarantees
intact.

The score is then used to define a margin loss. If a failed screen can
require at most \(K(z)\) further endpoint queries in context \(z\), the
additional count is bounded by \(K(z)\) times the clipped squared margin
loss. This is a deterministic statement for every specified context
catalogue. A separate finite-family validation theorem gives an
expected-work bound under an explicitly declared, independent,
predictor-independent context law. Recursively generated on-policy
contexts are not assumed to satisfy that law merely because they are
stored in a transcript.

The fixed-feature ReLU construction goes further. Nonnegative
partition-of-unity features and a box readout produce a feasible action
inside a fixed context interval. The squared positive-part certificate
surplus, weighted by the fallback work cap, is convex in the readout.
Its achieved objective bounds unresolved work, and its box dual gap
bounds the optimization error. The deposited exact-rational
implementation returns both quantities after finitely many
conditional-gradient iterations. Exact tests include nonvacuous
pre-query closure and successful certificate-aware fitting examples.

This constructive readout is distinct from unrestricted hidden-layer
learning. The benchmark's L-BFGS models are not relabeled as minimizers
of the new objective. Their action-label mean squared errors, warnings
and actual certificate progress remain separate empirical records. The
theorem therefore supplies the requested operational connection without
asserting an unexecuted population generalization result for the
benchmark fits.

\hypertarget{b5-m4-prospective-cached-reuse-including-failure-handling}{%
\subsection{B5, M4: Prospective cached reuse, including failure
handling}\label{b5-m4-prospective-cached-reuse-including-failure-handling}}

The new reuse catalogue fits once, writes an identified model and loads
it for twenty-four predetermined new workloads. ReLU and quadratic seeds
6603 and 6607 were fixed in the protocol. Adaptive and bisection
controls receive the same workload sequence. All model reads, digest
checks, identity checks, predictions, verifier calls and durable outputs
are charged.

At block index 7, the loaded acquisition-contract identifier is
deliberately stale. The service rejects reuse and executes the
conventional adaptive verifier. The stored model is not corrupted or
silently repaired. The failed reuse and fallback are part of both the
transcript and the complete measured process. This replaces a
hypothetical reuse allowance by an executed failure path.

The supplementary table reports the first observed cumulative crossing
against the matched adaptive repetition, a crossing that persists within
the remaining observed window, and the final complete-process
comparison. We distinguish a prefix clock ending at a durable block from
a process clock ending after shutdown. No prefix is called the complete
cost of a stopping experiment that was not run. All declared seeds and
repetitions are retained, including reversals and noncrossings; no
amortization beyond the observed twenty-four blocks is extrapolated.

\hypertarget{b6-m5-actual-continuous-law-costs-of-the-callable-controllers}{%
\subsection{B6, M5: Actual continuous-law costs of the callable
controllers}\label{b6-m5-actual-continuous-law-costs-of-the-callable-controllers}}

The corrected inference services evaluate the actual adaptive,
bisection, ReLU and quadratic callable controllers at every date. They
do not substitute an old tensor policy, a fixed-action rollout or a
deterministic workload average. The fitted seed is fixed before
evaluation. Initial states are uniform on the full cube and the
innovations retain their original continuous laws.

Forty-eight-bit bins enclose complete continuous initial and shock
coordinates. The acquisition rule is checked on the propagated state
interval. Any ambiguous acquisition receives a full remaining-cost
support enclosure rather than deletion or midpoint substitution.
Marginal cost and paired difference intervals use outward endpoint
samples, signed path supports and the stated empirical-Bernstein
inequality. All four absolute costs and six differences at both tasks
belong to one simultaneous family with error at most \(1/100\).

There are 8,192 distinct common-path rows per task, hence 16,384 across
the two tasks. Four method arms and repeated R66/R67 streams do not
multiply this count. The random seed binds reproducibility; independent
bin sampling is the explicitly stated statistical model. The repaired
execution and independent path checks are prerequisites for using the
intervals, not cosmetic postprocessing.

The current paired intervals remain unresolved in sign. The manuscript
reports this finding rather than turning uniform accuracy or a tighter
certificate into an identified expected-cost improvement. The requested
direct endpoint is now measured for the new policies, and its
limitations are visible in the full contrast table.

\hypertarget{b7-m6-training-variation-and-numerical-variability}{%
\subsection{B7, M6: Training variation and numerical
variability}\label{b7-m6-training-variation-and-numerical-variability}}

Eight fitting seeds replace the earlier two-seed design. Each has two
separate complete-process timings; the latter are not counted as
independent training initializations. The frozen catalogue retains all
warnings, losses, model identities, query counts, screen outcomes and
complete times. A warning does not authorize removing a run or selecting
a different trained model.

The resulting evidence measures variation across the declared training
seeds. It is not an optimizer-convergence probability for a population
of initializations. The constructive readout proposition supplies a
different guarantee: the achieved finite optimization certificate for
the fixed features and contexts actually used. This distinction prevents
numerical repetition from being used as a substitute for either a
training theorem or an independent statistical sample.

\hypertarget{m8-direct-progress-of-routed-and-screened-proposals}{%
\subsection{M8: Direct progress of routed and screened
proposals}\label{m8-direct-progress-of-routed-and-screened-proposals}}

The scientific kernel records old and new global lower endpoints, old
and new upper witnesses, the actual queried action, the query interval
and the routing indicator for every refinement. The audit checks that
upper and lower progress sum to gap reduction and that the maintained
bounds do not move in the wrong direction. It separately counts routed
queries with no progress, upper-only progress, lower-only progress,
simultaneous progress and successful screens without a new query.

The displayed progress table counts each distinct transcript once. Both
original clock repetitions remain available. We do not infer distance to
the true minimizing action from action-label error, nor do we claim a
query was useful solely because it was routed. These diagnostics make
the computational mechanism observable on the same original model and
tolerance.

\hypertarget{b8-m7-more-controls-innovations-and-horizon}{%
\subsection{B8, M7: More controls, innovations and
horizon}\label{b8-m7-more-controls-innovations-and-horizon}}

The manuscript gives a proved simplicial query for the already specified
two-control, shared-capacity investment extension. A semiconcavity
minorant is formed on each action triangle. Its lower bound is computed
by an outward supporting plane, so an approximate quadratic minimizer
has no certificate authority. Verified feasible upper witnesses and a
continuous simplex cover give the same local-gap premise as the scalar
policy theorem. The proof includes two-innovation quadrature and finite
rational recovery under the explicit precision slack.

The executed vector catalogue covers state dimensions two and eight and
horizons two, three and four, with two controls and two independent
continuous innovations. No state lattice or stored reference policy is
loaded. Query counts, descendants, batch size, rational recovery and
process memory are reported. The four designed initial states per
service are identified as scaling paths, not as an estimate of
population expected cost. The much larger horizon-four work is retained.

This is a multivariate-control extension of the verified NBO backend,
not a trained two-output selector comparison. The work recursion
includes the \(q^2\) descendant factor. Batching is not mislabeled as
depth-first memory or as elimination of the continuation tree.

\hypertarget{b9-m9-economic-purchase-rather-than-certificate-ranking}{%
\subsection{B9, M9: Economic purchase rather than certificate
ranking}\label{b9-m9-economic-purchase-rather-than-certificate-ranking}}

The purchase account now has direct cost intervals for the new callable
policies and actual complete receipts for both cold start and reuse. For
a candidate's expected gain interval \([l,u]\), measured work difference
\(\Delta W\), normalized value \(v\), compute price \(p\) and adoption
fee \(f\), the net-benefit interval is
\([vl-p\Delta W-f,vu-p\Delta W-f]\). Its positive lower endpoint
licenses purchase; a negative upper endpoint rejects it; otherwise the
choice remains unresolved.

A purchaser who requires only the stated uniform accuracy contract can
instead select the least measured complete work among eligible catalogue
services. That is a different decision from claiming an identified
expected-cost gain. The paper keeps the two criteria distinct and
reports normalized model quantities, not an invented empirical
calibration of hardware, installation costs or welfare.
