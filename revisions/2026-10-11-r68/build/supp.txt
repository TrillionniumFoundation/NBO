                    2. NUMERICAL ACCOUNTING AND INDEPENDENT CHECKS                                 19

20     The source-frozen experiment preceding this revision used a cumulative path- 20
21   cost interval initialized by a point constructor. That constructor can share its lower        21

22   and upper arrays. The subsequent statements assigning a new lower endpoint and                22

23   then a new upper endpoint therefore left both running endpoints equal to the latter. 23
24   This is an enclosure error even when its magnitude is small. It cannot be justified           24

25   by the statistical interval being much wider.                                                 25

26     For the first designed zero-state path in the two-state, two-date adaptive compari- 26
27   son, the original archived cost lower endpoint is approximately 2.6744468902315184. 27
28   An independently evaluated exact rational midpoint trajectory has cost approxi- 28
29   mately 2.6744468902315166, below that endpoint. The complete rational value, raw              29

30   file hash, action sequence and original endpoint are recorded in the erratum audit. 30
31   The midpoint is used here only to refute the claimed numerical enclosure; it is not           31

32   used as an exact expectation under the continuous law.                                        32
