                                    1.2. Feasibility at boundaries
13                                                                                            13

14       The capacity c( x ) = 1/8 + ∑ j x j /(8d) is affine and nondecreasing. Barycentric   14

15   combinations of feasible nodal controls are feasible at the interpolated state because   15

16   their summed controls are bounded by the same barycentric combination of ca- 16
17   pacities. The tests include vertices, faces, equality at capacity and two-coordinate     17

18   controls. Downward state acquisition preserves the ordering of capacities. The           18

19   implemented query action is rounded downward on the thirty-two-bit lattice before        19

20   its original-law endpoint is evaluated. The numerical certificate therefore belongs      20

21   to the action actually used.                                                             21

22       For a truncated capacity endpoint c, the omitted interval is charged by Λ(c −        22

23   c). No lower envelope is permitted to ignore that interval. Projected derivative         23

24   certification at zero and at capacity uses the appropriate one-sided optimality sign;    24

25   a constrained optimum need not have derivative zero. The example f ( a) = a shows        25

26   why a boundary prediction-error term can be first order even though an interior          26

27   one is second order.                                                                     27

                             1.3. Localization and corrupt-record tests                       29

30       The new rational tests compute exact parabolic lower bounds for linear and           30

31   quadratic objectives, including both boundary minimizers and an interior mini- 31
32   mizer. They check the certificate-localization bound with endpoint uncertainty, a        32

1    shortened capacity interval, all three parabolic-minimum cases and the running- 1
2    bound identity. A constant objective with an exact optimal witness and a deliberately      2

3    loose curvature allowance provides a useful counterexample: prediction accuracy            3

4    alone does not close a coarse lower certificate.                                           4

5      The trajectory checker is tested by separately corrupting an acquired state, a           5

6    returned action, a reported local gap, an exact path cost and the requested target. 6
7    Each corrupted object is rejected. This verifies that the replay is checking economic      7

8    implementation and accuracy fields rather than only accepting a success flag. It is        8

9    not a claim that these five mutations exhaust all possible implementation faults.          9

                       2. FINITE-PRECISION AND WORKLOAD CONTRACTS
11                                                                                              11

12     A service receives the dimension, horizon, target, workload size, mode, fitting          12

13   seed and repetition number. Those fields are bound to its summary and parent re- 13
14   ceipt. Every scientific source freeze is checked before replay, including the imported     14

15   original primitives. Each returned trace contains true initial and innovation dyadic       15

16   indices, acquired states, actions, original-law lower and upper endpoints, local gaps      16

17   and probe counts. Models, exact costs, traces, summaries and clocks have separate          17

18   SHA-256 identities.                                                                        18

19     The local request is the recorded binary64 rounding of ϵ/(2 ∑t<T      βt ). The global   19

20   policy allowance uses this actual local request and a rational acquisition expansion       20

21   before upward conversion. For every declared target and horizon, the indepen- 21
22   dent suite checks the outward direction and the final comparison with ϵ. It also           22

23   checks the recovery condition δr > 8Λr 2−32 at the smallest descendant request. A          23

24   fixed-precision failure cannot be reclassified as a valid certificate because another      24

25   workload previously returned.                                                              25

26     The finite return theorem separates real-arithmetic refinement from executable           26

27   recovery. In the floating implementation, a failed width guard or refinement cap           27

28   invokes exact-rational enumeration, with separately counted queries. That proce- 28
29   dure refines only the required descendants and never loads a conventional full-state       29

30   policy. Its rational bit complexity can differ substantially from the floating operation   30

31   count. No recovery was observed in the declared workloads; the manuscript does             31

32   not infer that recovery is costless or unnecessary in general.                             32

                        4. STATISTICAL AND ECONOMIC INTERPRETATION
8                                                                                            8

9
       The workload generation rule and named boundary replacements are fixed in             9

10
     each source-frozen protocol. Its dyadic average has an exact reproducible value but     10

11
     is not an independent continuous-law estimate. The earlier bounded-sample cost          11

12
     intervals remain attached to their original frozen policies and their declared simulta- 12
13
     neous families (Maurer and Pontil, 2009). The query-service catalogue adds neither      13

14
     new confidence intervals for expected gains nor additional optimizer replications       14

15
     to that earlier study.                                                                  15

16
       A complete cold-start time is a directly observed service cost on the recorded        16

17
     machine. A median of two conventional runs or four learned runs is descriptive. 17
18
     Fixed affinity and single-thread configuration remove some sources of variation         18

19
     but do not control CPU frequency, system interference or another platform. We           19

20
     therefore retain all clocks, logical operation counts and process memory, and make      20

21
     no hardware-uniform timing probability claim.                                           21

22
       For a decision maker with a declared dollar conversion per model-loss unit, a         22

23
     price per computation second and a deployment fee, an independently valid gain          23

24
     interval can be converted to a monetary purchase interval as shown in the article. 24
25
     A uniform regret bound instead supports an accuracy constraint. An accuracy             25

26
     constraint is not a measured welfare gain. Likewise, a conditional arithmetic break- 26
27
     even workload count does not establish that an unobserved future distribution will      27

28
     produce the same query savings.                                                         28
