The new mechanism work is specified before execution:

1. Prove action-contrast and directional first-order bounds for NBO proposals,
   including constrained actions and nested original-law risk. Distinguish a
   true action gap, its validated upper bound, and an empirical selection loss.
2. Give an explicit decision-theoretic realization of the existing oblique
   continuation example. Benefits are 1+(x1+x2-1)_+, states are uniform on the
   unit square and observed prices uniform on [0,3]. Decisions minimize the
   original purchase cost. Compare additive functions, total-degree-two
   polynomials, an oblique ReLU, and its identical conventional hinge encoding.
   Use exact rational integration/projection, not selected simulation draws.
   Retain equality of equivalent encodings and charge their same information.
3. Extend continuous-law information analysis to known nonuniform densities,
   distinguishing deterministic intervals, pathwise-sound randomized
   intervals, and mean-square randomized point estimation. No randomized
   matching rate or multivariate tractability result is presumed.
4. Independently implement and test the new finite-dimensional acquisition
   and decision identities. Production and independent paths must not import
   one another's fitting or integration routines. Exact arithmetic is used
   where claimed; floating-point regression is not a proof.
5. Package the complete executable source dependency closure and rebuild the
   article, supplement and retained development companion. Record source/PDF
   hashes and actual test/build outcomes; tests and compilation are not a
   claim of acceptance by Econometrica or independent proof certification.

The exact benchmark is a theoretical economic mechanism, not a calibration of
the existing investment application. No new population claim for the R97 or
