\begin{abstract}This supplement gives complete proofs of own-future construction, reference-centered risk qualification, continuous investment and decision-scale precision. It adds the action-support and prospective-selection arguments, nonuniform-law enclosures, explicitly quantified randomized information bounds, and exact economic decision separation. Complete prior expectation, signed-risk and uniform-information proofs remain in the Development Appendix.\end{abstract}
