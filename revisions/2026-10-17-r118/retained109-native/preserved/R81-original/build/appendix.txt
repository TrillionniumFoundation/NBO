       A prediction of the optimal action and a prediction of a useful numerical query are
6                                                                                                    6
     different objects. Inserting a learned action before examining an already available
7                                                                                                    7
     certificate charges an extra continuation query even when the two boundary actions
8                                                                                                    8
     suffice. We instead make the demand for another query a mathematical decision and
9                                                                                                    9
     let a trained NBO selector determine its location only after that demand has been
10                                                                                                   10
     established. The underlying continuation recursion and the economic optimum are
11                                                                                                   11
     unchanged.
12                                                                                                   12
       At a recursive state, start with the two feasible action endpoints. Compute the
13                                                                                                   13
     global lower bound from the parabolic minorants of Lemma 13, including the
14                                                                                                   14
     capacity remainder, and the smallest upper endpoint. Return immediately when
15                                                                                                   15
     their difference is at most the requested tolerance. Otherwise choose a partition
16                                                                                                   16
     interval with smallest current minorant. A learned action is used only if it lies in
17                                                                                                   17
     the central half of that interval; it then replaces the conventional proposed split.
18                                                                                                   18
     Outside that region the safeguarded parabolic split is used. The rounded split must
19                                                                                                   19
     pass the one-fifth interior check before evaluation. A prediction is evaluated at most
20                                                                                                   20
     once at each recursive state and is reused only as a proposed location. It supplies
21                                                                                                   21
     neither a value endpoint nor a lower bound.
