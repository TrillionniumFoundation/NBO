\begin{abstract}
This focused supplement gives the complete construction, complete-policy, signed-risk, reference-centered-risk, continuous investment, and decision-resolution proofs used by the article. It adds proofs of the deterministic adaptive information lower bound, exact representation comparisons, verified readout improvement, and the workload-consistent purchase frontier. Intervening historical proofs are preserved as complete sections in the Numerical and Development Appendix.
\end{abstract}
