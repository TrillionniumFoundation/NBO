This appendix retains the economic-inference, safeguarded-selection, localization, certified-readout, deployment, recursive-query and vector-control sections without textual deletion. It records their original scientific evidence and independent-verification boundaries. New exact action-switch duals and first-disagreement proofs establish the optimizer-class cost transfer. The complete prospective factorial catalogue reports all ninety-four original-law cost estimands, precision components and construction-work boundaries. Core proofs remain in the Technical Supplement; the original broader applications remain in their unchanged companion editions.
