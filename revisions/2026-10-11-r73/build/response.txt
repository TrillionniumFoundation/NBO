                 1.4. M3 / B5. Validation independence and adaptive deployment
21                                                                                              21

22     The retained study separates predictor-independent validation contexts from              22

23   contexts visited during deployment, and the intervening deployment theorem                 23

24   conditions on the actual history. R73 adds the corresponding distinction for policy- 24
25   cost qualification. Before an episode, its complete controller pair, continuation and      25

26   support must be fixed given the preceding history. The continuation can change             26

27   predictably between episodes without changing the expected original-law cost               27

28   difference of a fixed controller pair. Within an episode it remains frozen for the         28

29   telescoping identity.                                                                      29

30     The new cost theorem does not require independent, identically distributed               30

31   deployment contexts. It bounds the average conditional expected cost difference            31

32   under the stated conditional laws. That average equals one fixed policy-cost contrast      32
     4

1    only when the complete controllers, initial law and reset contract stay fixed. A            1

2    program with a cache carried between episodes can have a changing conditional               2

3    target despite an unchanged code identifier. The manuscript and ledger therefore            3

4    require a new error-allocated epoch for a changed fixed target. History-dependent           4

5    controller memory must be included in the policy description and in the uniform             5

6    greedy-gap qualification. Neither changing contexts nor reused validation data are          6

7    silently treated as new independent evidence for the final controller.                      7

16                     1.10. M9 / B9. The economic decision and its units                  16

17                                                                                         17

18     The economic object remains the actual feasible investment controller and the       18

19   cost of constructing, qualifying and replacing it. The new stopping theorem per- 19
20   mits a well-defined decision: stop qualification when the upper incremental-cost      20

21   endpoint, plus an explicitly priced setup charge per declared deployment and a        21

22   per-deployment charge, is negative. Equivalence at a specified loss band is a sep- 22
23   arate decision from strict improvement. New controller pairs can be admitted in       23

24   conditionally budgeted epochs.                                                        24

25     Measured processor seconds are not silently assigned a monetary value. The          25

26   deployment count over which setup is amortized is not automatically the num- 26
27   ber of qualification episodes. A cached controller does not inherit the signed cost   27

28   interval of a different fresh-search policy simply because both have the same re- 28
29   gret allowance. These conditions make the original adoption question operational      29

30   without inventing a calibrated installation fee, an estimated welfare effect or a     30

31   specifically neural purchase recommendation. The existing economic-choice and         31

32   information-acquisition results remain unchanged in the current reading set.          32
