23                                         1. INTRODUCTION                                      23

24                                                                                              24
       A numerical continuation matters because it helps produce an economic decision.
25                                                                                              25
     The error of a fitted value, the loss of the implemented policy and the resources
26                                                                                              26
     required to verify that loss are different objects. A policy may improve without a
27                                                                                              27
     tighter numerical certificate; a tighter certificate may leave the policy unchanged. A
28                                                                                              28
     useful computational method must connect its approximation and resource choices
29                                                                                              29
     to a feasible decision under the original economic law.

1        This paper develops Neural Bellman Operators (NBOs) along that chain. The                1

2    constructive implementation evaluates a continuation against its own fitted future           2

3    and retains feasible action witnesses. Its exact affine–ReLU realization and primitive       3

4    error budget do not require a theorem that unrestricted nonconvex training succeeds. 4
5    A complementary recursive implementation queries the original Bellman problem                5

6    only where its action search requires information. A trained readout proposes                6

7    actions; verified lower and upper bounds determine whether the actual policy                 7

8    meets its original-optimum contract.                                                         8

9        The nonlinear investment economy has state-dependent capacity, bilinear tran- 9
10   sitions and one or two independent continuous innovations. Primitive curvature               10

11   controls the convexity defect of the transition and supplies regularity of the true          11

12   Bellman value. The action certificate can therefore dispense with a stored state             12

13   lattice. A strong-convexity upper chord sometimes certifies a rounded action be- 13
14   fore another endpoint query. Protected conventional refinement remains available             14

15   when a proposal fails. The certificate-aware fixed-feature readout and its optimiza- 15
16   tion gap make the resulting training problem constructive. Neither a fitted neural           16

17   continuation nor an arbitrary proposed action is assumed convex.                             17

18       Removing a state lattice does not remove the cost of evaluating a future. A first        18

19   original-value query can generate a large descendant tree. Independently evaluating          19

20   optimal advantages for precise policy-cost inference can generate another such               20

21   tree. We address these two costs separately, while retaining the original operator,          21

22   economic law and optimum.                                                                    22

23       First, a frozen fitted NBO continuation can replace the optimal value in the cost        23

24   observation. Its Bellman-residual oscillations and implemented greedy gaps control           24

25   both the observation’s range and the actual policy loss. A terminal correction makes         25

26   the identity valid even when the fitted terminal value is inexact. State-independent         26

27   continuation shifts cancel. For shallow affine–ReLU continuations, the original              27

28   one- and two-innovation conditional expectations are finite positive-part moment             28

29   calculations. An explicit state-and-action-cover construction qualifies their residuals      29

30   without querying the optimal Bellman tree. This is an approximation, integration             30

31   and certification result, not a claim that a fitted critic is already accurate because its   31

32   training loss is small.                                                                      32

1      Second, a lower original-Bellman bound and its feasible upper witness can be            1

2    transported to a nearby state. The rule pays for state displacement, capacity repair      2

3    and action rounding before deciding whether the old certificate closes at the new         3

4    tolerance. A failed check invokes the original recursive verifier. The policy guarantee   4

5    holds after history-dependent selection among valid records, without assuming             5

6    equality with the fresh-search policy. A finite-cover theorem states the tighter fill     6

7    precision and retention conditions needed to bound the number of expensive fills. 7
8    The executed bounded cache is evaluated by its actual counts, rather than by              8

9    premises it does not satisfy.                                                             9

10     These additions distinguish three uses of a neural object: a constructed continua- 10
11   tion, an optional query proposal and a proposed record for retrieval. Their economic      11

12   value is not identified by calling a common correctness argument neural. The neural       12

13   continuation now enters a computable original-law evaluation identity; the pro- 13
14   posal and retrieval mechanisms can change work but cannot manufacture a value             14

15   bound. Exact ReLU encodings and conventional piecewise-linear interpretations             15

16   remain related representations, not independent algorithms with an automatic              16

17   ranking.                                                                                  17

18     The retained frozen study executes certificate-aware ReLU and equally sized             18

19   Bernstein readouts, independent-context validation, sixty-four-block reuse, direct        19

20   continuous-law policy-cost inference and trained two-control comparisons. Its             20

21   twelve scalar cost contrasts meet the prespecified equivalence margin, but none           21

22   identifies strict superiority. Its complete cold-start evidence does not support a        22

23   uniform neural work advantage. The new predeclared experiment compares fresh              23

24   calculation, exact-key reuse and geometrically transported witnesses on the same          24

25   localized query workloads, including a four-date, two-control stress case. It counts      25

26   every original-law descendant on a miss and every rejected lookup, invalidation           26

27   and durable record. The workload is not relabeled as independent policy-cost data. 27

                                       13. CONCLUSION
22                                                                                             22

23     Neural Bellman Operators connect the construction of a continuation to feasible         23

24   decisions under the original economic law. The witness-producing implementation           24

25   has an explicit own-future error account. Recursive action verification permits           25

26   trained proposals without allowing fitting error to alter the policy certificate. The     26

27   present additions place a frozen neural continuation directly in original-law policy- 27
28   cost evaluation and permit purchased original-value witnesses to be reused after a        28

29   verified capacity repair.                                                                 29

30     The resulting resource distinctions are substantive. Exact conditional moments          30

31   remove optimal-value descendants from the evaluation of a qualified shallow               31

32   continuation, but fitting and uniform residual qualification remain paid operations. 32
                                       NEURAL BELLMAN OPERATORS                                              43

1    Transported witnesses remove descendants on a certified cache hit, but the first fill                         1

2    and every later miss retain their original horizon cost. A history-dependent reuse                            2

3    controller retains a loss guarantee; it is not automatically the same policy used in an                       3

4    earlier independent cost experiment.                                                                          4

5      The retained cost-inference evidence identifies equivalence at a declared numeri- 5
6    cal margin, not strict superiority. The new query experiment tests localized reuse,                           6

7    not universal neural speed or calibrated welfare. Together with the constructive                              7

8    theorems, these results identify what can be certified, reused and economically                               8

9    compared, and what resources each step requires. They extend the original NBO                                 9

10   policy-evaluation and improvement objective while preserving every historical                                 10

11   application, assumption and unfavorable comparison for renewed scrutiny. The                                  11

12   qualification-optimal readout gives a direct link from an attained fitting objective                          12

13   to a uniform NBO cost and regret certificate. Its exact dual distinguishes verified                           13

14   optimization accuracy from a solver’s status. Predictable original-law cost intervals                         14

15   allow qualification to stop when a declared economic criterion is met, while keep- 15
16   ing fixed-policy and adaptive-deployment targets separate. Both additions use the                             16

17   original investment primitives and pay for approximation, uniform qualification                               17

18   and numerical evaluation; neither infers a population ranking from a better training                          18

19   objective.                                                                                                    19
