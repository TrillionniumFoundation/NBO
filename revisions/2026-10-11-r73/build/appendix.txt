       A prediction of the optimal action and a prediction of a useful numerical query are
6                                                                                                    6
     different objects. Inserting a learned action before examining an already available
7                                                                                                    7
     certificate charges an extra continuation query even when the two boundary actions
8                                                                                                    8
     suffice. We instead make the demand for another query a mathematical decision and
9                                                                                                    9
     let a trained NBO selector determine its location only after that demand has been
10                                                                                                   10
     established. The underlying continuation recursion and the economic optimum are
11                                                                                                   11
     unchanged.
12                                                                                                   12
       At a recursive state, start with the two feasible action endpoints. Compute the
13                                                                                                   13
     global lower bound from the parabolic minorants of Lemma 6, including the capac-
14                                                                                                   14
     ity remainder, and the smallest upper endpoint. Return immediately when their
15                                                                                                   15
     difference is at most the requested tolerance. Otherwise choose a partition interval
16                                                                                                   16
     with smallest current minorant. A learned action is used only if it lies in the central
17                                                                                                   17
     half of that interval; it then replaces the conventional proposed split. Outside that
18                                                                                                   18
     region the safeguarded parabolic split is used. The rounded split must pass the
19                                                                                                   19
     one-fifth interior check before evaluation. A prediction is evaluated at most once at
20                                                                                                   20
     each recursive state and is reused only as a proposed location. It supplies neither a
21                                                                                                   21
     value endpoint nor a lower bound.
