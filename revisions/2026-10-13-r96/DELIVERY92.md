## Substantive development

A reference-centered conditional risk operator retains the dual penalty generated by the actual stored NBO continuation. State-dependent subsolutions and supersolutions compare complete implemented policies with the original Bellman optimum, covering every original feasible action in the lower certificate. Exact state-dependent recursions recover the original values and are no looser than the inherited signed scalar account. The shared parameter and entire continuation sequence are fixed before a class guarantee is taken.

For a finite exact affine risk representation, the paper supplies an exact penalized risk-occupation identity and rational primal/dual witnesses. The occupations are risk distorted, not physical probabilities. A strict support-function certificate excludes risk pieces over a previously verified successor-error set; equality and unverified cases are retained. Full proofs treat risk-kernel changes, quantile atoms, the entropic identity, zero discounts, state-dependent bias and the occupation-sensitive precision budget.

The two-state absorbing example makes the refinement strict: the scalar account is the horizon, while the state-dependent loss certificate is zero. This is an exact mathematical example, not a measured investment gain. The original preference objective is never replaced to obtain a favorable bound. Basic conditional risk duality, dynamic programming and weak duality are attributed; the cited 2010 risk paper's 2014 erratum is included, with measurable recursion and selection hypotheses explicit.
