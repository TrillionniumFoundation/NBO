26   computed and which decisions can be justified under the original law. Neural               26

4        Our first contribution is constructive. Each dated NBO is formed against its own    4

5    already attained future, with explicit primitive state and action covers, integration   5

6    allowances and feasible action owners. The affine–ReLU realization does not assume      6

7    successful unrestricted nonconvex training. Original-owner compilation preserves        7

8    the continuation and its feasible witness while changing evaluation work. Joint- 8
9    domain and common-readout extensions retain the coupling between decisions at           9

10   different acquired states. Their hypotheses and full statements remain available in     10

11   the accompanying material.                                                              11

12       Our second contribution is an original-law qualification account for complete       12

13   policies. Under recursive risk, centering the original conditional dual at the stored   13

14   continuation retains the penalty for changing the maximizing risk density. State- 14
15   dependent subsolutions and supersolutions then compare the implemented policy           15

16   with the original optimum. The continuous investment construction derives the           16

17   required curvature, value supports and all-action inequalities from the original        17

18   technology. Feasible risk witnesses minorize the lower action problem, and full-law     18

19   envelopes dominate the policy value. These inequalities are valid for any sup- 19
20   plied continuation satisfying their assumptions. Neural construction, representation    20

21   quality, certificate validity and computational gain are therefore separate questions. 21
22       Our third contribution concerns economically useful precision. The preceding        22

23   investment experiment established equivalence at a broad normalized tolerance; its      23

24   intervals did not resolve small policy differences. We derive a convex-order AVaR       24

25   enclosure with width proportional to the inverse square of innovation resolution,       25

26   without requiring a differentiable value function or a fixed risk maximizer. Coupled    26

27   with a complete admission-and-fallback policy, this gives a deterministic support for   27

28   paired policy differences. The numerical and statistical budgets can consequently       28

29   be chosen together before evaluation. The new margin is one percent of the largest      29

30   first-date investment charge, eighty times smaller than the preceding margin, and       30

31   the prospective simultaneous half-width budget is less than half that margin.           31

2                                                                                            2
       Neural Bellman Operators connect an attained own-future continuation to feasi-
3                                                                                            3
     ble decisions under the original economic law. The constructive neural realization,
4                                                                                            4
     complete-policy comparison and explicit acquisition budget remain the paper’s
5                                                                                            5
     foundation. Under recursive risk, state-dependent qualification retains the original
6                                                                                            6
     objective and the all-original-action lower obligation. The investment technology
7                                                                                            7
     supplies primitive sufficient conditions under which these inequalities can actually
8                                                                                            8
     be computed.
9                                                                                            9
       Convex-order risk integration adds a second-order precision account without
10                                                                                           10
     smoothness of the risk-averse value. A complete admission-and-fallback service
11                                                                                           11
     then turns the original-optimum guarantee into a support bound for paired eco-
12                                                                                           12
     nomic inference. Together these results permit numerical resolution, sample size
13                                                                                           13
     and a model-relative economic margin to be fixed before evaluation. This conclu-
14                                                                                           14
     sion concerns the declared initial-state distribution and the original continuous
15                                                                                           15
     innovation law, not an unreported change in either law.
16                                                                                           16
       The empirical questions remain distinct. A storage benefit need not be neural-
17                                                                                           17
     specific; tighter uniform residuals need not yield better policy choices; and a valid
18                                                                                           18
     screen need not save work after its construction is paid for. The new controlled-rank
19                                                                                           19
     comparison and complete-service records make those distinctions observable. The
20                                                                                           20
     acquisition-and-reuse region states which additional cost and price information
21                                                                                           21
     supports an economic purchase. The fixed-accuracy horizon construction retains its
22                                                                                           22
     explicit state, action, innovation and bit-growth costs; an unexecuted largest-vector
23                                                                                           23
     comparison is not inferred from it.
24                                                                                           24
       The result is a constructive economic method with an original-law policy account
