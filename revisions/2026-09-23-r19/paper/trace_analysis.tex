\section{Continuation targets and attribution}\label{sec:r19trace}
A fixed-policy continuation value gives a constructive target even when the optimal value is unknown. Fix one newly trained time-control vector, without the initial-wealth transfer used on $K$. For $x\in[1.25,2)$, its deterministic wealth remains above $.5$, and its wealth drift is strictly negative. The upper face is therefore inaccessible before the preference exit or terminal date. Let $q_\pi$ denote its true stopped policy value with this fixed control context.

\begin{proposition}[Policy-induced continuation trace]\label{prop:r19trace}
The one-sided limit $q_\pi(0,u,2-)$ exists for $u\in[1.98,2.02]$. The original-utility Gaussian evaluator, with the same uniform stopping allowance and no wealth transfer, encloses this limit. Its certified excess over $g(0,u,2)$ is at least $\NineteenTrace$ uniformly on that preference segment for all five new policies.
\end{proposition}
The proof does not redefine the absorbing boundary value. A process initialized exactly on the stopping boundary has immediate payoff $g$; the displayed object is the limit from interior initial wealth. This distinction is precisely why forcing a critic to match settlement on this face is inconsistent. The true $q_\pi$ has the fixed-policy Feynman--Kac characterization on each time slab. It is not an optimal upper witness, and the numerical execution here certifies its continuation trace rather than a global Bellman residual for a learned approximation to $q_\pi$.

A continuation-aware critic update can now impose a verified inequality against a feasible-policy continuation target, rather than merely omitting an equality constraint. For an upper witness $w$, the appropriate necessary requirement is $w(0,u,2-)\geq q_\pi(0,u,2-)$, up to an explicitly allocated comparison error. The target is generated from the new policy and original payoff; no optimal-value labels are supplied. Joint control of this trace and the interior positive Bellman residual remains necessary.

The latter qualification is tested directly. In a predeclared intervention, only the final additive bias of each of five old critics is increased until directed arithmetic proves the earlier feasible-policy trace requirement at $(0,2,2)$. All actors remain fixed. Every one of the 1,024 boxes is recomputed. Table~\ref{tab:r19intervention} shows that the resulting total bounds are about 22.7--23.4. A scalar lift removes the particular pointwise obstruction but worsens the global certificate. It is neither selected as an improved witness nor used to claim full-domain accuracy.
