\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Neural Bellman Operators require a separation between learning a policy, evaluating that policy, and bounding the unrestricted optimum. The value-separated update studied here supplies that separation in executable form. New neural actors improve independently certified original-economy payoffs, and a coupled-payoff theorem promotes the improvement to a continuum of initial states. Their reference-state certificates meet the unchanged $.01$ target; their state-rectangle certificates admit explicit feasible wealth compensation. These achievements concern the newly generated policies themselves, rather than an inherited non-neural library.

The same separation clarifies continuation learning and computational work. Feasible-policy evaluation supplies verified one-sided continuation targets, but a scalar critic lift is not a global Bellman solution. Learned initialization reduces matched-tolerance gradient work in identified nonlinear-control regimes, but the cube-uniform correction theorem remains available without training and classical acceleration remains a strong comparator. Complete feedback certificates, every historical failure, and all matched classical results are retained. The objective remains an accurate, competitive solution of the original economic problem over its full state--time domain. The new policy-sensitive results advance that objective without substituting a change of witness, a restricted initial set, or a different numerical method for the result being claimed.
