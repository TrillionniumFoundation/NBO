\title{Certified Bellman Operators with Neural Proposals: Constructive Witnesses and Costly Policy Revision}
\runtitle{Certified Bellman Operators: R32}
\begin{aug}\author[id=au1,addressref={add1}]{\fnms{Qian}~\snm{QI}\ead[label=e1]{qiqian@pku.edu.cn}}\address[id=add1]{\orgname{Peking University}}\end{aug}
\begin{abstract}We study certified Bellman computation when revising an installed policy is costly and changes future implementation exposure. In a continuous-state maintenance economy with action-dependent transitions, we construct upper witnesses from primitives by exact backward operations and shape-preserving dyadic compression. A nonempty action class guarantees uniform operating accuracy at every restart; a second Bellman recursion minimizes intervention cost within that class. A separate incumbent certificate identifies globally minimal zero-intervention solutions. All 42 registered class configurations pass an independent whole-cell checker, and six predeclared mechanism-stress configurations exhibit strictly positive savings relative to pointwise revision. Twelve spline cases admit zero intervention, whereas none of 18 raw neural cases meets its operating target. Complete construction costs and strong classical controls are reported without inferring neural-specific acceleration. In the unchanged original stopped economy, an interval-wide derivative bound identifies the unique global optimum of an active-boundary constant-control class. The original all-domain feedback target remains distinct from these constructive and computed results.\end{abstract}
