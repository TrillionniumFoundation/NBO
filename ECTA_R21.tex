\begin{abstract}
We develop Neural Bellman Operators with policy-specific payoff acceptance and separate unrestricted optimal comparison. In the original stopped consumption--portfolio economy, newly trained networks generate stochastic consumption and a nonzero, exactly self-financing portfolio. A continuum-mixture theorem and a verified Jensen-gap bound establish actual policy improvement at every initial state of a rectangle, rather than only at four expert states. Five confirmatory seeds attain final regret below $.002909$ there, and initial-to-final true regret falls by more than $85.8$ percent. Fixed-policy price transport extends a $.003327$ bound over the rectangle and an adjustment-price interval. An additional $.002$ of initial wealth is sufficient compensation for each learned stochastic policy. A direct same-family comparator is more accurate at every vertex and materially cheaper to generate. The network observes time only; restricted wealth feedback arises through its analytic decoder. Global feedback accuracy, localized optimality, policy improvement, and high-dimensional warm-start effects are kept distinct. The full-domain $.01$ objective and all prior substantive results are retained.
\end{abstract}
