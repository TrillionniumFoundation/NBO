\section*{Part I: Reading and replication map}
The current manuscript is \texttt{ECTA\_R9.tex}; its Appendix proves the new results. The accompanying response addresses the September 22, 2026 R8 numerical-methods report at commit \texttt{9749c3c}. The new work is isolated on the R9 constructive-certification branch. Earlier main and review branches have not been advanced by this revision.

The R9 replication directory contains the original-payoff actor and moment evaluator, fitted-upper construction and Bernstein verifier, restricted market-deflator verifier, scalar global action certifier, interval neural-jet evaluator, absolute-value relaxation, and frozen external-comparison driver. Numerical tables are generated from their retained JSON records. Every new trained external network and all 4,096-path samples for each of the 72 holdout pairs are retained. Source and preservation hashes, exact commands, scope checks, and failed-pilot status are in the R9 replication and validation records.

The following material is historical but preserved in full. References to a preceding revision, its new experiment, its current target, or its conclusions refer to that historical record. In particular, the R8 manufactured-payoff result is not evidence for original-payoff precision, and its six-seed external comparison is superseded for current inference by the frozen R9 holdout. Neither passage is deleted or silently rewritten. Historical finite safeguards remain distinct from the new continuous-action oracle. The R4 recursive, temporal-self, and game derivations retain their own assumptions and equilibrium concepts; no new large-scale extension result is inferred from the additive-control computations.
