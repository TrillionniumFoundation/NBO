\title{Supplement to ``Neural Bellman Operators''}
\runtitle{Neural Bellman Operators: Supplement}
\begin{aug}
\author[id=au1,addressref={add1}]{\fnms{Qian}~\snm{QI}\ead[label=e1]{qiqian@pku.edu.cn}}
\address[id=add1]{\orgname{Peking University}}
\end{aug}
\begin{abstract}
This supplement retains the operator, verification, transition, recursive-utility, equilibrium, comparative-oracle, and economic-certificate proofs of the preceding complete manuscript. The additional section proves participation and procurement conditions, the surrender-policy envelope, the statewise enforcement frontier, and the exact first-date risky-share reduction. It derives the permission and termination sensitivities of regular benefit crossings, extends the joint-region certificate to a finite surrender charge, and records the additional arithmetic and target-transfer account. Executed validation includes exhaustive small-model policy comparisons, direct selected-control reconstruction, fee and term experiments, and reoptimized permission frontiers. All historical applications, adverse comparisons, and original numerical targets remain available. The main paper and supplement use the same policy-evaluation and stopping conventions.
\end{abstract}
