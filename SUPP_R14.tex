\begin{abstract}
This supplement gives the analytic dual, the numerical enclosure constructions, the neural derivative and action calculations, and the replication protocol for Revision R14. It distinguishes interval proofs from high-precision diagnostics, frozen inputs from freshly generated policies, and a current technical supplement from the preserved historical record. Earlier manuscripts, extensions, experiments, and unsuccessful calculations remain unchanged in the repository and are indexed here rather than silently deleted or recast as current evidence.
\end{abstract}
